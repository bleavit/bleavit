Finding, not an amendment — doc 13 §5's proof-size budget is derived from a 5 MiB PoV ceiling the pinned platform no longer uses, and every share computed from it is half the true value.

13 §5 states the normal-class evidence budget as **3,932,160 B** and derives `decide` = 19.0 % and `settle_cohort(12)` = 23.9 % from it; `runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/pov_budgets.rs:992` repeats the figure in a doc comment. 3,932,160 is 75 % of **5 MiB**. The runtime's own live assertions are only reproducible against **10 MiB**: `buy` declares 108,804 B generated + `EXTERNAL_TRADE_ROUTE_PROOF_SURCHARGE` 3,056 B = **111,860 B** (`RuntimeDbWeight::reads` carries no proof component), and `pov_budgets.rs` asserts at runtime that `max_block.proof / buy.proof == 93`, `primary.proof / buy.proof == 70` and `external.proof / buy.proof == 23`. Those pin `max_block.proof` into [10,402,980, 10,514,840) — which contains 10,485,760 and excludes 5,242,880 — and `primary_capacity()` is `max_block - Operational.reserved` = 75 % of it, so the true normal-class budget is **7,864,320 B** and `decide` is **9.5 %**, not 19.0 %. Against the smaller figure the pinned primary ceiling would be 35 rather than 70. **Nothing in `docs/architecture/` or the runtime was changed.** Correcting §5 means re-deriving every share it prints, reconciling doc 10 §9.1 and `reference-model/.../frontend_budget.py` (whose `NORMAL_REF_TIME_BUDGET` = 1.5e12 *is* 75 % of the block, so the two halves of one constant already disagree), and it is outside an explainer session's scope — recorded here for the owning session rather than half-fixed. The explainer uses the measured 10 MiB figure and says on screen that it disagrees with 13 §5 and why.
