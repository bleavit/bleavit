## `ε_max` is deleted from the product rather than shipped with a caveat.
authorized_by: Me, under R-1, on a verification finding that refuted my own design
docs_touched: `16-hosted-question-service.md` §5.1; PLAN N5

The approved design sold three figures per question; it now sells two. `ε_max = (1/n)·Σ[min(1, p̄(1+κ)^i) − p̄]` was to be published as a *feasibility ceiling* — a proof that a question is unmanipulable at `ε` within its window — and it is not an upper bound: 04 §7's κ-slew segment accumulator credits observations **backward across gaps**, so displacement paths exist that beat the expression while still passing coverage. ## `ProtocolAccounts` splits into three predicates, and `PolLine::of` becomes a typed funding domain rather than an `Option`.
authorized_by: Me, under R-1, on verification findings
docs_touched: `03-conditional-ledger.md` §1a; `04-markets-and-pricing.md` §3; `16` §7

Both are money-movement corrections found by adversarial review of the design, not of the code. (a) One enumerated set currently serves destination-refusal, fee/deposit/position-cap exemption and inflow-cap exemption; with two ledger instances it is wrong in **both** directions at once — per-instance destinations strand a service position in a primary book address, union exemptions hand foreign accounts zero deposit and no position cap. Split into `ReservedProtocolDestinations` (union), `LocalProtocolAccounts<I>` (per instance) and `InflowCapExemptAccounts` (separate). (b) `PolLine::of` is total over `BookKind` and `market.seed` debits POL unconditionally, so an `External` arm inherits it; the fix is `FundingDomain ∈ {Protocol(PolLine), ExternalClient(ClientId)}`, **not** `Option`, so the non-protocol case carries its funder. The naive-arm failure was also *mis-described* in my draft: `debit_pol_custody` moves no tokens, so the full-cycle error is `r − h` (the external maker's P/L, unsafe when the book **wins**), not gross NAV inflation. And a harder blocker sat behind it — `withdraw_book` requires both parties be protocol accounts, so a correctly non-protocol client **cannot be refunded at all**; that needs a narrow exact-funder return capability, never a classification exemption.
