docs_touched: `05` §4.1/§4.3; `13` §1; `15` PT-10; `16` §8.5/§12; primitives metric namespace; welfare/runtime implementation; derived design kit

The external side is the existing `RuntimeBlockWeights::Operational.reserved` value in both `Weight` dimensions; the primary/system side is its physical complement. `Normal.max_total` remains the full physical block so `CheckWeight` cannot reject a primary call merely because the reserved external slice is unused; the final `ResourcePartition` extension and the XCM call dispatcher are the authoritative hard-admission paths. The production P seam is primary-only now, before any producer exists. The quota and reservation derive from the already configured operational reservation and its complement, so there is no invented fraction, calibration or unsafe unbounded direction; a missing/malformed reservation or reversed complement fails closed. The final sentence claiming closure and the fixed-schedule PT-10 evidence are superseded by the adversarial review; the four sound axes remain.
