## 03 §7 R-4 trued and implemented (milestone B14) — the endowment clause was specified but existed nowhere in code, and the obvious reading of its book-account half turned out to be wrong.
authorized_by: Milestone B14 (user: "Implement milestone B14… Decide on your own", 2026-07-21)
docs_touched: 03 §1, §7 R-4; 04 §6.1; 08 §1.4

R-4 required the ledger sovereign, treasury sub-accounts and book accounts to be "genesis-endowed and can never be reaped"; nothing endowed anything, so the last full redeemer of the last open vault hit `Token(NotExpendable)` on a redemption 03 §5.3 makes legal. The rule is now written around its actual mechanism — under `Preservation::Preserve` reducible balance is `balance − min_balance`, so an endowment of exactly `min_balance` is a permanent floor, and it binds **only** where every custody path out of the account preserves. That framing immediately exposed a second gap the original text hid: MAIN's one custody outflow used `Expendable`, which ignores the floor, so endowing MAIN alone would have changed nothing. **The book-account half was the substantive ruling.** Per-market accounts cannot be genesis-endowed (their addresses embed a runtime-allocated `MarketId`), and the tempting conclusion — that R-4 therefore does not reach them — is false: a Baseline book retains its sell-side fee as *plain* USDC, and below `min_balance` that retention is unpayable, so ordinary sells from `MinTrade` = 1 USDC up to ≈6.35 USDC were rejected. Verified by binary search against real custody (6,349,782 fails, 6,349,783 passes retaining exactly `min_balance`) rather than by reading. R-4 now distinguishes the three cases on whether the account custodies plain USDC at all, and names the registry sovereigns and epoch bond escrow as accounts that MUST stay un-endowed because their `Expendable` payouts exist precisely so draining the last bond stays admissible. 03 §1's "1-unit genesis endowment" was a straight internal contradiction (below `min_balance`, protects nothing) and is repaired. 04 §6.1 gains the Baseline sell-side degeneration that makes the carve-out legible; 08 §1.4 pins `fund_budget_line` to `MAIN − min_balance`. **No 02 surface touched — no contract bump** (`INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` stays 5).. **Corrected in-session after dual review, before merge:** the first draft of this amendment claimed the reaped Baseline residue is "recoverable only by TREASURY-class proposal under R-6" — false on two counts (`recover_foreign` refuses `AssetKind::Usdc` outright and moves no custody, and R-6 is scoped to USDC arriving *outside* protocol flows), so the rule now states the residue is unrecoverable and names what implementing recovery would take. The bullet-2 MUST was also unqualified where a real seam violates it: the ledger `TREASURY` sub-account is XCM-addressable and the SDK `FungiblesAdapter` withdraws `Expendable` unconditionally. Finally the Baseline endowment is now normatively **best-effort and idempotent**, because making it a hard precondition of the seed propagates out of the epoch tick and reverts every proposal in the batch — G-1 requires the narrow failure, not the broad one. Writing the spec first and the code second would have shipped all three; the reviews are what caught them.
