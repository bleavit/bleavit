# Decisions — 2026-07-30

## SQ-523 — 15 §4.5's benchmark mandate did not reach…
authorized_by: R-1 (spec genuinely incomplete; decided and proceeded)
docs_touched: 15 §4.5

SQ-523 — 15 §4.5's benchmark mandate did not reach transaction extensions, and that is why an unweighted path shipped.

The mandate read "`frame-benchmarking` for every call and hook". A `TransactionExtension` is neither, so `pallet_asset_tx_payment` — which has **no dispatchables at all** — sat outside the scope of a section that otherwise governs weights exhaustively, and the runtime's own registry comment rationalised the omission ("carried by the extrinsic base weight") in terms that were true before E3 and false after it. Fixing only the runtime would have left the next such substitution equally unguarded, so the mandate itself is amended: it now names **transaction extensions**, and a new normative bullet states the obligation and its two reasons. Those reasons are what make this worth spec text rather than a code comment. (i) The extension's post-dispatch refund **cannot** correct it, and the reason is sharper than "there is no refund" (my first draft said that, and it is false): the refund exists, but it computes `declared - WeightInfo::charge_asset_tx_payment_asset()` where `declared` *is* that same function, so on the charged path it is identically zero. It corrects a mis-predicted branch, never a mis-measured one. (ii) It is charged on **every** extrinsic taking the path, not on one feature's users, so the error scales with block occupancy instead of with adoption; here it was **+3,729 B of proof per USDC-paid extrinsic**, a 2.01x understatement of the extension's PoV. The bullet also carries the fixture rule the same investigation produced: upstream fixtures are written against the **reference** runtime's admissible inputs, so wherever this runtime narrows them — an asset filter, an origin filter, a governed rate that must exist — the fixture MUST be re-pointed and its preconditions seeded, else it measures the *rejection* path and weights the paying path with a cheap early return. No contract bump: weights are not a 02 surface, and no 13 row moves (§4.5's standing "consumers read the **dispatched** weight" rule already covers the PoV-budget side).

## 08 §10's two "figures that do not derive from their own…
authorized_by: R-1 (arithmetic defect in normative text; decided and proceeded)
docs_touched: 08 §10.5, §10.6

08 §10's two "figures that do not derive from their own tables" were one arithmetic error, and it inflated the cost base by 18 %.

The audit had them as two independent minors — 77,864 vs 65,864 per epoch, and 8.2 vs 9.7 years. They are the same defect twice: **77,864 = 65,864 + `keeper.budget_epoch`**, because §10.6 counted the keeper cost **gross** (`580,320 × 0.09` = 52,229) while also keeping the separate 12,000 `KEEPER` row that the gross figure already contains; and **8.2 yr** is that same mistake annualized (77,864 × 17.393 = 1,354,279) and divided into the same `25M − 13,862,944` = 11.14M envelope the 9.7 yr cell uses. Neither 77,864 nor 1,354,279 appears in any §10.1 table, which is exactly the symptom the audit named. **Corrected:** §10.6's quiet-epoch figure is **62,281/epoch** — and that is not simply 65,864 either, because the sentence names *collators, keepers and the `ops.*` lines*, so the two rows a quiet epoch genuinely does not incur come out (`REWARDS` 2,500, paid by §1.1 only on `Executed`; the ≈ 1,083 POL divergence, which needs a seeded book). §10.5's runway is **9.7 years**, stated with its quotient so it is checkable against the table above it. **Direction matters and is recorded: both errors ran conservative** — an overstated cost base and a shorter runway — so no self-funding claim was ever stronger than the arithmetic supported, which is why this is a correction and not a retraction. The stale 1,354,279 / "4–9× short" pair in PLAN's own session log was corrected to 1,145,562 / 3.1–7.6×, matching the Track E section that had it right all along.

## 03 §3's `ProtocolAccounts` enumeration called itself a…
authorized_by: R-1 (spec contradicted a correct implementation; decided and proceeded)
docs_touched: 03 §3, §5.3a(1)

03 §3's `ProtocolAccounts` enumeration called itself a closed set and was short by nine members — the same failure the 06 §6.3 review caught one round earlier.

§3 named "the book, fee, POL, POL_BASELINE, INSURANCE and treasury sub-accounts" and asserted "this enumeration is normative and is the same closed set §5.3a(1) names". The runtime's `ProtocolAccounts` also contains **ten families the list never named**: the ledger sovereign, the treasury `MAIN` sub-account, the per-market **book** namespace (the old text named only the per-market *fee* account), and the market, epoch, execution-guard, oracle, welfare-settlement, incident-registry and milestone-registry sovereigns. I first wrote "nine" by counting the book namespace as already-named; it was not. **The implementation is right and the spec was wrong**: every one of those is protocol-owned and bounded, which is the rationale §3 itself gives, and charging any of them the §5.3a fee would be the treasury taxing itself. The danger was directional — a reader checking whether an account may be charged would have read absence from a "closed" list as licence to charge it. **Fixed by making the criterion normative and the list its current membership**, with the relationship stated explicitly (the list follows the criterion, never the reverse), so a future protocol sovereign is exempt by meeting the criterion rather than by waiting for this sentence to be rewritten. §5.3a(1) redirected to §3 rather than re-enumerating, which is what let the two drift apart. **Pattern worth naming:** this is the third example-list-presented-as-closed-set in two rounds (06 §6.3's frozen calls, 15 §1's L-7 copy, this) — prose that enumerates *and* claims closure is a recurring defect shape in this doc set, and the durable fix is to state the rule and mark the list as derived.

## Milestone E5 opened: the cost side of the crossover,…

Milestone E5 opened: the cost side of the crossover, and a ruling on which lines are cuttable.

Track E's revenue instruments cannot close 08 §10's crossover alone, and the arithmetic says the cost half is the cheaper one to move. Two findings drove the milestone. **(1) The derivable base is 94.5 % two lines** — keepers 908,408 USDC/yr (79.3 %) and collators 173,929 (15.2 %) — and the largest single line, `ops.keepers` at 699,694 (61.1 %), funds coverage 08 §6.3 itself describes as degrading "chart density, never decisions". **(2) Both keeper lines are linear in `1 / mkt.obs_interval`**, so one PARAM key, moved in the direction the 13 §5 occupancy screen already admits, cuts ≈ 40 % of the base. **Cuttable, in order: `mkt.obs_interval` 10 → 20; re-base `keeper.rebate` against the now-benchmarked fee basis (its `[VERIFY]` has been unresolvable until every pallet was benchmarked — it no longer is, and R-2 requires resolving rather than carrying it); `collator.comp_epoch` 2,000 → 500; fund `ops.keepers` against measured rather than worst-case zero-trade load; phase-gate the `ops.*` lines Phase 4 does not need.** **Refused: `REWARDS` and the realized POL divergence** — 5.4 % of `C` between them, and each buys the mechanism (proposal flow, and the price discovery 08 §10.6 says the treasury is deliberately purchasing). Cutting either attacks the product to save nothing. **Recorded as not reducible and not in the table at all:** `ops.coretime`'s `broker.renew` price — the one unavoidable cost 08 §10.1 excludes. **The consequence is a change in kind, not degree**, which is why it is a milestone rather than a parameter tweak: applying 08 §10.4's own `H* = C / 0.00825` at τ = 3, the first and third levers take `C` 1,145,562 → 560,907 and `V*` 138.9M → 68.0M, i.e. from 1.17× a saturated 5×TREASURY slate to **0.89× a saturated 5×PARAM one**, with runway to the 13.86M CODE seeding floor 9.7 → 19.9 yr. Those two derived figures live in PLAN only until E5 lands them in 08 §10 (R-4); they are derivations from 08's own formulas, not new spec. No spec or code changed on this pass.

## E5 pass 1: the cost base was not mostly a policy…
authorized_by: R-1/R-2 (a `[VERIFY]` resolved against measurement, as R-2 requires rather than permits); standing goal instruction to reduce the cost base autonomously
docs_touched: 08 §6.2, §10.1, §10.5; 13 §1; PLAN.md

E5 pass 1: the cost base was not mostly a policy choice, it was mostly an unverified number.

Three findings, in the order they were found, each one underneath the last. **(1) SQ-531** — `keeper.rebate` = 0.09 USDC was seeded as 3× an *assumed* 0.03 USDC crank fee that 08 §6.2 called "assumed" and 13 §1 tagged `[VERIFY fee basis at benchmark time]`. Every pallet is now benchmarked, so it is measurable; measured, a sanctioned crank costs **0.000085 USDC**, making the seed ≈ **1,058×** the fee it claimed to be 3× of. Both keeper lines — **79.3 % of the entire annual cost base** — were scaled by that error. Kernel basis 30,000 → **85 µUSDC**, seed → 0.000255. **(2) SQ-528**, found while trying to measure (1) and strictly worse: `FeeMultiplierUpdate = ()` requires `Convert<Multiplier, Multiplier>`, which the pallet supplies only for `TargetedFeeAdjustment`/`ConstFeeMultiplier`, so `()` fell through to sp-runtime's blanket `impl<A, B: Default> Convert<A, B> for ()` returning `FixedU128::default()` = **0**. `on_finalize` runs unconditionally, so genesis's multiplier of 1 survived one block and **transaction weight went unpriced from block 2 onward** — on a PoV-bound parachain a standing DoS subsidy, and the reason no fee basis was measurable. Fixed with `ConstFeeMultiplier` at 1.0 rather than `SlowAdjustingFeeUpdate`: 1.0 is exactly the model 08 §9 documents, whereas congestion pricing is a policy addition no spec section owns (deferred as SQ-529). **(3) SQ-530**, found in parallel by a code-outflow audit: four fail-soft treasury paths persisted `load()`-folded `PendingMainCredit` without discharging it, so NAV over-stated custody cumulatively — the over-permissive direction for `trs.cap_proposal`·NAV, `pol.budget_epoch`·NAV and the 08 §4.1 arming floors. Fixed by making the discharge structural (`put_folded_state`) rather than a rule to remember. **What was REFUSED:** the `Pays::No` + due-gate restructuring E5 originally implied, on an adversarial Codex review's objection that `keeper.rebate` is the shared payout for epoch/execution/cleanup/welfare/ORACLE cranks too, so blanket retirement needs a per-dispatch useful-success proof across several pallets — large, risky, and made unnecessary by the basis correction. Also refused: cutting `REWARDS` or the POL divergence loss (5.4 % of the base between them, and each buys the mechanism). **Result:** cost base **1,145,562 → 239,728/yr**; runway to the CODE seeding floor **9.7 → 46.5 yr** and to the binding arming floor **3.3 → 15.6 yr** at zero revenue; with `collator.comp_epoch` at its registry minimum, **109,281/yr, 101.9 yr and 34.3 yr**, and `V*` below a minimum-activity PARAM slate so revenue covers cost for τ ≥ 2. **None of it is austerity** — no service level, mechanism or commitment was reduced.

## `collator.comp_epoch` is NOT lowered to its 500…
authorized_by: R-2 escalation rule (unsafe error direction, no evidence anchor) — decided conservatively rather than deferred, per the standing instruction not to wait
docs_touched: PLAN.md only; no value changed

`collator.comp_epoch` is NOT lowered to its 500 registry minimum, and that is a decision rather than an omission.

It is the largest remaining line — 173,929/yr, **72.6 %** of the post-SQ-531 cost base — and halving it twice to the minimum would save 130,447/yr and lift the runway to the *binding* 21.26M arming floor from 15.6 to 34.3 yr. It is lawful: D-15 calls 2,000 "initial (PARAM-adjustable)" and 13 §1 admits [500, 10,000]. **It is declined because it is the one lever in this whole exercise that could cause a liveness failure, and it is the one with no evidence behind it.** Everything else E5 changed was a correction — an unmeasured placeholder, an unpriced weight term, a double-counted credit — where the right value was derivable and the error direction was known. Collator pay is different: 12 §6.1 mandates growth to 8–12 **bonded permissionless** collators from Phase 4, and no unit cost, hardware profile or operator quote exists anywhere in this repository (the same `[VERIFY]` hole 08 §10.1 names for eight other ops lines). Underpaying collators loses block production, which is the unsafe direction, and R-2 reserves exactly that shape — an unsafe error direction with no evidence anchor — for a values judgement rather than an implementation choice. **The goal does not require it**: 46.5 yr to the CODE seeding floor at zero assumed revenue already clears 25, and the arming floor binds once at Phase 6 rather than continuously. Recorded so launch governance takes it with real operator quotes, which is strictly better than deriving it from nothing.

## SQ-532 ruled: the keeper meter is vestigial at the…
authorized_by: R-1 (spec ruling); decided rather than deferred
docs_touched: PLAN.md only

SQ-532 ruled: the keeper meter is vestigial at the corrected basis, and the kernel floor stays.

13 §1's hard minimum `keeper.budget_epoch` ≥ 6,000 USDC/epoch is justified as the point "below [which] the metered budget cannot cover decision-critical cranks at the 1× rebate bound and A-1 fails silently". Under SQ-531 that load is ≈ **11.38** USDC/epoch at the 1× bound, so the floor is ≈ 527× its own stated rationale and `KeeperBudgetExhausted` is unreachable in normal operation. **The floor is nonetheless left alone.** What the meter protects against is runaway rebate spend, and at 0.000255/crank the budget admits ≈ 47 million cranks per epoch — block space binds three orders of magnitude before the meter does, so the protection is subsumed rather than lost. Lowering a kernel floor is a CODE change (13 rule 7 puts `keeper.budget` in the kernel-bounded set, whose entire governance-metadata tuple is genesis-fixed and which `amend_registry` refuses outright), and it would buy a rail that nothing needs. **The row stays open deliberately**, bound to the TGE re-seed: `keeper.rebate`'s USDC leg still moves with the launch `fee.vit_usdc_rate`, so if the launch price lands far from §9's 0.05 placeholder the rebate scales with it and the meter can become load-bearing again. Re-evaluate at the same moment the `[VERIFY]` price is fixed, not before.

## SQ-529 and SQ-533 filed with their derivations rather…

SQ-529 and SQ-533 filed with their derivations rather than implemented, and that is the R-2 line.

Both are real gaps found by E5 and both need a number this repository does not contain. **SQ-529 (`proof_size` is never priced):** `WeightToFee = IdentityFee` charges `ref_time` only, so the dimension a PoV-bound parachain actually congests on is free — `record_snapshot` carries 645,546 B of proof, ≈ 12 % of the 5 MiB budget, and pays nothing for it. Both rows carry the analysis so pass 2 is a decision rather than a rediscovery.
