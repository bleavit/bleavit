## SQ-234 — the entrenched track's four epochs are…
authorized_by: Delegated (R-1, 2026-07-25)
docs_touched: 06 §2.1, §2.4

SQ-234 — the entrenched track's four epochs are counted, and the delay is sized against `epoch.length`'s ceiling.

06 §2.4 buys four *epoch boundaries* of priced second opinion, not a number of days; `epoch.length` is governable 14–42 d, so the original `4 × 21 d` delivered four boundaries only at the default and **two** at the maximum — the review window halved exactly when governance had lengthened epochs. Stock `TrackInfo` is block-denominated and the delay is read once at approval, so neither a live-length derivation (shaveable by a later lengthening) nor an exactly-four dispatch gate (no approval-epoch anchor exists in `pallet-referenda`) is available without new machinery. Ruled: size it at `4 ×` the **ceiling** (168 d), which makes ≥ 4 boundaries unconditional because the ceiling is kernel-bounded (13 rule 7) and shortening epochs only adds boundaries. 06 §2.1's cell and a new §2.4 paragraph record the rule **and its cost**: the delay doubles at the default length and binds every `entrenched`-class row in both directions, `att.bond` raises included.

## SQ-490 — a generated weight file may not contain…
authorized_by: Claude (R-1 delegation, 2026-07-25; values-layer numbers and testing-regime rules are the assistant's call)
docs_touched: 15 §4.5; 13 §5

SQ-490 — a generated weight file may not contain hand-written terms, and 13 §5's pinned PoV figures are re-trued to the dispatched weight.

Two amendments from one implementation. (a) **15 §4.5 gains a normative rule**: a weight term must never be hand-written into a `frame-benchmarking`-generated file, because the generator does not emit such edits, so regeneration deletes them and the deletion presents as a weight *decrease* — the one shape a growth-only gate is structurally unable to see. Where a call's worst case cannot be exercised by any single fixture (here: a term that fires only on an epoch crossing, while the call's heaviest fixture by construction crosses nothing), the term must be benchmarked separately and composed at the `#[pallet::weight]` attribute; a delegating wrapper outside the generated files is explicitly **not** an acceptable alternative, because the gate parses the generated artifacts and a wrapper removes the term from coverage. Consumers that pin a call's weight must read the **dispatched** weight, since a composed call's generated function is only one addend of what the chain charges — both of this repo's pinning consumers were reading the addend. (b) **13 §5's figures move** from `decide` 404,514 B / `settle_cohort(5)` 384,825 B to **745,551 B** / **725,862 B**, and the local drift ceilings from 512 KiB / 768 KiB to **1 MiB** / **1.25 MiB**. That is a measurement replacing a guess: the A13 collator-compensation term was a hand-written 48,000 B / 1.8 ms estimate and benchmarks at 389,037 B / 4.39 ms. The 8x PoV jump is the generator's synthetic per-key envelope (120 payees' `ForeignAssets::Account` keys charged as independent maximum-depth trie paths, ignoring their shared prefix; recorded proof was 17,874 B, ~22x smaller). The estimate is declared anyway because the error directions are asymmetric — over-declaring PoV costs block capacity, under-declaring yields blocks that exceed their proof budget at execution, and a sparse benchmark trie under-represents production path depth, so `pov_mode = Measured` would be optimistic about exactly the wrong thing. The resulting capacity cost is real (~389 KB reserved per crank for a payout that fires once per epoch) and is recorded as **SQ-499**, whose remedy is the post-dispatch refund 15 §4.5 already mandates for payload-executing extrinsics.

## SQ-490 item (4) — the absence of a regeneration becomes…
authorized_by: Delegated (R-1, 2026-07-25)
docs_touched: 15 §4.5

SQ-490 item (4) — the absence of a regeneration becomes a gated, per-function property, and the hard/advisory split is set by measurement.

15 §4.5's existing weight bullet says CI "proves executability, not calibration"; that was a statement about a job which ran every benchmark at `--steps 2 --repeat 1` and **discarded the results**, so it could see a benchmark that crashes and never one that disagrees with the committed file. Amended: a per-commit job now re-measures every pallet and diffs the committed artifact **function by function, never by file date** — a header attributes all of a file's functions to one generation event, which is exactly how two hand-appended functions hid inside an otherwise-generated `pallet_attestor.rs`. **The split between hard-gated and advisory dimensions was measured, and the first measurement changed the design.** Storage dimensions are invariant to sampling fidelity — `pallet_attestor` at 2×1 and 50×20 gave byte-identical `proof_size`/`reads`/`writes` for all six functions while ref_time moved −1.2 %…+64.6 % — which is what makes per-commit re-measurement affordable at all. **Two further generalisations of mine were then wrong, and the full sweep is what caught them.** First I made fitted slopes advisory across fidelities, reasoning a 2-point fit need not equal a 50-point one; `pallet_multisig` (11 component terms) agreed on all 46 quantities, so I made every storage figure hard at any fidelity. Running the sweep over all 31 files then produced **42 \`STALE\` lines across 12 pallets, and most were not drift at all**: gating the intercept and slope *separately* reports the generator's own re-decomposition of one measured cost — `pallet_collator_selection::set_candidacy_bond` moves its proof slope 901→1,306 at 2×1 while its worst-case writes stay at exactly 201. So the comparison is now on **worst-case totals** (base + slope × component high), the same quantity `check-weight-regression.py` uses. And the invariance claim does **not** hold universally: re-measuring `pallet_collator_selection`, constant-weight functions agree 2/2 while **component-bearing functions differ 5 of 9**, with `new_session` losing its per-candidate write slope entirely — worst-case writes **100 at 50×20 against 3 at 2×1**. A 2-point fit can miss a linear term, and missing it *understates*, so the rule that survived is: constant-weight totals hard at any fidelity (the class the 8-vs-261-reads defect belonged to, so the cheap per-commit run still catches the real thing), component-bearing totals hard only at matching fidelity and advisory otherwise, ref_time advisory always, and component ranges — declared by the fixture, not fitted — hard always. Full-fidelity coverage of component functions is a release-time obligation that must be reported, not implied. Two obligations added with it: one unsatisfiable fixture MUST NOT discard its pallet's other functions (the state that froze all 39 functions of `pallet_assets.rs` at one date), and a function carried forward unmeasured MUST carry a marker the gate reads and reports, so "measured long ago" is never silently indistinguishable from "measured now". No 02 surface; contract stays at **v15**.

SQ-493 — 07 §10's two-consecutive-flag rule is fully specified, and the renormalization's degenerate cases are decided.

§10 stated the rule in one clause and left four things undetermined, each of which an implementation had to guess: whether consecutiveness is a property of the component or of the cohort's window; what an absent flag record means; which source classes can be flagged at all; and what happens when a drop would empty a pillar group. §10 now carries all five points normatively, and the last one is the substantive ruling: a group reached only through a gate (`S`, `C`) **declines** the drop, because a gate has no weight to renormalize away and an empty `min`/empty weighted product both evaluate to 1 — the most favourable value — so honouring the drop would convert total unavailability into a perfect score (R-7). `P`/`A` renormalize at the composite level instead. SQ-493 — a degenerate weighted-geometric group is evaluated exactly (05 §4.4 rule 3).

Where one term participates at weight exactly 1, the group's value is that term's value. Found by a core test asserting that dropping a component equals the spec set that never had it, and independently confirmed by the reference model author. ## SQ-498 — the entrenched track keeps its…
authorized_by: Claude (R-1 delegation, 2026-07-25)
docs_touched: [06](../../../../docs/architecture/06-governance-and-guardians.md) §2.4

SQ-498 — the entrenched track keeps its block-denominated delay; the two-phase arm/apply surface is refused.

06 §2.4 gains the ruling as normative text rather than a tracked gap. The argument that decided it: *the delay cannot buy what a stalled chain cannot sell*. The four boundaries exist to open new Baseline markets, so on a chain whose epoch clock is not advancing the priced second opinion is unavailable under either design — arm/apply does not deliver it, it withholds the decision until someone restarts the clock, and `epoch.tick` is permissionless so the boundaries then accrue anyway. The designs differ only on a chain that stays dead. Against that, arm/apply costs a permanent `apply` dispatch, an authority-matrix row per armable call, and a re-attemptable failure path on the one track built to be hardest to move — trading a clock assumption reachable only on a halted chain for a keeper assumption on every entrenched decision. And the shortfall is gross, not subtle: the delay is four *maximum-length* epochs, so it yields four crossings only at the 42 d ceiling and eight at the 21 d default, meaning the fourth boundary is lost only when the clock has lost more than half its expected advancement across 168 days — the halt 05 §4.8's snapshot-overdue detector already answers.

## SQ-181 rulings — `U`'s denominator, `F`'s two…
authorized_by: Claude (R-1 delegation, 2026-07-25)
docs_touched: [05](../../../../docs/architecture/05-welfare-and-decision-engine.md) §4.3.2

SQ-181 rulings — `U`'s denominator, `F`'s two unsupplied quantities, and the class of failure `Π` counts.

05 §4.3 gave all three a formula and none of them an observable source; new §4.3.2 fixes each, and every one is anchored to something that already exists rather than to a new tunable. **`U`:** "scheduled slots" read as parachain blocks makes the ratio identically 1, since `epoch.length` is itself denominated in them — so slots are **relay** slots, measured by `PersistedValidationData.relay_parent_number` advancement across the window, and an empty block is one whose extrinsics are all inherents. **`F`:** `target = 1`, derived from the nominal one-block-per-relay-slot cadence. `Λ_max` is **not** derived and ships `[VERIFY]` with `F` fail-closed. A first attempt anchored it to `kernel::DEAD_MAN_RELAY_BLOCKS` so `F` would reach 0 where §4.8 declares the chain stalled; the Codex review of #186 showed that is false in kind, not degree — `F` aggregates by **median**, and a median cannot see the tail the dead-man latches on, so a window holding one catastrophic outage among healthy blocks scores a perfect `F` while the dead-man is active. No scale repairs an aggregation mismatch. The correction states the division of labour instead: the dead-man owns the tail, `U` owns the count of unfilled slots, `F` owns *sustained* degradation — which is what makes the median right, and also why `Λ_max` has no anchor. Its error direction is unsafe (too large and a persistently degraded chain never trips the `S` gate), so **Both** its error directions are unsafe — too large and a persistently degraded chain never trips the `S` gate; too small and measurement noise VOIDs cohorts and arms the guardian against a healthy chain — so there is no safe direction in which to guess. **Decided: `F` is admissible but not registered in v1.** `[VERIFY]` + fail-closed is not an alternative here, and the reason is mechanical: a registered component with no value makes `record_snapshot` refuse, so a genesis set containing `F` could never record a snapshot at all — welfare wedged, not degraded. Registration is the choice point. Structurally free, because `S` is a `min` carrying no weights (the weight-sum rule binds `C`, `P` and `A`, never `S`), and liveness stays priced by `U` for the count and the dead-man for the tail. `F` keeps id 11 and becomes registrable as a later `MetricSpec` version once Phase-2 evidences `Λ_max`. v1 ships `S = min(U, D_eff)` on two fully-derived signals rather than three with one guess. **`Π`:** an event counts iff the runtime detected a violation of an assumption it holds unconditionally, the fallback discarded correctness-relevant state or engaged a fail-static latch, **and** no defined path restores what was lost. That third clause is the whole ruling — it excludes bounded-maintenance backpressure, which matters because at 0.25 per event four of them zero `Π`, and routine capacity drops must not be able to arm the guardian's `suspend_on_gate` through a §4.7 breach flag.

## SQ-181 — `H`'s two-dimensional reduction and its…
authorized_by: Claude (R-1 delegation, 2026-07-25)
docs_touched: [05](../../../../docs/architecture/05-welfare-and-decision-engine.md) §4.3.2

SQ-181 — `H`'s two-dimensional reduction and its mapping are normative, not a code comment.

05 §4.3.2 gained the rule (Codex P1 on #191, valid). §4.3 says `1 − mean(block weight used ÷ limit)`, but a FRAME block weight is a **pair** — `ref_time` and `proof_size` — so that expression is not yet a number, and the reduction moves `H`, `C_onchain` and potentially a §4.7 daily gate outcome. Ruled: per block take the **maximum** of the two dimensions normalized against their own limits, rounded **up**; `max` is the only reduction that keeps `H` a *lower bound* on true headroom, since a block at 95 % proof and 10 % ref_time has 5 % headroom and not 90 %, while a mean or sum reports a balanced block as healthier than it is. The window value is the mean over sampled blocks under the affine clamp `(1 − mean)/(1 − 0.40)`, so at-or-below-target utilization reads exactly 1 — spare capacity is not *better* than healthy, which matters because without the clamp a chronically empty chain would outscore a target-utilization one on a metric measuring headroom rather than idleness. `target` stays a kernel constant, since §4.3's table states it. A window with **no sampled blocks** leaves `H` unavailable rather than 1, the deliberate asymmetry against `Π`'s "no events ⇒ 1": an absence of recorded failures is evidence, an absence of measurement is not.
