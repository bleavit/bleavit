Quota is now `MAX_LIVE_PROPOSALS × ARTIFACTS_PER_UPGRADE` = 64, with the artifact multiplicity named as its own constant carrying the §3.2 citation so a spec change to that count moves the quota with it. 64 is what the *fixed-pair* property forces, and that is the property to hold — `MIN_MEMBERS` = 3 at 2-of-N means surviving on exactly one available pair, so "optimally distributed across the roster" (which would admit 43) is not a safe reading. The pair holds 128 records (half the ledger), a fully loaded minimum roster 192 (inside the 256 bound), and one seat a quarter. Regression test attests both artifacts for all 32 proposals from a minimum roster; fails at quota 32 on the 17th proposal and at 16 on the 9th. **Gates:** attestor 31 tests; benchmark runtime rebuilt and `pallet_attestor` weights re-measured at 50×20 (the fixture's per-signer share moves 31 → 63, so a no-change claim needed measuring, not asserting); changed-scope workspace gate.

## **Seventh Codex connector review of PR #194 — 1 P1, valid, fixed (no milestone advanced)**

**The most consequential of the series: MAX-03's phase-recovery lane could enter Phase 4 on an unapplied ledger migration.** Round 3 replaced a hard refusal with `RetiredMigrationCursor::kill()`, reasoning that "the terminal image replaces the runtime that owned the MBM, so there is nothing left to repair". Replacing a runtime does not migrate its storage: `BackfillTotalEscrowedV1`'s nonterminal steps are **read-only** (they accumulate a total inside the cursor) and only the terminal step writes `TotalEscrowed` and storage version 1, so killing the cursor discarded the whole obligation. Nothing recovered it — `onboard_new_mbms` is reachable only from `on_runtime_upgrade`, `progress_mbms` returns early on a `None` cursor, the wrapper does not delegate while locked, and the recovery profile registers `type Migrations = ()`. The consequence is solvency-visible and silent: `TotalEscrowed` is `ValueQuery`, so it reads **0** rather than absent, `maintained_collateral_totals` understates the I-4 liability by every pre-migration vault, and `reconcile`'s `liability > custody` drift test stays *false* — the 03 §5.4 detector reports healthy on a ledger whose mirror was never built, out of `OnlyInherents` and in Phase 4. **Fix:** the phase branch resolves its bridge commitment first (fail-closed with nothing moved), then repairs whenever the segment is un-migrated — keyed on `on_chain_storage_version() == 0`, the migration's own version gate, not on a cursor having survived, because `discard_unserviceable_cursor` reaches the same hole with no retired cursor at all (Codex's second entry shape, also correct). `repair_retired_mbm` now takes `Option<&CursorOf<Runtime>>`: a retired cursor is evidence about the source state, validated when present, never progress to resume from. `configs::step_ledger_recovery` performs the Phase-4 transition on its terminal step, after the mirror/version commit, so both causes clear in one lane under `OnlyInherents`, exactly once; its envelope reserves `phase_four_transition_weight()` in that lane only. **The existing SQ-383 test had encoded the defect** — un-migrated fixture, cursor killed, `PARAM_ARMED` asserted straight after `on_runtime_upgrade` — and now drives the repair first, preserving what SQ-383 is about (the arming gate must not bind on the recovery lane) without asserting the sequencing bug with it.
