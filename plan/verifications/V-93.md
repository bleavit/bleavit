## Item

chainHead follow: does a subscription that stops before `initialized` leave boot pending?

## Spec ref

10 §4.1

## Status

complete (2026-08-03)

## Result

**YES — it hung.** `chainHead_v1_follow` resolves and removes itself from `#pending`, so the `stop` handler had nothing left to reject and the wait for the first finalized block never settled. Fixed: the initialization promise carries a reject path, `stop` and `close()` both fail it, and `open()` disconnects rather than leaving a live socket behind a failed boot. Raised by the Codex connector on PR #229
