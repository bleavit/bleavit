## Item

**The mechanical-expiry check fired on its first real occasion, hours after it was written**

## Spec ref

10 §10.2; 11 §11.2

## Status

**observed 2026-08-05**

## Result

`PENDING_SCREENS` entries carry the component they wait on, and the suite fails a `not-built` entry whose export **exists**. Building F18's `DepositForm`/`WithdrawForm` turned that test red immediately — the first time the check had an opportunity to fire, and exactly the staleness prose could not hold (nine entries had gone stale within a day of the previous fix). Recorded because the design point is transferable: a declaration that names the condition it describes can be made to expire, and the difference between that and a comment is one test
