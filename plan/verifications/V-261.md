## Item

Can a surface tell 10 §6.3's two *unchecked* causes apart from what `verifyRanges` returns?

## Spec ref

10 §6.3, §2.2, §8.4

## Status

✅ Measured (2026-08-07)

## Result

**No.** `CoverageVerification` declares `unchecked: readonly CoverageRange[]`, one flat array, and `verifyRanges` appends to it from **two** branches — `observe(range) === undefined` (the chain could not be asked) and `verdict.kind === 'unchecked'` (the range's edge is `unverifiable`, so two of the three checks have no field to read). The two never clear the same way: the second can **never** be compared, since §2.2 gives provider data no promotion path, while the first clears as soon as a chain is reachable. Nothing in the returned value distinguishes them; the only distinguishing fact is each range's own `RangeEdge` arm, which F25 renders. Filed as SQ-922 rather than repaired, since which distinction §6.3 obliges is a ruling
