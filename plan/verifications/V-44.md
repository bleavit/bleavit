## Item

**`keeper.rebate` launch fee basis (SQ-117)** — the calibrated genesis seed is a function of the per-crank fee cost, itself a function of the launch `fee.vit_usdc_rate`, which does not exist pre-launch

## Spec ref

13 §1 (`keeper.rebate`); 08 §6.2

## Status

**open — `[VERIFY]` left in place (R-2)**

## Result

The value is deliberately NOT resolved by assumption. 08 §6.2 names the placeholder basis `≈ 0.03 USDC` (itself `[VERIFY against benchmarked weights + fee.vit_usdc_rate at launch]`). Implemented the **mechanism** with a conservative documented default: `kernel::KEEPER_REBATE_FEE_BASIS_USDC = 30_000` µUSDC seeds `keeper.rebate` at 3× (default) / 1× (min) / 10× (max). At benchmark time the real per-crank fee is fixed from the launch `fee.vit_usdc_rate` and the seed is **recomputed and rounded down to µUSDC against the claimant** (R-7); the 13 §1 row keeps its `[VERIFY fee basis at benchmark time]` tag until then. No calibrated number was invented (per the standing instruction).
