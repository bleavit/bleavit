## Item

**F17's own §11.8 compliance read — six requirements are genuinely not built, and one was a real safety gap**

## Spec ref

11 §11.8.1–§11.8.6

## Status

**verified 2026-08-05 by reading §11.8 clause by clause against the built code**

## Result

Done before considering F17 ✅, and it changed the answer. The gap that mattered: §11.8.2 requires the approve flow to render *"the **exact enumerated call batch** being approved (playbooks are preimage-committed enumerated batches — decoded and displayed, never summarized away)"* — and `ApproveAction` showed a `justification_hash` and nothing else, so a guardian could put the system's most privileged signature behind calls **nobody had seen**. Closed: `PendingAction.calls` is a **required** field, an undecodable call renders as raw SCALE with no name field to guess from, and both an undecodable call and an **empty batch** now *block* the approval (R-7 — on powers that cannot be undone, *"could not decode"* must not land on the same side as *"decoded and fine"*; an empty batch reads as harmless and means the batch could not be read at all). §11.8.5's *"staleness shown prominently"* was also missing and is now built, with **both thresholds as required arguments** — *"> 4 days"* is a chain tunable, and baking it in would leave the client warning late after an amendment. **Five remain, recorded rather than glossed** (below). Also learned: a mutation adding `hidden` to the batch list left every *"the call names appear"* assertion passing, so the container is now checked for markup-level concealment — with the CSS-level limit stated rather than implied, the same boundary `tests/ui` already declares
