## Item

**`finalizedBlockHashes` is an array, and the transport kept only `.at(-1)` — right for the head, wrong for ingestion**

## Spec ref

10 §4.1, §6.5

## Status

**verified 2026-08-05 while wiring F8's loop to the transport**

## Result

`chainHead_v1_follow` reports finalization as an **array**, because several blocks can finalize at once, and `ChainHeadConnection` keeps `hashes.at(-1)` as its finalized head. Correct for *"where is the chain now"* — and an ingestion consumer built on it skips every intermediate block of a multi-block finalization. The local index *would* notice, because the loop turns unseen blocks into holes rather than spanning them (V-121), but it would show holes **constantly, under entirely normal operation**, and a gap indicator that is always on tells a user nothing. New `onFinalized(listener)` emits every hash **in order**, placed after `#announcePinned` so a listener reading at the hash it was handed finds it pinned. Also worth recording: the first version of the comment beside it claimed emission must also precede `#unpin` *"so a listener never sees a released hash"* — **not true**, since `#unpin` takes `prunedBlockHashes`, a set disjoint from the finalized ones. Corrected rather than left, because a reassurance with nothing behind it is the thing later readers rely on. 4 mutations, 0 survived — including reverse-order emission and a `unsubscribe` that does not unsubscribe
