## Result

Three read-only Codex reviews (`gpt-5.6-luna`, `max`, `--sandbox read-only`) returned 25 findings across the bundle controls, the pipeline and the literal gate. **What was proven after fixing.** The **built** `dist/sw.js` — loaded into a `vm` with a fabricated `ServiceWorkerGlobalScope` — refuses a tampered body, a `404` carrying HTML, a forged cache entry and a path the release does not contain; deleting the 404 refusal kills 1 test and trusting the cache on read kills 1. The **literal gate rewritten on the TypeScript AST** catches all five evasions the review named: a typed binding (`MaxPayloadBytes: number = 65536`), `1n << 63n`, `0x10000`, plus the two decimal forms — injected into a real package and each reverted. Its witness now asserts **per declared line** rather than per rule, with two negative controls (a regex literal and a string containing `/*`) that must produce nothing; 11/11 expectations fire, 0 undeclared. **`verifySri` recomputes rather than counts** — appending a byte to a built asset fails `release:check` with the declared and actual digests, which a presence check cannot see. The **allowlist enforcement is its own exported call**, so the test exercises the `throw` rather than the diff that feeds it. Every app gate green (20), and the three known reds are unchanged and unrelated (the v24 chain feed vs the v25 contract, blocked on PR #232)
