## Item

**What did a failed `quote()` decode render, and under which badge?**

## Spec ref

10 §2.2; 11 §11.5, §11.12 E6; 15 §2 INV-FE-9

## Status

**measured 2026-08-07** (5 mutants M12–M16, 0 survived)

## Result

**`0.000000 USDC`, three times, each with a `verified-finalized` badge.** `market-reads.ts` substituted `NO_QUOTE = {cost: 0n, fee: 0n}` and `derive`d it from the read, so `cost`, `fee` and the buy total all carried the read's own pin; `market-trade.tsx` rendered them through `Amount` under *\"What the chain says this costs\"*. 10 §2.2 assigns that status *\"only to values read through smoldot with storage proofs checked, or computed client-side purely from such values\"* and 11 §11.5 forbids rendering a fail-closed zero quote as a market price — this is V-183's `balance-reads.ts` defect one module over. Repaired: `QuoteAgreement.fromChain` and the screen's `quote.fromChain` are `undefined` on a decode failure, a `BalanceUnreadable`-shaped refusal renders in the figure's place, and `tradeBlocks` pushes `P-1 quote()` with **no** code — `FE-CHAIN-005` asserts a disagreement, which an undecoded quote is not. The sell-side balance row is deliberately still evaluated, since it compares holdings against the amount and not against the quote (M16)
