## Item

**The `stamp()` producer mint was invisible *because* the consumers were weak, and fixing the consumers is what made it a type error**

## Spec ref

`app/src/features/tx/src/core-screen-reads.ts`; `app/packages/chain-client/src/provenance.ts`; 10 §2.1, §2.2; 15 §2 INV-FE-1

## Status

**measured, 2026-08-06**

## Result

The reader had a local `stamp(at, value)` returning a hand-written `verified-finalized` object — V-182's `market-reads.ts` defect, in the file that feeds S5's and S6's precondition gates. It typechecked for one reason only: the model leaves were `Verified<T>`, and a status object satisfies that. The producer and the consumer halves are therefore **one defect**, which is worth recording because the two were reported and fixed as separate findings on separate branches: once the leaves became `Finalized<T>`, the same `stamp` body stopped compiling — measured, as a rejected mutant, with the exact diagnostic *"Property '[FINALIZED]' is missing"*. `derive(read, compute)` is the sanctioned unary spelling and is ported byte-identical from `track-f/f7b-render`, so the two branches reconcile to one definition rather than two
