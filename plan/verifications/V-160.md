## Item

**The client's hasher set was derived from this repository's pallets and was missing `Identity`, which two frozen read surfaces use**

## Spec ref

02 §7.6; 10 §5.4; 11 §11.5

## Status

**fixed 2026-08-06**

## Result

`StorageHasher` shipped as `Blake2_128Concat | Twox64Concat` on the stated grounds that those are the only hashers *in-repo pallets declare* — verified by grep, and true. The read surface is not the in-repo pallets. Counting hashers in the shipped `app/fixtures/chain-feed/2/metadata.scale` (147 map items, 232 plain) gives **130 `Blake2128Concat`, 50 `Twox64Concat` and 4 `Identity`** — three of the last in `pallet_preimage`, and **`Preimage.StatusFor` and `Preimage.PreimageFor` are frozen 02 §7.6 reads** backing 11 §11.5's P-10 rows (*"the payload preimage is noted with a matching hash and length"*, *"pinned and cannot be reaped"*). So a client keying either would have hit `UnsupportedHasherError`: fail-closed, and therefore not dangerous, but two dispatch checks it must mirror were unmirrorable. **The general form is the one worth keeping**: *ask the artifact the client ships, not the source tree it was built from.* The grep was correct and the conclusion wrong, because the question was scoped to the wrong corpus — the same error shape as V-159 one measurement earlier. Fixed by adding a `Preimage.StatusFor` entry to the runtime fixture (with an `Identity` column in the hasher section, published even though the answer is the input verbatim — the hasher that looks too simple to get wrong is the one whose wrong output nobody notices), extending the coverage assertions on both sides, and taking `Identity` from `@polkadot-api/substrate-bindings` rather than writing `(x) => x` so all three come from one place. Mutation-checked: `Identity: Blake2128Concat` is killed by 3 tests. **Two further facts the measurement turned up.** Metadata spells it `Blake2128Concat` and FRAME spells it `Blake2_128Concat` — one underscore apart, so a metadata-driven caller must map, and the negative test now uses the metadata spelling as its unknown-hasher case rather than an invented name. And **02 §7.6 freezes a storage item the SDK has deprecated**: `Preimage.StatusFor` is `#[deprecated]` in favour of `RequestStatusFor`. Both are live in this runtime's metadata and both are `Identity`-hashed, so nothing breaks today; the fixture keys the **frozen** one under a scoped `#[allow(deprecated)]` with the reason written down, because keying the replacement would verify a surface the client does not read and leave the one it does unverified.
