## Item

**The market rate for a treasury-funded parachain collator** — `collator.comp_epoch` is the one 13 §1 default that is a market price rather than a derivation, and E5 pass 1 declined to set it on the stated grounds that no evidence anchored it. That was true of this repository and untrue of the world, so it was checked against live sources rather than carried.

## Spec ref

13 §1 `collator.comp_epoch`; 08 §2.4, §10.1; 00 D-15

## Status

complete (2026-07-31, E5 pass 2)

## Result

**Primary source — Polkadot OpenGov referendum #1870 (status: Executed / Passed):** funds **38** system-parachain collators (AssetHub 8, BridgeHub 8, People 8, Coretime 4, Collectives 2, Bulletin 8) at **$250/collator/month** from March onward, reduced from $300 in the superseded proposal, plus a fixed **$425/month** hosting line, **$750/month** curators (3 × 250) and a **$1,000/month** coordinator; total ask $146,700 = 99,795.92 DOT at $1.47/DOT. Fully loaded per collator = (250×38 + 425 + 750 + 1,000)/38 = **$307.24/month**; at 17.393 epochs/yr that is **211.97 USDC/epoch**. **Corroborating source — published dedicated-server pricing at the official collator spec** (docs.polkadot.com run-a-collator: 4+ cores / 8+ recommended, 32 GB RAM min / 64 GB recommended, 200+ GB NVMe with both parachain and relay chain pruned, 100+ Mbps): $180–300/month bare, $300–600/month with monitoring and alerting — so the referendum rate is at-cost, not margin-bearing, and 500 USDC/epoch ($724.70/month) sits above the top of the monitored band. **A third figure was found and rejected as an anchor:** a commercial estimate of $2,000–5,000/month per collator "in server and engineering costs", which bundles engineering headcount and is not governance-approved; it is recorded because it is what the superseded 2,000 seed ($2,898.81/month) happens to match, and matching an unsourced estimate is not evidence. **Applied:** seed 2,000 → 500 (the registry minimum, 2.36× the loaded anchor); bounds unchanged. **Known limit of the anchor, stated rather than smoothed:** a Polkadot system-parachain operator already runs Polkadot infrastructure and therefore quotes a *marginal* cost, while a new chain's operators amortize nothing — the 2.36× margin exists to absorb exactly that, and it is why the registry minimum was taken rather than the anchor itself.
