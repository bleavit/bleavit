## Item

**`System.Events` cannot be frozen by expansion: its layout is 2,265,785 characters and drifts on any pallet's new event**

## Spec ref

02 §6, §7.6

## Status

measured 2026-08-04

## Result

Rendering `Vec<EventRecord<RuntimeEvent>>` in full produces a 2.2 MB string — 12× the entire manifest — because `RuntimeEvent` transitively contains every event of every pallet. Worse than the size: the entry's drift signal would fire whenever *any* unrelated pallet gained an event, which is the definition of a checker people switch off. Eliding `RuntimeEvent` gives **236 characters** and leaves the `EventRecord{phase,event,topics}` container fully checked. The elision is sound **only** because 02 §6 freezes that subtree event-by-event (125 manifest entries); the rule adopted is therefore *elide only what another frozen entry covers*, and `Referenda.ReferendumInfoFor` / `Scheduler.Agenda` embed `OriginCaller`, which nothing else freezes, so both stay expanded at ~29 KB each despite being the two largest entries in the file
