## Item

**FE-P3: SharedWorker cannot host the shipped smoldot worker, and the API the gate names is not smoldot's**

## Spec ref

10 §4.4, §12

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07, negative, by pinned-source reading**

## Result

10 §12 asks about *"SharedWorker with `startFromWorker`"*. `smoldot@3.3.2` exports no `startFromWorker`. Its worker split is `smoldot/worker`'s `run(messagePort: MessagePort)` against a client-side `ClientOptions.portToWorker`. `startFromWorker` is PAPI's wrapper, typed `(worker: Worker, options?) => Client`, and it calls `worker.onmessage = …` then `worker.postMessage(port1, [port1])`. A `SharedWorker` is not a `Worker` and carries neither member. **The decisive half is the worker side.** `@polkadot-api/smoldot/worker` is three lines over the bare global `postMessage` and the bare global `onmessage`. A `SharedWorkerGlobalScope` has neither. It delivers connections through `onconnect` instead. So the shipped module cannot run in a SharedWorker at all, whatever a browser matrix would report. A SharedWorker deployment needs a bespoke worker entry driving `run()` with the port from `onconnect`. That is buildable and it is not what this client uses. **The client was already right.** `packages/chain-client/src/light-client.ts` types its option `worker: Worker` and documents the dedicated-worker construction form. 10 §4.4's *"SharedWorker remains a desktop optimization, never a correctness dependency"* now rests on a mechanism instead of on caution. **Still open in FE-P3:** Safari Web Locks behaviour, BroadcastChannel snapshot latency and Android dual-instance memory. Each needs the device lab.
