So the repair is delegation rather than a field rename: `CoverageView` renders `CoveredHistoryDisclosure` for all three of `CoveredHistory`'s emptiness channels and keeps only what F25 does not answer on the read path, which is §6.3's complete-within summary, the distinct sources and each range's edge fact. **F25's own assertion held and was weaker than it read.** *"a migration discard has one renderer, not one per path"* compares `bootDisclosure` against `historyDisclosure`, and **both sides of its `deepEqual` are produced by `chartDiscardItem`** — so it can fail only if that one module grows two renderers, and it can see none anywhere else.
