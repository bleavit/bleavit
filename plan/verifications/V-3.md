## Result

Verified via docs.polkadot.com (coretime-renewal + obtain-coretime), the Polkadot wiki (Agile Coretime) and `pallet_broker` rustdoc: bulk renewal = `broker.renew(core)` on the Coretime chain, **Signed origin, price-capped** ("rent-controlled") renewal charged at the sale's renewal price; the payer is whoever signs — for a parachain that is its **funded account on the Coretime chain** (sovereign account; auto-renewal exists upstream, paritytech/polkadot-sdk#4424). B4's obligation is therefore exactly the 09 §4 funding leg: a DOT reserve transfer to the chain's renewal account there; no `Transact` needed for funding. Exact renewal-price *values* stay ops-verified at Phase-2/3 (V-12 family).
