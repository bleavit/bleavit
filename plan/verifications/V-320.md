## Item

**Did a provider read really satisfy the S4 redemption payout precondition on the shipped code, and did anything in `app/src` call it?**

## Spec ref

11 §11.4 rule 4, §11.5 rule 5; 10 §2.1, §2.3(c); 15 §2 INV-FE-1

## Status

**measured 2026-08-07** (R-6 review of F7b, on `integration/track-f-v2`)

## Result

**Yes to the first, and no to the second — so the exposure was latent rather than live.** `RedemptionRateReadings.metadataBps`/`paramsPerbill`, `RedemptionInputs.gross`/`settlementScore`/`minSplit` and the `minSplit` parameters of `redemptionRateBlocks` and `feeThreshold` were all `Verified<T>`, which admits `provider`, `stale-cache`, `derived-local` and `external-proposal`. Driven with a `provider`-status rate and `MinSplit`, `quoteRedemption` returns `kind: 'charged'` with a `net`, and `mayPrepareRedemption` returns `true`; the net is emitted through `AlwaysVisible` because 11 §11.2 constraint 3 names it. `grep -rn 'quoteRedemption' app/src` finds only the barrel re-export — the only callers are the two screens suites — so no shipped path fed it a provider read. Repaired by retyping every leaf `Finalized<T> | undefined`; the fixture and the control are V-321
