## Item

**Does a second `Chain` handle over an already-added chain spec cost a second sync?** (SQ-1013 — the compat probe attaches its own handle beside the reader's, and 10 §9.4 budgets three chain specs and one Asset Hub client)

## Spec ref

10 §4.1, §9.4; 11 §11.9.1

## Status

**verified 2026-08-08 against the pinned artifact — negative, and the vendor forbids the workaround**

## Result

Read from `smoldot@3.3.2`'s own `public-types.d.ts` in the lockfile-resolved package, not from documentation on the web. `addChain` states that smoldot *"will automatically de-duplicate chains if multiple identical chains are added, in order to save resources"*, and goes further than permitting it: *"When the same client is used for multiple different purposes, you are in fact strongly encouraged to trust smoldot and not attempt to de-duplicate chains yourself, as determining whether two chains are identical is complicated and might have security implications."* So the transient probe handle is a **lookup, not a second sync**, and the hand-rolled sharing an earlier reading of §11.9.1 would have implied is the worse engineering rather than the thriftier one. Recorded because the opposite conclusion is the intuitive one and would have sent a session to rewrite a correct connector
