## Result

The third instance of V-182's helper, and the one whose substance was right: every value it badged — `positionId`, `instrument`, `balance`, and the four vault-projection leaves — really was part of what `crossCheckedCall` returned, so nothing on this screen was a false statement. What was missing was the **reason** the badge was true. The helper took its pin from `reader.at` rather than from the answer, and `projectVault` received it as a parameter, so a closure that had never seen a read decided the provenance of a settlement branch. Two consequences follow from the shape rather than from today's behaviour: a later edit reading one field from elsewhere inherits the pin by sitting beside the others, and the module was structurally indistinguishable from `market-reads.ts`, which *was* wrong. `derive(raw, …)` fixes the direction of the dependency, and `projectVault` now takes the read instead of a stamp. **The pin test needed a disagreeing double to exist at all**: `reader.at` and the call's own status are equal in every real reading, which is exactly why the stamp survived review — so the test double answers at a different block and the leaves must follow the answer. Proved live by mutation: a `providerRead` substituted into the vault arm fails only that assertion, so the vault half is not carried by the three above it
