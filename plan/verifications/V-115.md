## Item

**`PhaseFlags` is the only phase surface the contract freezes, so two spec conditions were unperformable — and one of them skipped a containment control**

## Spec ref

02 §7.3; 11 §11.9.1, §11.10

## Status

**verified 2026-08-05 and repaired under R-1**

## Result

Found by taking the sudo-banner defect one step further: if the client could not compare a phase *number*, could the spec? `Constitution.PhaseFlags` is the **only** phase-related frozen surface and it is a `u32` bitset — there is **no governance-phase integer** anywhere in 02. Yet §11.10 said the banner clears when a read *"shows Phase ≥ 4"* and §11.9.1 gated D-13's exposure caps on *"while PhaseFlags < Phase 4"*. Not loose wording: a bitset compares as a number **without error**, so a client implementing either literally gets a confident wrong answer. The real bootstrap value `17` satisfies `17 ≥ 4` — **hiding the sudo banner on a chain running sudo** — and fails `17 < 4`, which **skips the Phase-3 exposure caps entirely** during exactly the window D-13 built them for. The second is the worse of the two: the banner is a warning, the caps are the containment. Both conditions are the single bit 4, and both sections now say so, with one normative note in §11.10 explaining why the comparison is unperformable rather than merely imprecise. No 02 change and no contract bump — the surface was always right; the prose describing how to read it was not
