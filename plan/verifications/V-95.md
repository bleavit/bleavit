## Item

chainHead pins: does the transport ever send `chainHead_v1_unpin`?

## Spec ref

10 §4.1, §9.4

## Status

complete (2026-08-03)

## Result

**NO — never, anywhere.** chainHead pins every announced block until told otherwise and the node's budget is finite, so this did not degrade gradually: it accumulated until the node ended the subscription and every read failed at once. Fixed with a bounded pin window (16 blocks ≈ 3 min at a 12 s slot) rather than a reader refcount — a refcount is more precise and leaks the moment one caller forgets to release, reintroducing the same unbounded resource behind a better API, whereas a window depends on nothing a caller does. Found alongside it and not in the report: `#headerNumbers` was never evicted either, growing once per finalized block for the life of the tab. Raised by the Codex connector on PR #229
