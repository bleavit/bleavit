## Item

**Had anything in `app/src` ever imported `@bleavit/providers`?**

## Spec ref

10 §10.1, §10.2

## Status

**measured 2026-08-07** (F23)

## Result

**Nothing had.** Before this change, `grep -rn "@bleavit/providers" app/src` returned no match: the package was declared in `src/features/analysis/package.json` and referenced in its `tsconfig.json`, and no module in any of the three compilation units imported a symbol from it. So every mechanism 10 §8 mandates existed, was tested, and was called by nothing outside `app/tests/providers` and `app/tools/snapshot`. The seven F23 modules are the first consumers. This is the measured form of what F9's row means by *"each is still a function nobody calls, which is the state that looks finished in a status table and is not"*, and it is why the firewall edge (`analysis` may import `providers`; `tx` may not) was until now non-vacuous only in the negative-compilation corpus.
