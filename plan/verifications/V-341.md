## Item

**Could F23's badge assertion `badges.size === 1` ever have failed for the reason its message states?**

## Spec ref

10 §2.1, §8.4; 15 §2 INV-FE-9

## Status

**measured 2026-08-07** (control experiment: the M7 mutant run against both forms of the assertion)

## Result

**No.** `badgeCopyFor` is a pure switch over `VerificationStatus` and the loop passed `providerStatus('pub-1', wouldBadgeSampled(report))` three times with `wouldBadgeSampled` false for all three — byte-identical arguments, so the set had one member unconditionally. The message *"the three no longer share one badge — re-point this test"* describes a third per-row channel, which would break that call site at **compile** time and never reach the assertion. Measured rather than argued: with `mint.ts`'s `sampled` mutated to `spotCheck.reach !== 'ceiling'` (two of the three become `sampled: true`), the **old** form did not fire at all — only the unrelated expression-binding test failed — while the new form, whose statuses come from `mintSnapshotRows`' own output, fails with exactly that message
