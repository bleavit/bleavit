## Item

**The relay's bonding and slash-deferral durations, for FE-P8's long-range bound**

## Spec ref

10 §2.4, §12

## Status

**verified 2026-08-05 against the Fellowship-maintained Polkadot relay runtime and the Paseo relay runtime**

## Result

`SessionsPerEra = 6` with a 4 h epoch, so an era is 24 h; `BondingDuration = 28` eras and `SlashDeferDuration = 27` eras. Read out of `relay/polkadot/src/lib.rs` and `relay/paseo/src/lib.rs` rather than assumed, and **the two agree exactly** — which is the finding, not a formality: the release targets both, testnets routinely shorten their bonding duration, and a client shipping Polkadot's 28 onto a chain with 7 would be wrong in the **unsafe** direction, presenting reads as verified for three weeks after the guarantee lapsed. One bound therefore covers both targets, and 10 §2.4 requires the figures to travel in the release document rather than as frontend literals, so a relay amendment moves them
