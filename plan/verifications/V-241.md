## Item

**Is `whole-document` reachable with `compared: 0`, contradicting its own definition?**

## Spec ref

10 §8.4; `packages/providers/src/snapshot.ts`

## Status

**measured 2026-08-07** (F23)

## Result

**Yes, on the shipped code.** `spotCheckSnapshot` initialises `reach` to `whole-document` and rewrites it to `above-window-only` only when `compared === 0 && outOfReach > 0`; `checkCoverage` raises no finding for an **empty** `coverage` array (it iterates the array and checks each `op` against it, and both loops are vacuous). So an admitted document that covers no blocks ends the pass at `whole-document` with `compared: 0, outOfReach: 0` — the arm whose declared meaning is *"every covered block was asked about **and at least one was compared**"*. The mint is safe (`sampled` already requires `compared > 0`); the **screen** was the exposure, since `whole-document` is documented as the one arm a surface may read as *fully re-derived*. Handled in the renderer rather than by widening the union: `reachReading` is a total function of the arm **and** the counts, giving five readings over four arms, and the empty document gets `nothing-to-re-derive` with its own copy. Whether `SpotCheckReach` should gain a fifth arm, or `admitSnapshot` should refuse a document covering nothing, is F9's to rule — this row records the measurement and the screen-level containment.
