## Item

**FE-P1's four unanswered halves, and the row that called the gate resolved covered only two of six**

## Spec ref

10 §2.3, §5.2, §12; 11 §11.3

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07 by pinned-source reading**

## Result

10 §12 asks FE-P1 for six things. V-87 answered the compatibility API and V-96 answered the pjs-signer exports. PLAN recorded the gate as resolved on that basis. Four halves were still open, one of them named in a normative clause that had softened itself while it waited. **At-block query options.** `PullOptions.at` accepts `"best"`, `"finalized"` or a block hash, and **defaults to finalized**. `watchValue` takes `"best"` or `"finalized"` and defaults to finalized. `watchEntries` also defaults to finalized, but types its options as `{at: "best"}` only. So its default is safe and the one spelling a caller can write is the unsafe one. The flag it passes down is `isLastArgOptional && lastArg.at === "best"`, checked in the implementation rather than read off the type. **Best-block observable.** `bestBlocks$` and `getBestBlocks()` return an array, best first and finalized last, beside `finalizedBlock$`, `getFinalizedBlock()` and `blocks$`. Its docstring states that *"some blocks might not get reported"*, so `bestBlocks$` is not a complete feed and cannot drive ingest. `blocks$` is the one with a stated parent-before-child guarantee. **Fee estimation.** `getEstimatedFees(from, txOptions?)` returns `PaymentInfo.partial_fee` from `TransactionPaymentApi_query_info`, over an extrinsic signed with a fake signature for length. Both the call and the runtime context are issued at `null`, which V-82 traced to the **finalized** hash, and the docstring agrees: *"Estimate fees against the latest known `finalizedBlock`"*. Three traps ride with it. A `TxOptions.at` moves only the fake extrinsic's mortality anchor and never the `query_info` call. `partial_fee` excludes the tip. `partial_fee` is denominated in the native token even under `ChargeAssetTxPayment`. **`CheckMetadataHash` handling.** PAPI signs it as `both(zero, zero)`, so **mode 0, Disabled, unconditionally**, unless the caller supplies `customSignedExtensions.CheckMetadataHash`. That is the override B21 will need, and it is the only one. **What changed in code:** the fee half was the one 10 §2.3 had qualified, so `estimateFee` now takes `Finalized<bigint>` and `Finalized<AdmittedRate>` and returns through `meet`.
