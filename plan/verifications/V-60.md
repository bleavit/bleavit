## Item

**Provenance of 08 §10.2/§10.4's simulation figures — they are NOT in the committed artifact**

## Spec ref

08 §10.2, §10.4; 15 §4.9; `simulation/results/phase0-calibration.json`

## Status

recorded 2026-07-29 (E1 spec pass)

## Result

**What was measured:** turnover ratio `τ` (all-book 5.8× min-book contest capital median, mean 7.29, p95 14.32; 47.9 % round-trip churn; ≈ 3.0 ex-churn all-book, ≈ 2.2 decision-pair), held-capital-to-floor ratio (1.5–1.8× `dec.v_min`, median 1.76 among passing books), and gross fee per proposal (median 20,864, mean 48,691, decision-grade median 24,686; all-at-floor cohort mean 12,149) at `mkt.fee` = 30 bps. **How:** an **ad-hoc instrumented re-run** of the 15 §4.9 Phase-0 model with fee and volume instrumentation added — *not* a read of the committed artifact. **Status: unpublished.** `simulation/results/phase0-calibration.json` carries **no** turnover or fee series; its only fee-related key is `.config.mkt_fee`, the input rate, because `ExecutedBook.fees` is computed and discarded (15 §4.9 obligation 2, SQ-506). None of these figures is Merkle-bound, none is covered by `run-calibration.py --check`, and none may be cited as artifact-backed. **They are also floors, not point estimates:** obligation 3's `book.events.clear()` truncation under-reports lifetime volume 2.5–3.3× for the 51 % of proposals that extend, so every volume-derived figure is low — which makes §10.4's crossover an upper bound on the depth required and the "floor cohort short by ≈ 3×" an upper bound on the shortfall. 08 §10.2 and §10.4 were relabelled accordingly in the same pass. Closing this needs obligations 2/3/5 of 15 §4.9 plus a `--full` regeneration (SQ-506, SQ-508).
