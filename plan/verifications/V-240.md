## Item

**Do the three `compared: 0` situations `SpotCheckReach` was split to separate really render identically today?**

## Spec ref

10 §8.4; 10 §2.1; `app/packages/ui/src/badge.tsx`

## Status

**measured 2026-08-07** (F23)

## Result

**Yes, byte-identically.** `badgeCopyFor`'s `provider` arm reads exactly two fields — `providerId` and `sampled` — and appends *"and spot-checked against the chain"* only when `sampled` is true. All three situations end with `compared: 0`, so `mintSnapshotRows` writes `sampled: false` for all three and the mark is `unverified — X` with the same title in every case. Driven out of the **real** `spotCheckSnapshot` rather than asserted: a `below-window` checker gives `window-floor`, an `above-window` checker over a document smaller than the ceiling gives `above-window-only`, and the same checker with the ceiling reached first gives `ceiling`. The measurement is kept as a live test (`the three situations the row badge cannot tell apart are told apart here`) with the badge set asserted to have **size 1**, so if the badge ever gains a per-row channel the test fails and is re-pointed rather than deleting a claim that stopped being true. The complement is asserted in the same test: the three reach disclosures are three distinct renderings, and no one of them contains another's heading. **Why it matters beyond tidiness:** F9 *admits* the ceiling case rather than refusing it, and the whole justification is that the limit is disclosed — with no consumer of `reach`, that justification was circular.
