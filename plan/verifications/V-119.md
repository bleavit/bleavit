## Item

**§11.8.3's `nav()` view forbids three renderings you would naturally build**

## Spec ref

11 §11.8.3; 02 §4; 08 §1.2/§4.1

## Status

**verified 2026-08-05 by building each control and mutating it**

## Result

The section reads as a list of fields; it is really a list of *prohibitions*. (1) The account balances are a **partial** view — nine `ops.*` lines have no field in `NavView` — so the obvious table with a sum at the bottom is **wrong**, and a reader who sees a decomposition assumes it decomposes and reads the residual as a chain error. Every line therefore carries a **required** `partialNote` and the module exports **no function that sums them**. (2) *"The FE never renders full backing while the flag is set"* cannot be a discipline — it is one `<Amount datum={nav.total}>` away at all times — so the `haircut` arm of `NavPresentation` has **no `headline` field at all**; adding one is a compile error, confirmed by mutation. The **residual is stated**: a component also holding the raw `NavView` can still render `total` by hand, so the render test asserts the haircut output carries no bare `NAV` label, with the full-backing render as the anti-vacuity control. (3) An income figure *"MUST … be labelled as the partial, window-bounded total it is — never as lifetime protocol revenue"*, so `WindowedIncome` **requires** `fromBlock`/`toBlock` and there is no constructor for an unbounded one; `InsuranceSwept` is not in 02 §5's ingest set and has **no slot**, which is the enforceable form of *"no income or reserve figure may depend on observing it"*. Class-floor distance is **continuous and measured against `spendable_nav`**, never `total` — under a haircut that is 0 and every floor is genuinely unmet, while measuring `total` would report floors met on a spend the protocol refuses
