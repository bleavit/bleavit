## Item

**The `ChainHeadTransport` interface shipped synchronous and cannot be implemented**

## Spec ref

10 §4.1, §4.2; INV-FE-1

## Status

found + fixed 2026-08-03 while writing the smoldot transport (F3)

## Result

The read layer landed with `pinnedBlock()`/`storage()`/`call()` returning values rather than promises, and its suite passed. smoldot runs in a **Web Worker** (10 §4.1) and every answer arrives by `postMessage`, so the only synchronous implementation is a main-thread block on `Atomics.wait` — which needs cross-origin isolation the Arweave distribution does not control (12 §5.1), and which freezes the UI for the duration of a proof-backed read. The interface was therefore satisfiable by a **test double and by nothing else**: exactly the failure where a mock agrees with a contract no real implementation can meet, and the suite reports the agreement. Fixed by making the interface async and driving the suite through the real `ChainHeadConnection` rather than a bespoke adapter.
