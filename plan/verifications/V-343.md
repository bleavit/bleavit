## Item

**Does `archive_v1_storage`'s `storageDone` prove a storage iteration finished?** (SQ-612's pagination half — it decides what a snapshot's `observed` ranges may honestly claim)

## Spec ref

10 §8.2, §8.5.1

## Status

**verified 2026-08-07 — negative, and the negative is the useful answer**

## Result

Read from the published Polkadot JSON-RPC interface specification's `archive` group rather than inferred. Two findings. **(1)** The group is real and versioned `v1`, with the ten methods `archive_v1_body`, `_call`, `_finalizedHeight`, `_genesisHash`, `_hashByHeight`, `_header`, `_storage`, `_storageDiff`, `_stopStorage`, `_stopStorageDiff` — so §8.5.1 binds to a surface that exists, and `paginationStartKey` is real and is valid only for `descendantsValues`/`descendantsHashes`. **(2) The specification says `storageDone` is *"always generated after all storage events have been generated"* and says nothing whatever about early termination, response caps, or discarded items.** So `storageDone` is a server's claim and not a completeness proof, and a reader that treated it as one would publish an `observed` span it never observed — a document that passes every §8.4 screen, because the movements it does carry are consistent, while describing a history that did not happen. §8.5.1 therefore makes completeness something a reader **establishes**: continue with `paginationStartKey` until nothing new appears, and record a span only when every read covering it concluded. Recording the silence matters as much as the method list — a later session reading only the method names would reasonably assume the terminator terminates
