## Item

**The multisig client refused what the runtime accepts: a threshold-ready call could not be completed by anyone**

## Spec ref

11 §11.3; 15 §4.8

## Status

**found by Codex review, confirmed against pinned source 2026-08-05**

## Result

`deriveApproval` returned `already-approved` on list membership alone. In pinned `pallet-multisig` **49.0.0** (`operate`), the execution branch `maybe_call.filter(|_| approvals >= threshold)` is evaluated at line 693 — **before** the `AlreadyApproved` arm at 741 — so once `approvals.len() >= threshold` *anyone* supplying the full call dispatches it, already-approved or not. `approve_as_multi` stores a hash-only approval and never carries a call, so a 2-of-2 whose members both used it sits exactly there: threshold met, nothing dispatched, and the canonical client told **both** members they had nothing to do. A client refusing a lawful action, which is the direction 15 §4.8's mirror rule forbids. New `completes` arm, ordered threshold-first because that ordering *is* the fix; `alreadyApproved` is carried for wording only, since `maybe_pos` is filtered at `approvals < threshold` and no approval is recorded either way. Also corrected a stale citation to 46.0.0. 3 mutations, 0 survived
