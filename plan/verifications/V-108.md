## Item

**React 19's patch pins, read from the registry rather than assumed**

## Spec ref

10 §1; 01 §9

## Status

**verified 2026-08-04 against `registry.npmjs.org`**

## Result

10 §1 makes *"a Vite-built **React 19** + TypeScript SPA"* normative, and 01 §9's frontend-stack line pins every other npm package to an exact patch (`polkadot-api` 2.1.8, `smoldot` 3.3.1, `vite` 8.1.4, `dexie` 4.4.4, …) while naming no React version at all — so F7 cannot start without resolving it, and R-2 forbids resolving it by assumption. Current published: **`react` 19.2.8**, **`react-dom` 19.2.8**, **`@types/react` 19.2.18**, **`@types/react-dom` 19.2.4**, **`@vitejs/plugin-react` 6.0.5**. Recorded here rather than in 01 §9 until F7 actually installs them, since a pin nothing consumes is a claim about a dependency the repository does not have.
