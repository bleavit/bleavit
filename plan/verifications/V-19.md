## Item

Coretime chain accepts DOT only as a relay teleport (renewal-funding route shape, 09 §4)

## Spec ref

09 §4; 09 §6.1

## Status

complete (2026-07-16, B4 re-review)

## Result

Verified against the live `coretime-polkadot` runtime `xcm_config.rs` (polkadot-fellows/runtimes, main): `type IsReserve = ();` and `type IsTeleporter = ConcreteAssetFromSystem<DotRelayLocation>;` ("Only allow teleportation of DOT") — a `DepositReserveAsset` leg into the Coretime chain would fail `UntrustedReserveLocation` (the B4 adversarial review's blocker 3). The renewal route is therefore two-legged: local `InitiateReserveWithdraw` of DOT to the relay, then relay-side `InitiateTeleport` to `Parachain(1005)`; the teleport executes **on the relay** — Bleavit's own `IsTeleporter` stays `()` (09 §6.1 scope clarified in the same session).
