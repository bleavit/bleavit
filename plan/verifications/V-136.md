## Item

**FE-P10's capacity half was partly answerable from the tree after all — and the answer was a defect: the guard's own upgrade-apply call is 256 KiB short of the bound its own pallet publishes**

## Spec ref

11 §11.8.4; 09 §2.2; 02 §9; 10 §12

## Status

**verified 2026-08-05 by execution, fixed**

## Result

V-97 recorded FE-P10's demand half as 4 MiB and its capacity half as not answerable from the pinned tree, because smoldot's pool and gossip limits live inside the Rust wasm. That reading took *capacity* to mean *smoldot's capacity* and stopped. The chain has a capacity of its own, it is in this repository, and it turned out to be the binding one. `CheckWeight::check_block_length` measures the encoded extrinsic against the ceiling of the call's **`DispatchClass`**; this runtime sets every class to 5 MiB and then scales **only Normal** by `NORMAL_DISPATCH_RATIO` (75 %) to **3,932,160 B**. `frame_system::apply_authorized_upgrade` is `Operational` — upstream's deliberate choice, frame-system 48.0.0 — so it sits under **5,242,880 B** and is fine. `pallet_execution_guard::apply_authorized_upgrade`, the guard's own *permissionless second phase*, `CallDomain::Public` in the SafetyFilter and carrying the whole image as one argument, declared **no class at all** and was therefore **Normal**. Measured rather than reasoned: the new test reports **4,194,310 B** encoded against a **3,932,160 B** ceiling, a **262,150 B** shortfall. An image in that gap passes the `BoundedVec`, passes `commit_recovery_image`'s `len` check and passes the descriptor qualification, is authorized on-chain, and is then refused **in the pool** with `InvalidTransaction::ExhaustsResources` — no dispatch, no error to decode, no event — on the one call whose entire purpose is liveness. Today's runtime image is **1,892,381 B**, so nothing was broken yet; that is exactly why no test found it, and why the fix ships with one that re-derives the relation from **both** sides (bound from the pallet `Config`, ceiling from `BlockLength`, class from a real `RuntimeCall`) instead of restating either number. **Anti-vacuity: the one-line class change was reverted and the new test watched failing with that message, then restored.** The lesson is FE-P6's, one milestone on — a gate filed as needing hardware had a load-bearing half sitting in pinned source, and the device test would have shown a correct browser and a refusing chain.
