## Item

Which chain answer can the recorded chainHead corpus supply for a snapshot spot check?

## Spec ref

02 §11; 10 §8.4

## Status

complete (2026-08-06)

## Result

**One block, and it is an empty one — which is the forgery direction that matters.** The corpus pins a single block; its number is **1**, decoded from the recorded `chainHead_v1_header` (`parent_hash [32]` then a single-byte compact `0x04`), and its `System.Events` value is two `System.ExtrinsicSuccess` records carrying no ledger movement at all. So the recording answers *"nothing happened here"* — which is exactly what catches a publisher **fabricating** a movement at a block this device can read, the case `admitSnapshot` is structurally unable to see because a fabricated movement with matching balances is internally consistent. The opposite direction (the chain has movements the document deleted) cannot be driven from this corpus and is covered over synthetic observations instead, stated in the suite rather than implied
