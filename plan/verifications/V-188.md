## Item

**S7 asked a stored-record decoder for three fields the stored record does not contain**

## Spec ref

`crates/constitution-core/src/lib.rs` (`ParamRecord`); `runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/views.rs` (`params`); 02 §4, §7.3; 11 §11.2 S7, §11.4 rule 2

## Status

**measured against the runtime, 2026-08-06**

## Result

`constitution_core::ParamRecord` carries `key`, `value`, `min`, `max`, `max_delta`, `cooldown_epochs`, `last_changed_epoch`, `last_change_block`, `class`, `kernel_bounded`. `ParamView` additionally carries `min_next`, `max_next` and `cooldown_blocks`, and the runtime computes all three — the first two from `record.admissible_next_interval()`, the third from `cooldown_epochs × ` the live `epoch.length`, saturating at `u32::MAX` when that key is unreadable. The reader read the raw `Constitution.Params` prefix and handed the bytes to a decoder typed for the **view**, so once wired the decoder would have had to invent the arithmetic or return nothing for three promised dashboard fields. Two independent statements already said not to: 11 §11.2's own S7 Primary-reads column names `params()`, and 02 §7.3's row says the item is *"read via `params()`"*. 11 §11.4 rule 2 is the general form — an exact chain read where a client computation would otherwise stand in, the same reasoning 02 §4 gives for `is_reserved_protocol_destination` being a method (SQ-588)
