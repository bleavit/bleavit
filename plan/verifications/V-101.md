## Item

**Vite pinned at exactly 8.1.4, the version 01 §9 verified — not `latest`**

## Spec ref

01 §9; 12 §1.1

## Status

verified 2026-08-04

## Result

`pnpm view vite dist-tags` reports `latest: 8.2.0`; 01 §9's frontend-stack line pins **8.1.4** as the version it checked against `registry.npmjs.org`, and `pnpm add -E vite@8.1.4` installed it (15 packages). Taking `latest` would have been a silent, unrecorded major-line move of a build tool whose output the reproducibility gate compares byte-for-byte — app-code rule 14 requires a Decision-log entry for that, and none exists. `permaweb-deploy@5.0.0` and `@ardrive/turbo-sdk@1.42.0` were also confirmed to exist and were deliberately **not** installed: they are only reachable through a live gateway (FE-P7), so installing them would add supply-chain surface no gate can exercise
