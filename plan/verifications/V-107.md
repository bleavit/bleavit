## Item

**PAPI's whitelist is global, and a per-chain one silently guts every chain it does not name**

## Spec ref

10 §5.1, §5.2; 02 §11

## Status

**verified 2026-08-04 by measurement; design withdrawn**

## Result

Found by reading the generator while implementing `.papi/README.md`'s own stated plan ("the Asset Hub set … does use a whitelist"), then proven by running it. Two facts make that plan unimplementable. (i) `replacePackageJson` writes `"name": "@polkadot-api/descriptors"` as a **string literal**, so two generated descriptor packages cannot coexist in one pnpm workspace — every chain shares one package, one config, and one `.papi/whitelist.ts`, which `readWhitelist` loads from a fixed cwd-relative path. (ii) `generateMultipleDescriptors` computes `globalWhitelist = whitelist["*"] ?? []`, and **`[]` is truthy**, so a chain with no key of its own gets `applyWhitelist(metadata, [])` — every filter over an empty list, every result empty. Measured on this repo's metadata with a whitelist naming only `assethub_paseo`: `bleavit.ts` **458,662 → 43,258 bytes**, `descriptors.ts` **60,457 → 1,667**. `papi generate` reported nothing wrong. `descriptors:check` *would* have caught the drift — it byte-compares — but as "the Asset Hub whitelist broke generation", and the obvious next move (regenerate and commit) ships the gutted set. The `"*"`-passthrough alternative was rejected as worse than the ~1.3 MB it saves: `descriptors:check` regenerates **with the same whitelist**, so it agrees with itself and could not see a pallet omitted from the passthrough list. No whitelist anywhere is the shipped answer, and `.papi/README.md` now carries the measurement instead of the plan
