## Item

**PAPI *can* supply per-key-position encodings — my own note said it could not, and the design that note justified survived the correction**

## Spec ref

02 §7; 10 §5.1

## Status

**measured 2026-08-06 over 739 storage items and 11 runtime-fixture entries**

## Result

`storage-keys.ts` shipped saying *"PAPI's `getTypedCodecs` cannot supply that: its `args` codec encodes the whole key tuple as one buffer"*. The first clause is false. `args.enc` does encode the whole tuple — but **`args.inner` is an array of one codec per hashed key position**, which is exactly what a double map needs. Measured before being relied on, and over the whole surface rather than by spot check: across **739** items on the two pinned chains (379 Bleavit, 360 Asset Hub Paseo) `args.inner.length` equals the metadata hasher count **every time**, including `Welfare.Snapshots` — the tuple-key case, where it is **1 and not 2**, which is the one place a logical-argument count and a hashed-position count could have diverged. And on all 11 entries of `runtime/bleavit-runtime/fixtures/storage-keys.json`, `inner[i]` re-encodes the pre-image the **runtime itself** published, byte for byte. **The conclusion the wrong claim supported was still right**: `storageKey` taking pre-encoded bytes is the correct interface, because splitting encoding from hashing is what puts the one-versus-two-hash choice at the call site where the metadata answering it lives. So the signature is unchanged and only the justification is repaired. **What it changes is the composition root**, which no longer needs a hand-written pre-image for anything: callers pass typed values and the chain's own codec encodes them. `args.inner` is a `@polkadot-api/substrate-bindings` **internal with no compatibility promise**, so `storageKeyBuilder` refuses unless metadata and codecs **agree on the arity** — neither artifact is presumed right — and the agreement is re-checked per commit over both chains' entire surfaces. Third instance in three days of the same shape: a claim about an artifact, checked against my reasoning instead of the artifact (V-159, V-160).
