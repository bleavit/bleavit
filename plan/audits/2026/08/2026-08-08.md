Integration contract v30 (R-1): the four guardian allowance constants and two playbook maps are frozen.

An R-6 review of F17 found `AllowanceMeter.limit` to be a `Verified<number>` with **no producer** — `Guardian.Allowances` stores used-counters only, no `constant.guardian.*` published a bound, and the screen suite shipped `finalized(3)` for all five powers where the kernel's values are 1, 2, 1 and none. A fabricated provenance stamp deciding a privileged control (INV-FE-1), on the precondition 11 §11.8.2 names by name. **13 §1 called the exposure *"unresolved"*, and that was wrong rather than deferred**: 06 §7 had already mandated it, so the note contradicted its own owning document before it contradicted 11. **Ruled: freeze, do not close S15.** These are compile-time kernel constants and the pallet already carried a `#[pallet::extra_constants]` block, so four accessors are additive and change no behaviour; closing a guardian's core safety power over a compile-time number is the wrong R-7 trade. The same bump freezes `Guardian.PlaybookRegistered` and `Guardian.ActivePlaybooks`, whose refusals fall on the **dispatching** approval, with two forbidden readings stated — registration is admissibility and never a trigger source, and presence blocks `PB-LEDGER-FREEZE` alone, since every other playbook renews in place. **`spec_version` deliberately does not bump**: the counter's contract is a replacement image on a live chain, no runtime is deployed, `git tag` is empty, and v13, v21 and v23 each added metadata constants at `spec_version` 2 — and a bump is not local, since 09 requires the paired recovery image at exactly primary + 1. Once a runtime is deployed a change of this shape bumps like any other, and 02 §13's entry says so rather than leaving it implicit. **The `pause_intake` meter is windowed** and 11 §11.8.2 now says so: outside `PauseIntakeAllowanceWindowEpochs` the stored counter is stale and reads as a full allowance, so a client comparing counter to limit without the window shows an exhausted meter for an available power.
