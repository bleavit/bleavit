## Question

**07 §11's "met by construction, not by hope" fails 19 of 117 lawful configurations, and §7's incident aggregate is under-covered 5.7×.** Sweeping the max-Δ/day-aligned box of (`epoch.length`, `orc.window`, `orc.rounds`), the settlement deadline can land where only round 1 closes — at (201,600, 72,000, 4) the deadline is d13⅓. Safety depends **jointly** on all three keys, not on `epoch.length` alone as the audit claimed. The effect runs opposite to intuition: a truncated ladder makes *lying* harder, because §11 force-neutralizes an unfinished dispute — what it actually drops is the **griefing price**, from 3,750 bps to 250, a **15×** reduction. Separately, an S1 incident produces an exact **9,999.68 bps** welfare swing against the 1,750 bps the §6.3 coverage rule provides — a 40/7 shortfall, crossing over at honest score 0.175013229. §7's premise "until the MetricSpec surface carries `Δs_max`" is **stale**: `ComponentSpec.delta_s_max_bps` exists and is bound-checked; the residual gap is that the incident multiplier `I` is not a `MetricId`. **Ruling needed:** must `I` carry a `Δs_max` and pass the §6.3 screen?
