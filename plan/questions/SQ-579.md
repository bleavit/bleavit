## Question

**11 §11.3 names a QR signing flow and no QR framing standard.** The section says "raw-payload flow (QR/hex + metadata-hash mode) for air-gapped and hardware signers" and stops there. Hex needs no framing and is implemented; **QR does**, and the real formats are external and versioned — UOS for a single frame, and RaptorQ fountain-coding once a payload exceeds one (a Bleavit `execute` payload routinely will). Implementing a plausible chunking would produce a format that looks right, interoperates with no existing signer, and fails in front of someone holding an air-gapped device — R-2 fabrication with a physical failure mode. **Ruling needed:** which standard a Bleavit release targets, and at which version, so the transport can be written against it rather than around it. Until then the raw-payload adapter ships hex-only, with framing delegated to the transport and no QR transport supplied — which is a working air-gapped path, not a stub
