## Question

**OPEN — a value derived from an in-bundle release constant has no `VerificationStatus`.** 10 §2.1 freezes six: four chain-read confidence levels, `derived-local`, and `external-proposal`. F7's S22 clamp display needs a seventh case it cannot express. 11 §11.14.3 lets the encoded limit be bound by *"the client's own policy cap"*, and such a number is neither read from the chain nor requested by a tool: `verified-finalized` claims a read that did not happen, and `external-proposal`'s own copy — *"a value an external tool asked for"* — is simply false about it. Either label is the exact failure INV-FE-9 exists to prevent, on the one surface whose job is showing a user where a number came from. Not urgent and not ignorable: the client ships **no** policy cap, so the case is unreachable today, and `app/src/features/handoff` **fails closed** on it (`UnlabellableClampError`) rather than picking a label. Deferred rather than ruled here because a seventh status is a 10 §2.1 amendment, which INV-FE-9 certification binds to and which 02 §13 makes a contract question — not a screen's call. Options: add `release-constant` to the lattice; or forbid policy caps outright, since 11 §11.14.3's narrowing rule is already satisfied by the chain-derived value alone
