## Question

**Of the couplings 13 rule 7 delegates to "the consuming engine", eight break in one lawful amendment and are checked nowhere.** Rule 7 says "bind at the consuming engine; the seeded static bounds are conservative over-approximations" — but for an equality or a ratio no static box is conservative. Unchecked and one-step breakable: all four `dec.sigma ≤ δ/2` relations and `gate.eps ≤ p_max/2` (both keys carry **no max-Δ**, so a single `set_param` does it, and each is unsafe-permissive because it lowers an adoption hurdle and flows straight into the veto and `effective_reject_1e9`), plus both treasury-cap orderings and the reserve timeout/interval pair, which the audit did not find. `welfare.wP + wA = 1` also breaks in one step (0.60 → 0.65, both inside [0.30, 0.70], Δ 0.05) but is consumer-validated: it raises `BadWeightSum` from the settle crank **and** `do_try_state`, halting rather than defaulting to status quo — a different failure mode that still wants a ruling. Positive controls confirm the mechanism works where it is used: all four `gate.v_min` pairs and `ledger.rdm_fee ≤ mkt.fee` come out boundary-screened. **Ruling needed:** whether rule 7's mandated consumer checks are added, or the two relations are promoted to boundary screening like `gate.v_min` and `ledger.redeem_fee` were. **CORRECTED 2026-08-01 by adversarial review of PR #206, before any ruling was taken.** The original row misread rule 7 and over-counted. Rule 7 says verbatim that these couplings **"bind at the consuming engine"** — so a consumer-validated coupling is *compliant*, not defective, and `welfare.wP + wA = 1` is exactly that (it raises `BadWeightSum` from the settle crank and try-state). This row is therefore an **implementation-conformance** finding — the runtime does not perform the check rule 7 requires — and not a specification gap; its disposition is code, not doc. Three of the eight reported couplings (`trs.cap_proposal ≤ trs.cap_30d ≤ trs.cap_180d` and `res.probe_to < res.probe_int`) appear in **no document** — 08 §1.3 says the caps apply *concurrently* and never requires an ordering — and have been removed. What stands: five relations for which rule 7 mandates a consumer check and none exists
