## Question

**A retained round whose verdict never lands holds its bond stack forever — 07 §11(1)'s reaping has no driver.** §11(1) retains a neutralized `Rounds` entry deliberately, so a later terminal verdict can still dispose of the stack, and bounds that retention explicitly: "Retention is bounded by the track's own schedule (7 d decision + 1 d confirm), after which the stack resolves and the entry is reaped." Nothing implements the *after*. `crank_round_close` skips a `money_settled` game unless a party's current-round obligation is already known, so a round-3 challenge that no `OracleResolution` verdict ever reaches is skipped indefinitely: its held bonds stay held against the I-29 custody identity, and its slot stays occupied against the 128-game `MAX_ROUNDS` bound — a slow-drip way to starve the reporting surface at the cost of one abandoned dispute per slot. Needs the track-schedule expiry the spec already names: once the retention window closes without a verdict, resolve the stack on the no-verdict default (§5's challenger-default rule is the closest existing analogue) and reap the entry. Surfaced by the SQ-182/491 review; deliberately not folded into that PR, which was already touching the deadline path.

## Status

**closed 2026-07-25** — branch `feat/sq492-round-retention-deadline`, integration contract **v15**. Both reapers implemented. Retention: each money-settled game is stamped `retain_until = neutralized_at + kernel::ORC_RETENTION_BLOCKS`, derived from the `OracleResolution` track's own 0/7 d/1 d schedule (06 §2.1) and bound to `configs::TRACKS[5]` by a runtime test so a track retune cannot silently shorten it; `crank_round_close` refunds both stacks and reaps at that block, through a new `BondDisposition::RefundBoth` rather than a second bool. The ruling above stands unchanged. 07 §11(1) now states the bound, the stamping formula and the refund disposition normatively. **The folded-in leak turned out to be the larger of the two:** `ComponentValues` had no reaper at all, so it reaches `MAX_COMPONENT_VALUES = 128` and every further settlement fails `AlreadyFinal` — a total oracle wedge within the first year of mainnet at ~16 entries/epoch, not a slow drip. `reap_component`'s per-triple seam could not be the driver: 07 §13 assigns reaping to \"cohort settlement\", but §11's own SQ-182 resolution establishes that `settle_cohort` reads the welfare *snapshot* and never this map, so the stated trigger named a caller that could not fire. The real last consumer is `record_snapshot(m)` in epoch `m+1`, so the reaper is an epoch cutoff on the boundary crank (`current − COMPONENT_VALUE_RETAINED_EPOCHS`, bounded by 13 §2's new derived `ComponentReapBatch`), and 07 §13 + 02 §7.2 were corrected to say so.
