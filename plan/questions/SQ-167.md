## Question

**PT numbering skew between 15 §4.3 and 03 §11.** 15 §4.3 restates PT-1…PT-7 with PT-6=VOID conservation/PT-7=gate conservation; 03 §11 defines PT-6=VOID reachability, PT-7=pair exactness, PT-8=bounds/key-order. The S1 suites follow 03 §11's numbering and additionally cover 15 §4.3's gate-conservation item.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — 03 §11's PT-1…PT-8 numbering is canonical (it is what the suites and `property-gates.sh` shards use). 15 §4.3 restated over it: the old PT-6/PT-7 obligations folded into PT-6/PT-7 with no coverage lost, PT-8 restored, and a note pinning the single home.
