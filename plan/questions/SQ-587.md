## Question

**RULED 2026-08-04 (R-1) — the gap was real, but both of the question's premises were wrong, and one of them was a spec-reading error I had parked as a user decision.** (i) *Which Asset Hub* was never open: [08](../../docs/architecture/08-treasury-and-economics.md) §2.5 and [09](../../docs/architecture/09-execution-upgrades-and-rollout.md) §6.3 open HRMP to Paseo's Asset Hub at Phase 2 and Polkadot's at Phase 3, so a release pins the Asset Hub of the relay it targets — a per-release property, exactly like the relay, not a standing choice 02 could freeze. (ii) The claimed contradiction with X-11a does not exist: X-11a forbids `Assets.Account(1337, …)` **on Bleavit**, where USDC arrives as a `ForeignAssets` entry, while 02 §8's own Location decodes to `PalletInstance(50)` = `Assets`, `GeneralIndex(1337)`, **on parachain 1000** — 11 §11.9.1's AH-side row and §11.2's S20 row are one asset seen from two chains. **What was real:** 02 froze nothing about the foreign chain, so 10 §5.2's classifier could not fail on it — an AH upgrade that moved `Assets.Account` would leave the banner reading `full` while the deposit leg broke. **Ruling:** 02 gains **§7.7**, freezing the three AH surfaces the deposit leg touches (`Assets.Account(1337, who)`, `System.Account`, `PolkadotXcm.limited_reserve_transfer_assets`), fail-closed on an unprobed surface, with withdraw explicitly unaffected (it is a local call over §7.4 reads). **And it does not bump the contract version** — the first 02 revision that deliberately does not, under a new §13 **rule 8**: that constant is stamped into *Bleavit's* runtime, which cannot observe Asset Hub in either direction, so bumping it would publish coverage nothing behind it can attest — v24's own defect inverted. The foreign surface is pinned in the release artifact feed (AH genesis hash / `spec_version` / metadata hash) and carries its own separate 10 §5.2 verdict. F18 and F4's AH leg are unblocked; the two `[VERIFY]` tags (asset index, exact AH extrinsic) stay open and fail-closed per R-2
