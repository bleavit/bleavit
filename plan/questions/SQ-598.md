## Question

**The round-1 oracle report bond is not computable from any frozen surface, so a reporter cannot be told what their report will hold.** 11 §11.5's P-13 row requires the round bond *"recomputed and displayed"*, and 07 §6.1 gives the formula `B_1 = max(orc.bond_floor, ceil(orc.bond_bps × StakeAtRisk / 10,000))`. Both parameters are readable through `params()` (02 §7's parameter-consumer table). **`StakeAtRisk(c, m)` is not** — it is the sum of `CohortEscrow(k)` over every cohort whose frozen MetricSpec consumes component `c` for measurement epoch `m`, and 02 freezes no view, method or storage row carrying it. `open_oracle_rounds()` returns only rounds that already exist (and the round-1 report is what *creates* one); `RoundState.stake_at_risk` exists but §7.2 says in as many words that the FE reads the `OracleRoundView` projection and not that struct, and the projection omits it. Reassembling the sum from cohort membership plus per-vault escrow would be a client **computation** where 11 §11.4 rule 2 requires an exact chain read — the same distinction 02 §4 draws when it explains why `is_reserved_protocol_destination` is a method rather than published constants (SQ-588). Note the *challenge* side is fine: an existing round's `OracleRoundView.bond` is the chain's own frozen figure and is read

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07 (contract v29).** One method, not two: `bond_quote` publishes **the bond** — the amount the action holds — not the exposure it derives from, because a client recomputing `max(floor, ceil(bps × X / 10,000))` would own three normative details 07 §6.1 states separately, and R-7 rounds against a claimant who must post it. `exposure` and `read_at` ship as disclosure the client never recomputes from. `None` is first-class and blocks (07 §7's `ExposureUnavailable`, G-1)
