## Question

`CohortSummary.proposals` is bounded at 5 (`BoundedVec<(ProposalId, ProposalClass, DecisionOutcome), 5>` in `futarchy-primitives`), but `epoch.slots` (proposals per cohort) has a 13 §1 hard-max of 12. If `epoch.slots` is governance-raised above 5, a cohort's summary view cannot hold all its proposals. Is bound 5 an intentional display cap, or should it track `epoch.slots`' ceiling (I-20/I-21)? Pre-existing (not introduced by the M1 pass); surfaced by the M1-finish spec-reviewer.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C) — **already resolved by contract v4; the row was stale.** 02 §4 bounds `CohortSummary.proposals` at `ConstU32<12>` with the hard-max rationale, and the code single-homes `MAX_COHORT_PROPOSALS = 12`; the alleged `.take(5)` truncation no longer exists (`epoch-core` pushes every supplied proposal). One derived-arithmetic repair rode the batch: 13 §5 still carried the pre-v4 81 B `CohortSummary` and a 59,236 B total — corrected to the pinned **158 B** and **61,700 B** (≈60.3 KiB, still inside the 70 KiB budget).
