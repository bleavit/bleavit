## Question

**P-3 requires fee headroom on a split and P-1/P-2 require none on a trade, in the same asset.** P-3 reads *"USDC balance ≥ amount + fee headroom (in selected fee asset)"* and P-9 carries the per-entry deposit headroom; P-1's balance row is *"user USDC balance (buy) / position balance (sell)"* with no headroom clause at all, and P-2 inherits it. So a buy drafted at exactly the account's whole USDC balance passes every §11.5 row and then fails at dispatch for want of the §11.3 transaction fee — whenever USDC is the selected fee asset, which is the case the fee selector exists for. The client implements the rows as written (`spendable` against `cost + fee`, no headroom), because inventing a headroom is a precondition row the document does not carry. **Ruling needed:** do P-1/P-2 gain P-3's headroom clause, or does the §11.3 fee selector own that check for every call — in which case P-3's clause is the redundant one?
