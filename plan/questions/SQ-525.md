## Question

**Five permanent protocol accounts are outside `ProtocolAccounts`, so 03 §5.4's `transfer` refusal does not cover them.** Raised by the round-5 spec-reviewer while auditing the §3 restatement. The treasury `KEEPER`, `ORACLE`, `REWARDS` and `COLLATOR` sub-accounts and the guardian sovereign are **not** members (`configs.rs` `ProtocolAccounts::contains` returns false), yet 03 §7 R-4 lists the first three among the genesis-endowed permanent protocol accounts. §5.4 requires Signed `transfer` to refuse every `ProtocolAccount` destination, so today a user can push conditional positions into an account **no origin can redeem from**. **Severity is bounded and the bound is why this is a question rather than a defect:** escrow still backs those positions, conservation and I-4 are untouched, the loss falls on the sender alone, and the vault reaps at the archive delay — it is a footgun, not a solvency hole. It is also **pre-existing**, not introduced by Track E. Widening the set is an audit-scope-A behaviour change and does not belong in a revenue-instrument PR, so 03 §3 now records the exclusion and its consequence explicitly instead of leaving it invisible

## Status

**open** — needs a ruling on whether these are intended members. If yes, the E2 precedent applies: widen `ProtocolAccounts` **only**, not `is_canonical_protocol_account`, so the 09 §5.2 inflow-cap exemption is not widened as a side effect
