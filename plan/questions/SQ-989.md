## Question

**How does a provider-origin chart row come into existence?** SQ-983 resolved 2026-08-07 by having the client derive chart rows locally from a provider's `ops` — and that is **not implementable as the format stands**, which the R-6 review of the ruling caught. `SnapshotOp` is 03 §5's complete-set movement alphabet (`split`, `merge`, `transfer`, `redeem`) and `parseSnapshot` refuses anything else; none of the four carries a price, a quote or an observation. So 10 §7's chart-row `origin` keeps two values nothing can reach, and SQ-983 moved the contradiction rather than removing it. Two ways out: §8.2's document gains an **observed trade** movement — an observed fact, so §8.5.2's argument against carrying aggregates does not reach it, and §9.1 already derives candles from a `Traded` stream — or 10 §7 drops `snapshot` and `indexer` from the chart-row origin and says provider history is movements only.
