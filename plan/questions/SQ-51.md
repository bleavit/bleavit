## Question

08 §1.3's per-proposal outflow cap (`trs.cap_proposal` = 5% NAV) is enforced per-`spend`-call in A9; a proposal batching many sub-threshold spends can exceed 5% within one proposal (still bounded by the 10%/30-day rolling meter). Is per-proposal aggregation the treasury's job, or enforced upstream against the proposal `ask` at qualification/queue?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** The guarantee is **not** orphaned — the aggregate cap binds before decide, twice: `static_check` at qualification and `queue_time_check` at queue re-run it (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:3208-3213`, `:3236-3248`), with `derived_treasury_ask` walking every leaf of every call in the batch and failing closed on any non-statically-sizable leaf (`:2864-2908`). 08 §1.3 gained a normative *Enforcement layering* bullet naming the decide-time aggregate as the per-proposal guarantee and the per-call check as backstop. Recorded caveat: the aggregate gate is currently `Treasury`-class-guarded, so when SQ-173 lands real envelope valuation, CODE/META must be brought under the same check or the guarantee becomes orphaned for them.
