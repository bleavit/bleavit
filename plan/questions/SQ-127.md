## Question

PB-MIGRATION checkpoint lifetime: 09 §3.2(2) makes `pre_upgrade_checkpoint` the audit anchor *during* a halt — which begins after the upgrade applied, i.e. after 09 §2.1 has the guard clear `PendingUpgrade`; but the implemented guard invariant (A11 try-state) pairs `PendingUpgrade`/`PendingUpgradeCheckpoint` both-Some/both-None. Does the checkpoint survive the clear during a halted migration? The B7 pb-migration fixture injects the consistent paired form; the halted-migration-shaped image is undecidable until answered.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch X)** — **fixed in code** (with **SQ-144**, one shared patch). Authorization-time `block_hash`/`state_root` capture and `BatchDispatcher::checkpoint()` are gone; the guard now captures a dedicated **application-time** anchor — `PendingAnchorCapture` plus `PreMigrationAnchor<(BlockNumber, H256)>` recording `(now − 1, parent_hash)` — and only when an MBM cursor actually exists (09 §3.2(2)). Anchors clear on migration completion and on successful recovery application; abort and zero-MBM application clear only the latch. The frozen 02 v6 queue shape is preserved by retaining the checkpoint field in place, always `None`. Test: `sq_127_application_anchor_replaces_recovery_anchor_and_survives_until_completion`.
