## Question

Paging on migration halt: 09 §3.2 says PB-MIGRATION activation "pages operators immediately" via RB-UPGRADE, but 12 §6.3 marks only RB-LEDGER and RB-RELEASE "(page immediately)" — the Upgrades row is unmarked, so O4's §6.3-derived frontmatter carries `page_immediately: false` for RB-UPGRADE. Which doc governs paging for a migration halt; should 12 §6.3's Upgrades row carry the marker?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B5 ratification; 09 governs, 12 amended to agree).** The apparent contradiction dissolves once the two events are distinguished — 09 §3.2(4) attaches paging to PB-MIGRATION *activation*, while 12 §6.3's row alerts on the machine *trigger* — but the ruling goes the safety-forward way rather than exploiting the gap: paging begins at the **trigger**, because a stall satisfying it already holds ordinary transactions under the multi-block-migration lockdown, so waiting for a guardian 5-of-7 to raise the page spends the outage on procedure. 12 §6.3's Upgrades row now reads `RB-UPGRADE (PB-MIGRATION, page immediately)` and 09 §3.2(4) states the trigger-not-activation rule, making the two documents one obligation. Wired end-to-end: `check-runbooks.py` taught comma-separated annotations (+3 tests), rule → `severity: page` (routes to the existing paging receiver unchanged), RB-UPGRADE frontmatter/index/escalation synced, and the page-domain test now **derives** its expected set from 12 §6.3 instead of a pinned literal.
