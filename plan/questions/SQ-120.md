## Question

08 §6.3 "100% ⇒ `KeeperBudgetExhausted`… rebates stop": a literal 100% trigger never fires unless spend lands exactly on the budget (budget rarely an exact multiple of `keeper.rebate`). B9 implements **effective exhaustion** — the first unpayable attempt emits the event (degenerately `{spent: 0}` when `rebate > budget` on a fresh meter, e.g. after a governance shrink) — **plus a payment latch**: once emitted, rebates stay off for the rest of the epoch even if a mid-epoch `keeper.rebate` reduction would fit again. Ratify both readings.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** Both readings ratified in a new 08 §6.3 bullet. Effective exhaustion (first non-fitting rebate attempt emits, reporting actually-spent — degenerately 0) and the per-epoch **payment latch** (no further metered rebate after it fires, even if a later amendment would create headroom) are both normative, with `KeeperBudgetLow` always preceding. Verified not a liveness trap: the rebate is an infallible post-effect that never alters its crank, every crank stays permissionless and dispatchable at ordinary fee, and the shipped keeper daemon has no meter awareness at all. The latch's only real effect is that a mid-epoch budget *increase* cannot reopen payments — the no-extra-outflow direction.
