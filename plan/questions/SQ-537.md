## Question

**No alert covers collator-set attrition, so the reactive half of SQ-536's safety argument has no detector.** SQ-536 seeds `collator.comp_epoch` at its registry floor and bounds the unsafe (under-paying) direction partly on *fast recovery* — ×2 per PARAM amendment on a 1-epoch cooldown. That path is only as good as the signal that triggers it, and there is none: `deploy/monitoring/prometheus/prometheus.yml` scrapes a `bleavit-collators` job, but **no row of 12 §6.3's alert tables covers a fall in the registered/authoring collator set**, and the on-chain accumulator that would reveal it (`CollatorAuthoredBlocks`, bounded at 120) feeds no exporter series. So collators leaving because the rate is below their cost would surface as a liveness incident rather than as an economics alarm — after the fact. Needs a §6.3 alert row bound to an RB-* runbook plus a `TelemetryApi` series for the authoring set, in the shape O5 already uses; the coverage gate (`tools/monitoring/check_alert_coverage.py`) would then hold it.
