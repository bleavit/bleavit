## Question

**Does 10 §4.4's `fut-ingest` single-writer discipline reach §9.2's retention pass, or only ingestion?** §4.4 and §6.5 both scope the lock to *ingest writes*: *"the ingest writer lock (`fut-ingest`) is held by the leader only"*. §9.2's retention pass is a different writer with the same hazard and a worse failure — it **deletes** rows and rewrites the `downsampled` label set wholesale, so two tabs applying it together let the later one persist an accumulator that erases a label whose rows the earlier already deleted. That is §9.2 obligation 1's *"never a silent splice"*, produced by a user having the app open twice rather than by any error. F14 wired the pass under `fut-ingest` on the conservative reading, so a tab that cannot take the lock defers and writes nothing; the alternatives are a lock of its own (two writers of one `meta` row, which is the problem) or an optimistic re-read inside each fold's transaction (which §9.2 obligation 1 would then have to state). §4.4 should say which, because a client that reads it literally today writes unlocked and is not wrong to.
