## Question

05 §2.1 T5/T6 do not define equal-bond tie-breaking or the exact rollover-exhaustion representation. A8 ranks qualification bond-descending then `pid`-ascending; a no-slot / lock-conflict rolls once then refunds.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B1 ratification, delegated ruling).** 05 §2.1 gained a normative *T5/T6 ordering and rollover exhaustion* note: rank is **bond-descending, then `pid`-ascending** (the tie-break is deterministic submission order, so equal bonds never depend on iteration order), and a proposal rolls over **exactly once** before cancelling with a full refund. The ruling additionally pins what the row did not ask but the code does: the single rollover allowance is **per proposal, not per cause** — a proposal deferred once by slot contention and then demoted by the 08 §4.4 POL-budget shrink has already spent it.
