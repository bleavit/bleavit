## Question

**What should `baseline_market(epoch)` and the frontend render after a Baseline book is reaped?** The earlier contract-v6 ruling allowed `BaselineMarketOf` to outlive its book, creating a retained id with no `MarketState`. Contract v7 supersedes that state: mapping and matching Baseline book co-exist and reap removes both atomically; `try-state` checks both directions and reap fails static on a mismatched inverse.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-22 (superseding the 2026-07-21 batch-D ruling)** — when cohort history identifies an epoch but `BaselineMarketOf` is absent, the frontend labels the book reaped/archived, renders no zero quote and disables trading. A present mapping with an absent/mismatched book is corrupt chain state and triggers the compatibility hard block. Contract-v7 joint sign-off is recorded in 02 §13.
