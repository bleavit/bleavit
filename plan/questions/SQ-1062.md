## Question

The monitor permits `ws://`, pins no Bleavit genesis, reads a finalized head and storage with plain
JSON-RPC and no proof, and uses one configured endpoint until it throws. A well-formed malicious RPC
can replay or fabricate the manifest hash, generation, revocation mask, spec version and `SECURITY`
flag without triggering failover. Bundle signatures still constrain arbitrary replacement, but the
monitor cannot independently establish the on-chain security state it claims to cross-check.
