## Question

**Oracle bond base-unit product still floors after the SQ-260 ruling.** 07 §6.1 now specifies `B_1 = max(orc.bond_floor, ceil(orc.bond_bps × StakeAtRisk / 10,000))` with the division rounding up; `crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs:1273-1277` still truncates (`/ 10_000` on `u128`). One-line ceiling-division fix plus a pinning test. Magnitude is ≤ 1 base unit (≈1e-10 relative at the default floor) and §6.3's admission rule is evaluated in bps space, so no invariant is at risk — but the direction is now normative.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B3) — fixed in the same session rather than carried. `crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs` `round_bond` now applies `.div_ceil(10_000)` to the base-unit product, carrying the 07 §6.1 rationale in-comment, and `round_bond_base_unit_product_rounds_up` pins the direction (1 bps of 15,000 → 2 not 1; exact products unaffected; the doubling ladder derives from the already-rounded `B_1`; rounding applied before the floor `max`, never after). oracle-core 18/18 · pallet-oracle 96/96 · fmt + clippy clean.
