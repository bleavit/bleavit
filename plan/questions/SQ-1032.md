## Question

**12 §5.2 point 2 and §1.4 gate 2 count attestations differently, and the out-of-band monitor implements the looser one.** §1.4 gate 2 requires *"≥ 2 independent attestations: builders in different organizations/infrastructure"*. §5.2 point 2 says only *"≥ 2 attestations against the current keyring generation"*. `tools/monitoring/attestation_monitor.py` counts **distinct keys**, and its `keyring.example.toml` carries `role`, `revocation_index` and `public_key` with **no organization field** — so it structurally cannot count by organization, while `verify-release` takes the organizational reading. Two implementations of one floor, disagreeing.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-08 (R-1) — §5.2 inherits gate 2's reading; it is a restatement in the monitoring context, not a second looser rule.** Independence is the entire claim a reproduction makes: two attestations from one organization is **one** reproduction, and a floor that counted them as two would certify a property nobody established. The looser reading also cannot be the intended one, because §5.2's job is to detect a compromised release **out of band** — the case where one organization is the adversary. So `verify-release`'s organizational count is correct and the monitor's is the defect. Two consequences, both recorded rather than assumed: 12 §5.2 point 2 is amended to cite gate 2's independence requirement explicitly rather than restating half of it, and the monitor's keyring format needs an `organization` field before its `attestations_valid` series means what §1.4 means. The monitor change is **not** made here — its keyring is an example holding placeholders, and changing a published format is O5's to sequence — so this row closes the ambiguity and leaves that work named.
