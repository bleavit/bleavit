## Question

**12 §6.3's three mandatory reserve-probe operator settings name no owning role.** *"The margin is a mandatory positive operator setting in DOT plancks, supplied to the exporter rather than invented as a protocol parameter"*, likewise the Asset Hub stale-head window and the pinned genesis hash. §6.1 splits the two candidates: the Monitoring coordinator runs the exporter (Monitoring & alerting row) and the Oracle operations coordinator owns the reserve line the settings measure (Reserve-health probe row). `deploy/ops-handbook/README.md` records the split as a handbook choice rather than a spec reading, and names both. A wrong margin is a monitoring-only error, so this is a values judgement rather than a solvency one
