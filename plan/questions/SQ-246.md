## Question

**`ops.coretime` USDC budget line vs DOT-denominated renewal route.** The budget line (08 §1.1) is USDC-denominated while the 09 §4 funding leg moves DOT via the relay-teleport route; no spec text defines the conversion (rate source, rounding direction, fee budget, the renewal account on the Coretime chain, weight caps for the two-legged program). `execute_coretime_renewal` debits the line by the quote price — in which asset? B10 left `RenewalDispatch` fail-closed rather than pick a conversion.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-18 (B12)** — quote price is DOT planck; line debit = USDC value of price + `ops.coretime_fee_dot` at `ops.coretime_dot_rate`, rounded up (Decision log)
