## Question

**08 §5.3's P-scaled `pol.b`/δ seeding has no runtime surface (pre-existing; made load-bearing by SQ-231).** The runtime seeds POL from the flat `pol.b.*` Params and binds flat `dec.delta.*` — the piecewise `pol.b(class,P) = b_floor·max(1, P/P_ref)` and δ slopes exist only in the spec/reference corpus (the treasury `scaled_defaults` vector row's prize-scaled fields are deliberately unasserted; found by the G0 criterion-A suite work). Consequence under the new gate-bearing ceiling: for honest proposals with `2P > sec.flow_cap·(b_acc+b_rej)` at floor depth, step 9 rejects (`SecuritySizing`) where the §5.3 non-bindingness argument assumes scaled seeding — conservative/status-quo direction (no unbacked claim possible), but functionally caps the passable prize until the seeding scaling is implemented. Which B-track milestone owns the §5.3 seeding implementation?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (A13, with SQ-300)** — the row's premise was already half stale: `scaled_pol_floor` / `scaled_decision_delta` / `proposal_p_ref` ship and TREASURY has used prize-scaled seeding and δ since the security-terms slice. What kept the scaling inert for PARAM/CODE/META was the absent prize source, not absent machinery — SQ-173 supplies it, so the §5.3 schedule is now live for every binding class.
