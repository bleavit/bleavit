## Question

**§11.5's P-7 row and its own rule 2 disagree about a pair call's fee, and the row is the one a reader reaches first.** P-7 says the pair path displays *"the `net = a − fee(a)` actually received"*. Rule 2 of the redemption-fee block says the opposite in as many words — *"Pair calls use `fee_pair` (§5.3a(2a)), not `fee(a)`"* — and gives the arithmetic: the sum of the fees the legs would pay, each leg applying its own waiver, with a worked example where `fee(a)` displays 60 against 43 charged. The client implements `fee_pair` (`redemptionAmountsPair`), because §5.3a(2a) is the owning section and rule 2 is the later text; the exposure is a reader who implements P-7 as written and overstates the fee on every pair redemption. **Ruling needed:** is P-7's `fee(a)` stale text to be corrected to `fee_pair`, or does the pair row mean something rule 2 does not?

## Status

**open — raised by the R-6 review of F7b.** No unsafe direction while it stands: the client already follows the owning section, so a correction to P-7 changes documentation and not code. Filed to **B7**
