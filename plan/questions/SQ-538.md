## Question

**`ops.coretime` is excluded from 08 §10.1 as unsizeable, but its renewal price cap compounds to +47 %/yr and no runway figure accounts for it.** §10.1 omits the line because the `broker.renew` price is an off-chain quote *and* "the renewal period is not stated anywhere in this document set". The second half is resolvable: the period is a property of Polkadot, not a Bleavit choice — bulk coretime is sold in **28-day** periods, so a year carries **13.0446** renewals, not 12. Resolving it shows the level was never the interesting quantity. Bulk renewals are price-capped, which reads as protection and per period is; compounded 13.04 times a year Kusama's configured 3 %/period is **+47.0 %/yr** (2 % → +29.5 %, 1 % → +13.9 %). A 4,000 USDC/yr line reaches **189,073/yr in ten years**, larger than the entire 109,281 cost base, and cuts the zero-revenue runway to the binding arming floor from **34.3 to 14.3 years** (10.1 at a 25,000 start). **Not an ungoverned drain** — `execute_coretime_renewal` debits `BudgetLine::OpsCoretime` and `fund_budget_line` needs the `FutarchyTreasury` origin, so escalation already requires a governed refill; a quote-side ratchet was designed and then **dropped as redundant**. What remains is that every 08 §10.5 runway holds `C` constant and is therefore not conservative w.r.t. this line

## Status

**closed 2026-07-31 — and the model it shipped was WRONG; see SQ-541.** The finding that the line is unmodelled stands and is now discharged: 08 §10.1 states it. The *arithmetic* filed here does not. It treated the renewal price as compounding at the per-period bump without bound and published 14.3 years on that basis; `pallet-broker::do_renew` clamps the price to the current sale price, so it saturates, and the runway under the same policy is 14.7 years, not 14.3, for a different reason than the one given. The 47 %/yr figure survives only as the ratchet's *local* rate below the ceiling. Superseded by SQ-541, which carries the verified rule and the policy that follows from it
