## Question

**The 06 §3.2 authority matrix has no rows for the operational pallets the runtime necessarily carries** (`XcmpQueue`, `MessageQueue`, `CollatorSelection`, `Session`, `Preimage`, `Migrations`, `Vesting`) — S5's 242-row inventory pinned the runtime's de-facto classifications (Public leaf with in-pallet origin gates, or nobody). Should 06 §3.2 gain rows so these classifications are spec-owned rather than implementation-owned? (Pre-existing since B1a; surfaced by the S5 full pinning.)

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — ruled: **ratify the effective origins**. 06 §3.2 gains a nine-row operational-pallet table that explicitly separates the **base-filter disposition** from the **pallet's own origin gate** — the conflation was the actual defect, since three rows gate on Root or `ConstitutionalValues`, not Signed. Denying all administration post-genesis was rejected (session-key rotation and collator self-service have no governance content).
