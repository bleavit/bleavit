## Question

08 §1.2 (and 02 §1.2 prose) describe `nav()` as `{ nav, spendable_nav, reserve_impaired, meter_utilization }`, but the frozen 02 §4 `NavView` struct is a 10-field per-account decomposition (`total`/`main`/…/`obligations`/`haircut_flag`) with **no** `spendable_nav` and **no** `meter_utilization`. A9's internal `NavComponents` captures the 08 §1.2 set correctly, but the B2 `FutarchyApi::nav()` has nowhere to place `spendable_nav`/`meter_utilization` that FE-15 needs.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C) — **already resolved by contract v4; the row was stale.** 02 §4's `NavView` carries `spendable_nav` and `meter_utilization_bps` (13 fields), `futarchy-primitives` pins the layout, and `views.rs` maps both from `NavComponents`. 08 §1.2 already says `nav()` is the committed 02 §4 view.
