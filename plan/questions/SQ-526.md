## Question

**`mkt.obs_interval`'s registry maximum equals the staleness gap threshold, so the top of its own admissible range guarantees staleness events — and the coupling is not screened.** Found while deriving E5's keeper-cost lever. 13 §1 gives `mkt.obs_interval` a max of **50 blocks**; 04 §7 increments `stale_events` on any observation gap **strictly greater than 50 blocks**, and that 50 is `futarchy_primitives::MKT_STALE_GAP_BLOCKS` — a **kernel constant**, movable only by CODE, not a registry key. At `mkt.obs_interval = 50` every on-grid observation therefore sits exactly on the staleness boundary and a single missed crank trips it, with real consequences: first `stale_event` forces a 3-day extension, second forces reject. The top of the row's own range is effectively unreachable. Unlike `gate.v_min` ↔ `dec.v_min` and `ledger.redeem_fee` ↔ `mkt.fee`, this pair is **not** in 13 rule 7's screened set and nothing refuses it at the amendment boundary. Three candidate dispositions, in increasing cost: lower the row's max to a value carrying a stated margin over the kernel constant (a 13 §1 metadata change, and the max is ordinary META-amendable); add the pair to rule 7's amendment screen; or leave it and document the reachable band, on the argument that PARAM class plus max-Δ +5 makes the walk to 50 visible. **The error direction is safe** — a chain that amends into it gets extensions and rejects, i.e. status-quo defaults, never a wrong decision — which is why this is a spec question and not a defect. E5's own lever targets 20, leaving a 2.5× margin, so E5 is **not** blocked on this
