## Question

**Two `CoverageRange` types exist with different fields, and only one of them is 10 §6.3's.** §6.3 publishes `CoverageRange` with `fromBlock`, `toBlock`, `origin`, `providerId?`, `ingestedAt` and a `RangeEdge`; `@bleavit/shared-types` carries the span and the provenance only, because `packages/ui` may not import `local-index` (10 §10.1) and a badge needs nothing more. The narrowing is correct and unstated: nothing in either document says the render-layer shape is a projection of §6.3's, so a field renamed on one side is found by whichever consumer reads it next — and for `origin` that is a badge silently unable to say who supplied a line, which is the half INV-FE-15 requires *"to the pixel"*. F8 adds a compile-time projection (`asSharedCoverageRange`) so the build fails on a divergence. **Ruling needed:** whether §6.3 should publish the narrowed shape as the render contract, or whether the projection is an implementation detail the spec need not name

## Status

**open — raised by the R-6 re-review of F8.** No unsafe direction: the projection is checked by the compiler. Filed to **B7**
