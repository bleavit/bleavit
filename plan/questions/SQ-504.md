The same shape reaches `:931` through the recovery-reset and valid-applied clears. **Consequence is the status-quo-violating direction (R-7, G-1):** execution resumes while the condition the latch exists to hold is unrepaired, and the 09 §3.2(4) `MigrationHalted` diagnostic is never emitted for either site because that too hangs off the first-activation edge of the bitmap. **No test covers it, and the existing one encodes the bug**: `pallets/execution-guard/src/tests.rs:2113-2114` calls `GuardPallet::migration_completed()` and then `MigrationHalt::<Test>::put(false)` on the very next line, mirroring production rather than asserting survival. **Fix, and it subsumes a second open item:** give the guard's fail-static latches a source bit of their own, signalled through a `Config` seam, so they participate in the bitmap instead of racing it — which simultaneously makes them visible to the `MigrationHaltSources` activation-edge recorder that A14's `Π` instrumentation needs (the two `MigrationHalt::put(true)` sites are the highest-value entries on `Π`'s uninstrumented list for exactly this reason). The weaker alternative — OR semantics in `sync_execution_migration_halt` plus an explicit guard-side clear — leaves the diagnostic gap open. Genesis site `:1233` is out of scope: a genesis-staged halt is a spec'd drill affordance, though it inherits the same erasure and should adopt whichever mechanism lands.

## Question

**The execution guard's own fail-static migration halts cannot stick — the runtime erases them from a bitmap they never wrote, one statement later.** `pallet-execution-guard` latches `MigrationHalt::<T>::put(true)` directly at two production sites: `lib.rs:931`, whose own comment says "Keep retrying under an execution halt until operators repair the pin state" when the recovery-image pin is corrupt or missing, and `lib.rs:1656` in `migration_completed()`, when `release_recovery_image()` fails to unpin. The runtime, meanwhile, owns a source bitmap: `set_migration_halt_source` ORs a bit into `MigrationHaltSources` and `clear_migration_halt_sources` clears bits, and **both** end in `sync_execution_migration_halt(sources)`, which does an unconditional `MigrationHalt::put(sources & EXECUTION_HALT_SOURCES != 0)` (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:839-841`). The guard's direct writes never set a bit, so the bitmap reads 0 for them and any subsequent sync rewrites the flag to **false**. **In one case the erasure is immediate and unconditional.** The runtime's `MigrationStatusHandler::completed()` is literally `MigrationFailedStep::kill(); ExecutionGuard::migration_completed(); clear_migration_halt_sources(MIGRATION_FAILURE_HALT | MIGRATION_STALL_HALT);` — so the halt set inside `migration_completed()` is cleared by the next statement of the same function whenever no `APPLIED_DETECTION_HALT` bit happens to be set, which is the ordinary completion path. The latch is not weak, it is inert. The same shape reaches `:931` through the recovery-reset and valid-applied clears. **Consequence is the status-quo-violating direction (R-7, G-1):** execution resumes while the condition the latch exists to hold is unrepaired, and the 09 §3.2(4) `MigrationHalted` diagnostic is never emitted for either site because that too hangs off the first-activation edge of the bitmap. **No test covers it, and the existing one encodes the bug**: `pallets/execution-guard/src/tests.rs:2113-2114` calls `GuardPallet::migration_completed()` and then `MigrationHalt::<Test>::put(false)` on the very next line, mirroring production rather than asserting survival. **Fix, and it subsumes a second open item:** give the guard's fail-static latches a source bit of their own, signalled through a `Config` seam, so they participate in the bitmap instead of racing it — which simultaneously makes them visible to the `MigrationHaltSources` activation-edge recorder that A14's `Π` instrumentation needs (the two `MigrationHalt::put(true)` sites are the highest-value entries on `Π`'s uninstrumented list for exactly this reason). The weaker alternative — OR semantics in `sync_execution_migration_halt` plus an explicit guard-side clear — leaves the diagnostic gap open. Genesis site `:1233` is out of scope: a genesis-staged halt is a spec'd drill affordance, though it inherits the same erasure and should adopt whichever mechanism lands.
