## Question

**Coretime renewal price is not a quote the protocol receives — it is set by WHEN the renewal is submitted, and the timing is worth +18.3 years of runway.** Raised by checking SQ-538's own arithmetic against source rather than against recollection. `pallet-broker` 0.28.0 (the version this workspace's `Cargo.lock` pins) `do_renew` charges `min(leadin_factor(t) · end_price, max(prev · (1 + renewal_bump), end_price))`, with `leadin_factor` decaying **100 → 1** across the sale's leadin. Three consequences SQ-538 missed: the ratchet is **bounded** (the `min` clamps to the open market, so it saturates at `leadin_factor(t) · end_price`, not without limit); the price **tracks the market down** as well as up; and the saturation multiple is chosen by submission timing. Renewing in the **interlude** — the obvious moment, since only renewals may consume cores there — saturates at **100× the market floor** (156 renewals ≈ 12.0 yr at 3 %/period). Renewing at the **end of the leadin** pays `end_price` exactly in every period, and *un-ratchets*: one late renewal drops a saturated price back to the floor. **Worth, against §10.1's base at a 4,000 USDC/yr floor: 14.7 → 33.1 years of zero-revenue runway and 6,252,022 → 100,000 USDC of cumulative 25-year spend (62×)** — the difference between missing and meeting the 25-year horizon, from a scheduling choice, with no parameter changed. The cost is liveness risk (`ensure_cores_for_sale` can return `SoldOut` after the interlude), which is bounded and observable from the same sale state the quote is already read from

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-31 (E7).** Verified (V-62), modelled (`sustainability.py` `CoretimeRenewalPriceTests`, 7 tests, leadin curve asserted against the pallet's own `leadin_price_bound_check`), and landed normatively: 08 §10.1 carries the derivation and retracts its "the one line that cannot be reduced" claim; 09 §4 states the operating rule (**as late as the observed remaining-core margin allows, never later** — a missed renewal is a lost core and outranks any price argument); RB-TREASURY carries the procedure. Deliberately **no on-chain enforcement**: the call stays timing-agnostic, and a sustained escalation surfaces as an `ops.coretime` refill request under 08 §1.1, which is the governance-visible backstop. `renewal_bump`/`interlude_length`/`leadin_length` are relay-governance `ConfigRecord` values and stay `[VERIFY]`; every figure is bracketed against Kusama's published 3 %
