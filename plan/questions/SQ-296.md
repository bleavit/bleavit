## Question

**Registry→oracle escalation and its §6 value-scaled re-bond are entirely unimplemented — and the underlying economics may not hold.** 07 §7 requires that "a registry dispute that escalates MUST carry §6 value-scaled bonds from that point, sized by the `StakeAtRisk` of the cohorts consuming `I`". No seam exists at all: `pallets/registry/Cargo.toml` carries no oracle dependency, no escalation code exists anywhere (`grep escalat` hits only oracle paths), and `required_bond()` is a flat two-way match on `reg.bond_incident`/`reg.bond_milestone` (`crates/registry-core/src/lib.rs:643-648`). Beyond the missing handoff: an **unchallenged** false S1 filing never escalates, is refunded its flat 5,000 USDC bond on `Upheld`, and drives `I` to 0 via `max(0, 1 − Σ severity)`, moving settlement-time `W` for every cohort consuming `I` that epoch — structurally the "flat bonds made high-value cohorts cheap to attack" hole §6 exists to close, while §2(5)'s bond-coverage admission rule is computed in `orc.bond_bps` terms that `I`'s actual bonded objects never carry. Needs an economic ruling before an implementation.
