## Question

**Relay `UpgradeGoAhead::Abort` recovery semantics are unspecified.** Stable2603 Cumulus deletes `PendingValidationCode` on Abort **without any runtime callback** (verified: `cumulus-pallet-parachain-system-0.26.0` src/lib.rs:739), after frame-system has already consumed `AuthorizedUpgrade` — without handling, guard `PendingUpgrade` wedges every future upgrade (`PendingUpgradeExists`). **B6 ruling (G-1 status-quo default, needs ratification):** the runtime detects the abort deterministically (scheduling latch + installed-code-hash check in the validation-data hook), clears guard `PendingUpgrade`/checkpoint + the ReleaseChannel pending fields, emits append-only `UpgradeAborted{code_hash}`, raises a guardian-visible halt-source bit, and the upgrade is **re-proposable through the normal process** — no automatic re-authorization (the relay said no). 09 §2 should gain the Abort arm.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B2 · ratified).** 09 §2.1 gained arm **(7)**: deterministic abort detection in the validation-data hook (scheduling latch + installed-code-hash mismatch), clearing `PendingUpgrade` and the checkpoint, tolerant clearing of the `ReleaseChannel` pending fields, append-only `UpgradeAborted { code_hash }`, a guardian-visible incident bit that does **not** by itself freeze the queue, and re-proposal through the normal process with no automatic re-authorization. False-positive analysis came back clean (five conjunctive conditions; the Abort signal is re-read from the relay proof every block; re-fire impossible once `PendingUpgrade` is `None`). Two row corrections rode the ratification: the Cumulus citation was stale (the pin is **0.29.0**, `src/lib.rs:786-789`, not 0.26.0), and a *clean* abort raises the incident bit **without** halting — only a failed cleanup halts, which the row conflated. Adding `UpgradeAborted` to 02 §6 needs the contract bump and rides batch **C**.
