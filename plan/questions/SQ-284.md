## Question

**Raw `futarchyTreasury.state` (the rolling-meter `TreasuryState`) does not SCALE-round-trip under portable metadata (polkadot-js): a read returns ~9,358 stored bytes that re-encode to ~17,678 (first surfaced by drill 05's raw-state read).** The runtime round-trips its own `TreasuryState` (audit-scope-A; unit-tested encode/decode + try-state), and the 02 contract routes every consumer through `FutarchyApi` views, never raw treasury storage — so this is a portable-metadata/polkadot-js decode quirk on the rolling-meter type, not a runtime-correctness or solvency defect. Confirm the 02-views-only reading (raw-storage decodability is outside the contract surface), or investigate the specific field (likely a `RollingMeter` bucket vector) that trips the polkadot-js decoder for indexer/tooling friendliness. Drill 05's probe was changed to dispatch + assert `RenewalWindowClosed` only (no raw-state read).

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C, contract v6) — **the contract question is answered: the 02-views-only reading is confirmed.** 02 §7 now states that storage items it does not list are not contract surface and that raw decodability of unlisted items under portable metadata is not guaranteed; every treasury consumer binds `nav()`, and raw `futarchyTreasury.state` is not in §7. The decode observation itself is recorded as **unreproduced**: the repository holds no captured bytes, portable metadata, polkadot-js version or decoder trace to confirm the 9,358 → 17,678 figures or localize a `RollingMeter` field, so it is closed as a non-contract tooling observation rather than carried as a phantom defect. Honest residual, deliberately not minted as a new row: drill 05's `stage` and `execute` branches still read raw `State`, so if the quirk is real it can still surface there.
