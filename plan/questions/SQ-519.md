## Question

**The Baseline **buy**-side fee reaches `POL_BASELINE`, never `MAIN`.** Found independently by the round-2 adversarial review and the spec audit. `buy_baseline` takes `cost + fee` as one `total` and has **no `fees_account` parameter at all** — unlike `sell_branch`, which takes one — so the fee is minted into complete Baseline sets held by the *book*, indistinguishable from seed inventory; `withdraw_fees` then returns `Ok(0)` for `BookKind::Baseline` and `withdraw_book` sends the whole lot to the seed funder. Two consequences. (1) An entire market class's buy-fee stream cannot fund the recurring cost base, which is exactly what E2 exists to deliver — the value is not lost, but it becomes committed liquidity rather than spendable income. (2) `pol_returned > pol_seeded`, which quietly falsifies 08 §10's "realized cost = divergence loss" identity in the opposite direction. 04 §6.1's E1 sentence covers the fee *account* (decision/gate) and the Baseline *sell*-side retained plain USDC, and is silent on the buy side, while §6.1's headline states the rule with no book-class carve-out ("realized fee value routes **100 % to `MAIN`**"); 08 §10.2's "Baseline books … their fee is retained as plain USDC" is inaccurate for this leg. **Fix shape, if ruled to `MAIN`:** segregate at *trade* time into `fees_account` (before the buyer's leg is paid, so a book that cannot fund the trade without spending fee revenue fails rather than silently dipping into it), then realize at Sweep — protocol accounts are exempt from the §5.3a redemption fee (§5.3a: the deduction applies to a **non-protocol** claimant), so the pair redeems to `MAIN` uncharged. Sweep-time accounting cannot substitute: by settlement the book's inventory may no longer cover the accrued fee, which is precisely why decision and gate books segregate at trade time

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-29 — ruled and implemented.** Baseline `buy` now splits `cost + fee`, transfers both fee legs to `fees_account` **before** transferring cost to the book and paying the buyer, and rolls the entire trade back when the post-segregation book is thin. `withdraw_fees` requires a terminal Baseline vault and redeems `min(LONG, SHORT)` from the protocol fee account to `MAIN` through the atomic pair call, uncharged. The independently derived Python wrapper generated two new corpus rows replayed by `market-core`; the real pallet proves buy fee + retained sell fee reach `MAIN` while `pol_returned` equals the zero-P&L seed.
