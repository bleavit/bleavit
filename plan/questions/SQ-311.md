## Question

**T24 becomes unreachable at the review deadline even while the proposal is still `Suspended`.** 06 §5.4(2) bounds `uphold_veto` by the proposal still being `Suspended` (before T12/T13 opens the rerun), and 05 §2.1 names it as `VetoUpheldByReview`'s single producing site. But the failed-review path cancels **both** scheduled referenda (`pallets/guardian/src/lib.rs:1146-1153`), so once the ratification deadline passes the veto verdict is no longer reachable even while the proposal sits in the state §5.4(2) says admits it. An upheld veto is a *different verdict*, not a late ratification — the values layer agreeing with the delay — so cancelling it alongside the ratification referendum may be over-broad. Should the `uphold_veto` referendum survive the ratification deadline, bounded only by `Suspended` as §5.4(2) states?

Custody, T12 cleanup and terminal-path regressions are green.
