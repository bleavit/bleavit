## Question

**No authenticated production source for coretime renewal quotes.** 09 §4 mandates quote-keyed (`period_index`) renewal funding with ≤ 8 open quotes, and the treasury helpers (`note_coretime_renewal_quote`/`prune_coretime_quote`) are bound-tested — but no spec text names WHO supplies an authenticated `(period_index, price)` in production (a Coretime-chain XCM query/response leg? an ops-attested feed?), its freshness window, the supersession rule for re-quotes, or the stale-prune trigger. B10 wired every other treasury sync surface and deliberately did not invent this one; the registry entry is re-owned to B12.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-18 (B12)** — quote authority = ops multisig via stored, `FutarchyTreasury`-rotatable account; TTL/supersession/prune per 09 §4 (Decision log)
