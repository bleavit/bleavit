## Question

**Should the treasury pot-account derivations be contract-frozen?** B3 pins MAIN = `PalletId(*b"bl/trsry").into_account_truncating()`, community/incentives = sub-seeds `communty`/`incentiv`; the deploy template + validator hardcode the resulting ss58-7777 addresses. 08 §1.1 names the sub-accounts but pins no derivation; if the FE/operators/auditors must recompute these, 13 §3.5 (or 02 §8, with a contract bump) should freeze the derivation.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C) — the derivations are frozen in **08 §2.1, mirrored in 13 §3.5** — deliberately *not* 02 §8. `PalletId(*b"bl/trsry")`, `into_account_truncating()` / `into_sub_account_truncating(seed)` and the exact seeds `communty` / `incentiv` are now normative. **Two row claims corrected:** it cites 08 §1.1, which owns budget-line accounts, not these genesis allocation pots (§2.1 does); and the asserted frontend need is not established — 10 §5.4's `ChainIdentity` pins ss58/paraId/genesis/chain-spec hashes and decimals, and no FE workflow recomputes pot addresses. The demonstrated consumer is genesis/deployment tooling, so no contract surface was enlarged. **Written against post-B14 main:** main's B14 (`73b9f6d`) landed mid-batch and broadened the facts from the row's three VIT pots to the ten statically derived protocol accounts of `usdc_genesis_endowments()`, so 08 §2.1 freezes that set and its exclusion rationale rather than the row's stale list. B14's own commit message says "No 02 surface" — independent agreement with this ruling's direction.
