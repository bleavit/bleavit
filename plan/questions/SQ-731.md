## Question

**The registry filing bond is value-scaled off an exposure no surface publishes — SQ-598's defect, one pallet over.** 07 §7 prices a filing at `F(kind, m)`, floored on `reg.bond_*` and scaled by `orc.bond_bps × Exposure(kind, m)`, where `Exposure` sums `CohortEscrow(k)` over the consuming cohorts — the same quantity `StakeAtRisk` is built from, and 02 publishes no view of either. Contract v28 froze the registry's own storage (`Filings`, `ClosedAt`, `AckRecords`), which answers the challenge window, the occupancy bound **and** a *challenge* bond (`registry_core`'s `challenge_filing` posts `filing.bond`, the filing's own stored amount) — but not the price of a filing that does not exist yet. So S19's file control asks a user to commit an amount nobody can show them. Client posture: `UNREADABLE['O-8']` is `blocking` and O-9 needs no declaration at all, the same asymmetry P-13 has against P-14
