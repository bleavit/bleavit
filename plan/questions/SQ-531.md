## Question

**`keeper.rebate` was 353× too high, and it carried 79.3 % of the entire annual cost base.** 13 §1 seeded it at 0.09 USDC as "3× fee cost", from an 08 §6.2 fee basis of "≈ 0.03 USDC" that was explicitly **assumed** and tagged `[VERIFY fee basis at benchmark time]` (SQ-117). Every pallet is now benchmarked, so it is measurable — and priced from the committed generated weights at the SQ-528-restored multiplier, a sanctioned crank costs **0.000085 USDC**, not 0.03. The seed was ≈ **1,058×** the fee it claimed to be 3× of; for 0.03 to be right VIT would trade at ≈ 17.6 USDC against 08 §9's documented 0.05 placeholder. Both keeper lines — 908,408 USDC/yr — were scaled by that error

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-30 (E5)** — kernel basis 30,000 → **85 µUSDC** with the derivation recorded; seed 0.09 → 0.000255. The `[VERIFY]` tag **stays but narrows**: it now covers the launch price, not the multiple, since rebate and fee scale with the same rate. Cost base 1,145,562 → 239,728/yr
