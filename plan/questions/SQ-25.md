## Question

Where is the boundary of 13 rule 1's "every K constant compile-time in `futarchy-primitives`"? LMSR error bounds live in `futarchy-fixed`, 02 §7 puts storage-bound arguments with owning pallets, and does 02 §2's "all types live in futarchy-primitives" cover §5–§7 event field types such as `MarketKind` (currently defined nowhere)?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (user-delegated batch; see Decision log): boundary written into 13 rule 1 + 01 §5.2 — every K numeric incl. LMSR domain/error bounds single-homed in `futarchy-primitives::kernel` (`futarchy-fixed` imports, never re-declares); carve-outs: §4 storage-bound arguments pallet-owned, rowless internal constants; all 02-named types incl. `MarketKind` live in primitives
