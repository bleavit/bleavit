## Question

**02 §7.3 assigns no `PhaseFlags` bit named *trading enabled*, and §11.5 P-1 asks for one.** P-1's last two clauses are *"`Constitution.PhaseFlags` trading-enabled bit set; no PB-LEDGER-FREEZE active"* — two clauses, read as two facts. §7.3's bit table assigns eight bits and none of them carries that name; bit 5 is `ledger frozen (PB-LEDGER-FREEZE)`, §7.3 calls `PhaseFlags` *"the key the frontend binds trading enablement … to"*, and 06 §6.3 makes bit 5 one leg of a three-part applied-effect tuple written only by the PB-LEDGER-FREEZE path. `market-reads.ts` therefore derives **both** clauses from bit 5 and says so, since R-2 forbids guessing a bit assignment. A wrong reading is unsafe in both directions: a client that invented a ninth bit would read an unset reserved bit as *trading disabled* and refuse every trade, and one that read the wrong existing bit would offer trades during a freeze. **Ruling needed:** is trading enablement the complement of bit 5, or does §7.3 owe a distinct assignment — in which case P-1's two clauses are two reads and this client performs one?

## Status

**open — raised by the R-6 review of F7b.** Conservative reading shipped: both clauses fail closed on an unreadable or undecodable bitset. Filed to **B7**
