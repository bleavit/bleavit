## Question

08 §2.3 calls the issuance meter "Rolling 365-day" but also caps against "supply-at-window-start". A9 originally implemented a FIXED 365-day window (reset `minted` at the boundary), so a governance-gated `issue_vit` at day 364 + day 365 could mint ≈ 2 × `iss.inflation_cap` across one trailing span.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (code, PR #51 Codex round): the fixed window was replaced with a bucketed **trailing 365-day `RollingMeter<366>`** (same primitive as the 30d/180d outflow meters). The cap base is `vit_supply − trailing_minted` (supply at the window start), and the trailing sum is never reset at a seam, so two mints straddling the year boundary are summed and cannot together exceed `iss.inflation_cap` — the doubling is closed
