## Question

**A competing venue at the arming bound destroys most of Bleavit's own governance formation, and the security gate reads clean precisely because it does.** Measured by the N12 competing-venue leg over the committed 10⁴ corpus (`competing_venue.rungs`, whole-slate sample, ~300 proposals/class). At the **0.50** arming rung — 16 §8.4's own arming condition read under proportional-to-depth allocation — decision-grade formation falls **PARAM 0.374 → 0.228 (−39.1 %), TREASURY 0.443 → 0.175 (−60.6 %), CODE 0.450 → 0.131 (−70.9 %), META 0.481 → 0.076 (−84.1 %)**, and the engine's silent Baseline fallback fires for ~250 of ~300 proposals per class against ~70 in the control. At the **0.75** stress rung CODE and META reach 0.017 and 0.006 — governance formation is effectively extinguished. Meanwhile the *security* leg reads 0.000000 false-pass at every rung, **because a book that never becomes decision-grade cannot produce a wrong decision**: the diversion converts corruption risk into denial, which is exactly TH-72's stated harm and not a mitigation. **The question is not whether the measurement is right — it is what it implies for D-20.** The hosted service's own §8.4 arming condition, taken at face value and read through the neutral flow model, admits a chain state in which META governance formation collapses by 84 %. Three readings, and they are materially different products: **(a)** the arming condition is too loose and must be tightened well below depth parity (e.g. `Σ b_ext ≤ k · Σ pol.b(live)` with k ≪ 1), which makes the service small by construction; **(b)** `svc.max_live` is the intended lever and must be sized from this curve rather than from the §8.5 resource partition, which is a different derivation than 13 §1 currently states; **(c)** the loss is acceptable because external questions are net capital *importers* (§8.4's primary structural mitigation) and this leg models diversion **without** the compensating import, which would make it a worst case rather than a forecast. **(c) is the most likely and is NOT established**: the leg thins organic formation and models no offsetting inflow, so it measures the diversion channel in isolation. That is the honest scope of the evidence and also its limitation. Deciding between (a)/(b)/(c) needs an import term, and it gates arming rather than any current milestone

## Status

**open** — needs the capital-import counterpart before the curve can be read as a forecast rather than a worst case. Blocks Phase-4 arming, blocks no current milestone
