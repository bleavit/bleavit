## Question

**No canonical `RuntimeCall` → `[u8; 8]` resource-key mapping exists.** A8 cannot mechanically prove `Proposal.resources` equals the payload footprint (I-11). Every non-empty payload therefore fails static checks rather than trusting proposer bytes; PR #93's bot fix also rejects empty/class-less batches under 05 §1/T4. Consequently **no payload can qualify in production** until the exhaustive bounded mapping is defined and tested by the values/classifier owner.
