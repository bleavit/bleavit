## Question

**08 §6.2 claimed the keeper meter covered a load it did not cover.** The section derived the decision-critical load as `133,920 × 0.09` = **12,052.80** USDC/epoch and then set `keeper.budget_epoch` = **12,000**, asserting "the budget covers the full decision-critical load". It was 52.80/epoch short (0.44 %), and §6.3 makes exhaustion a **latch** — once `KeeperBudgetExhausted` fires no further metered rebate is paid for the remainder of that epoch — so on a worst-case (zero-organic-trade) epoch the meter exhausted before the decision-critical load completed, every epoch.
