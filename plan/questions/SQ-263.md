## Question

**No real custody backs oracle/watchtower/attestor registration stakes.** Registration snapshots the live `Params` stake magnitude into the record and slashes compute against the stored value, but no balance hold/reserve is taken — an account without funds can register, and an insufficient-balance registration-refusal test cannot be written honestly. Pre-existing Track-A gap surfaced by B10's SQ-159 rebinding (the A9 fungibles/custody follow-up family, cf. SQ-123). Where does stake custody land — holds on registration, or the A9 custody milestone?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (stale — verified against HEAD)** — real custody exists for every registration path the row names: reporter and watchtower stakes and round bonds transfer through the configured `OracleCustody` interface into the oracle sovereign account over `ForeignAssets`, with funded and underfunded registration both covered by runtime balance assertions; attestor holds, liabilities, releases and slashing are implemented under B19. The remaining oracle value work is the separate `StakeAtRisk` source (SQ-174) and the registry value scale (SQ-296), not custody.
