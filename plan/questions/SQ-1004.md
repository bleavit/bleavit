## Question

**11 §11.5's P-13 says *"round open"* and no reading of it matches `oracle.report`.** `oracle_core::report` refuses when a round for `(component, epoch, spec_version)` already **exists** (`AlreadyFinal`, `crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs:767-775`) — `report` opens round 1, and a counter-report is `challenge`. So *"a round exists and is open"* inverts the runtime, and *"the game has not settled"* is answered by `Oracle.ComponentValues` rather than by the `Oracle.Rounds` the client's clause cites. The client copy is deliberately conservative and names no state until this is decided.
