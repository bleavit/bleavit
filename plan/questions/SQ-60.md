## Question

Who owns the 07 §11 money deadline (`OracleSettleDeadline`, d20)? 07 §12 implies welfare's `settle_cohort` neutralizes by *ignoring* rounds unclosed past d20; 07 §13's try-state invariant *"no `Rounds` entry survives its epoch's `OracleSettleDeadline` in a money-bearing state"* implies the **oracle** must force-neutralize stale rounds while late verdicts settle bonds-only. Which side owns it, and what is the oracle's B1a seam?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (user-delegated): the **oracle** owns force-neutralization via `force_neutralize_expired(m, expected)` / shell `note_settle_deadline(m)`, driven by the epoch pallet at the schedule-derived deadline (B1a); a late verdict finds no round and settles bonds only (I-18). 07 §11 amended; `adjudicate` also terminal-gated (F10).
