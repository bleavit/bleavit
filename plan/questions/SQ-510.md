## Question

**E1 makes NAV charge the POL seed roughly three times over.** Found by the E1 implementation, not by review. Post-E1 a seed debits `lines[Pol]` by the **cash** — `(pol.b + 2·pol.b_gate)·ln 2`, one `split` funding a branch pair — *and* `LivePolCommitments` still charges the full 08 §3 commitment, which is **2× that cash** because the meter charges both branches. NAV therefore dips ~3× the cash per live proposal where it dipped 2× before. The commitment is 2× the maximum loss that is even possible, since escrow only ever holds the cash. The clean reading is that once the cash is debited the NAV **obligation** term is subsumed — the cash debit *is* the exposure — leaving the §3 commitment as what it is described as, a `pol.budget_epoch` slate-capacity meter. **Not changed, deliberately:** the current behaviour is strictly fail-static (NAV understated ⇒ tighter caps and floors), correcting it *raises* NAV above even the pre-E1 baseline, and 08 §4.1's frozen floors were derived assuming the commitment. Under R-7 the conservative reading stands until the floor arithmetic is re-derived against it
