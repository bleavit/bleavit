## Question

07 §4 mandates a "keeper-class fee rebate" for watchtower `ack_observed`; B9 pays it from the **oracle budget line** (`CrankClass::OracleLine`, consistent with SQ-116's routing) in **both** the oracle pallet and the registry instances — though 07 §7's registry call table does not restate the rebate for the registry's ack. Confirm the routing and the registry-instance applicability.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B3): **ratified, and the gap was wider than the row stated** — 07 §7's call table carried **no** registry `ack_observed` row at all, and §4's rebate sentence is written against the oracle signature `ack_observed(component, epoch, round, report_hash)` while the registry's call is `ack_observed(epoch, filing_id)`, so "§7 does not restate the rebate" understated it. §7 now carries the call row plus a normative *Watchtower acknowledgment and its rebate* note: same keeper-class rebate, paid from the oracle budget line, with the differing exposures ratified as deliberate — the registry stops paying at `wt.quorum` acks per filing, an oracle round rebates one ack per registered watchtower up to `wt.max`. Both dedup per acknowledger and degrade fail-soft (ack succeeds, rebate simply unpaid) when the line cannot cover. Verified: `pallets/registry/src/lib.rs:552-580`, `pallets/oracle/src/lib.rs:664-684` (both `CrankClass::OracleLine`), `crates/futarchy-treasury-core/src/lib.rs:575-589` (fail-soft). §7's error list gained `NotRegistered`/`DuplicateAck`/`AlreadyQuorum`.
