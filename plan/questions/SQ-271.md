## Question

**Held-exposure self-certification of the depth certificate (mechanism round 2).** With SQ-231's contest-capital measure live, churn/wash can no longer inflate step-5 grading or step-9 `L̂` — yet the re-calibrated rates barely moved (PARAM 3.46 → 3.81 %, TREASURY 1.52 → 1.64 %): an attacker who *holds* net exposure through the window supplies genuine contest capital at the cost of market risk rather than 3P, so any aggregate depth measure the attacker can supply stays self-funding in thin organic environments. The sim's own evidence names the structural fix: CODE/META sit at 0.000 % **because gate books catch what the depth certificate cannot**. Candidate directions: (a) extend Survival/Security-style gate books to PARAM and TREASURY ≤ 1 % NAV (04 §1.1 inventory + POL cost impact); (b) an opposing-side/at-risk measure (likely the same aggregate self-funding wall); (c) accept-and-document (declined once). Blocks G0 criterion B and S4/V-12 publication.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-19 (user ruling; recorded 2026-07-20 in the triage sweep).** The at-risk depth measure (direction b/R1) was implemented and **reverted**: excluding balanced riskless inventory zeroed every false-pass but also broke honest formation (0 % adoption, 100 % false-reject), because the `v_min` floors and the `v_min = 2P` identity are calibrated on the gross measure — switching it would need a whole-scale depth re-calibration tied to the unmeasurable A-2 real-depth split. The user ruled **R2 (use gates)**; V-12 published with 0 profitable sub-3P exploits and per-class decidable-harm false-pass < 1 %, and G0 reached `phase0_exit: true`. Direction (a) — extending Survival/Security-style gate books to PARAM and TREASURY — remains a Phase-3 economics option, not a blocker.
