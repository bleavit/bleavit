## Question

**15 §4.3 PT-2(a)'s "net principal delta = −fees only" over-claims vs 04 §6.2 and D-1.** For a real wrapper-buy portfolio (`amount` bought leg + `cost` mirror bUSDC), the D-1 schedule pays `floor(amount/4) + floor(cost/2)` — equal to `cost` only at the neutral price 0.5. 04 §6.2's own text is the precise one: buyers recover the **neutral value**; the deviation is exactly the premium paid over the neutral prior. I-2(b)'s "recover par" phrasing shares the over-claim. Surfaced when PR #89 review forced PT-2(b) to drive the real 04 §6.1 buy accounting (the prior test injected the SHORT complement and hid it). The suite now asserts the 04 §6.2-exact recovery (`neutral value − cost − fees` net delta); 15 §4.3/I-2(b) wording needs a reconciling amendment.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — the batch's largest amendment. "Market buyers recover par under VOID" was **false** and had propagated to nine sites; corrected in 00 **D-1** and **D-3**, 01 G-3, 03 §5.5/§6.4/§11 PT-2, 04 §6.1/§6.2, 11 §11.6, 14 TH-10 and 15 I-2/§4.3 PT-2. A buyer's package recovers its D-1 **neutral value**; that equals `cost` only at the neutral prior 0.5, and net delta is `neutral recovery − cost − fees`. What D-1 guarantees is that no claim is valued *below* the schedule, not that a premium is refunded.
