## Question

**`EXECUTION_RETRY_WINDOW` is a dispatch-time deadline with no client-readable home, so a client cannot evaluate the `execute` window row for any mandate that has already failed.** 09 §1.2(1) (as corrected 2026-08-06) and 05 §2.1's T18/T23 put a `FailedExecuted` mandate on `failed_at + EXECUTION_RETRY_WINDOW` **instead of** `grace_end`, and `pallets/execution-guard/src/lib.rs:1956` implements exactly that. The stamp is readable — `ExecutionGuard.Queue` is frozen (02 §7.4) and its stored entry carries `failed_at`. **The window length is not readable by any means.** It is `kernel::EXECUTION_RETRY_WINDOW_BLOCKS`, exposed through no `#[pallet::constant_name]` and no `FutarchyApi` method, so it appears in neither 02 §9's frozen constant table nor anywhere else a client can reach; `ExecutionGraceFloor` and `ExecutionTimelockFloor` are published beside it and this one is not. Same shape as SQ-598: a §11.5 row whose formula is stated and whose one missing term has no chain surface. **The client half is closed fail-closed, not deferred**: a stamped `failed_at` with no window length yields a third window state (`deadline-unreadable`) that blocks and explicitly does **not** declare the mandate over — refusing a lawful retry is bad and ending a recovery window on the client's own authority is worse, and R-2 forbids guessing the value outright. Closing the surface half means publishing it, most simply as an `ExecutionGuard` metadata constant beside the two floors, which is a 02 §9 addition and therefore a contract bump with joint sign-off that must not ride inside a client fix. The **value** now has its 13 §2 home (43,200 blocks; recorded in the same pass, since 05 §2.1's *"72 h"* and the kernel constant were its only two statements and neither is a values table)
