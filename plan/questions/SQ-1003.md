## Question

**06 §3.2's authority matrix writes five guardian extrinsics that do not exist.** The rows name `guardian.{pause_intake, delay_once, force_rerun, suspend_on_gate}` and `guardian.activate_playbook(id)`, and `pallet-guardian` has **one** dispatchable over an enum — the same defect SQ-621 closed in 11 §11.8.2. It is filed rather than fixed because §3.2 splits the guardian into two rows by **origin** (`GuardianHold` against `EmergencyPlaybook`), and collapsing them to one call would erase that distinction. So the question is what that column enumerates — extrinsics or effects — and how a one-call-many-powers family should be written in it.
