## Question

What does a chainHead fixture "covering every event" (02 §11, 15 §5(4)) require on a fresh dev chain with no protocol activity? B8 records metadata presence per frozen 02 §6 variant plus real `System.Events` bytes at the pinned block on both transports; actually *emitting* every frozen event needs scripted scenarios that are impossible before A8/A11/B7. Pin the intended semantics (presence + system-event bytes now, scenario-driven emission once B7's suites exist?).

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B5 ratification; semantics fixed in 15 §5 — and the row's own reading corrected).** "Covering every event" is now defined per critical-surface entry as (i) metadata presence under the frozen name and (ii) rendered-layout agreement with 02 §11, plus **once per fixture** (iii) a real executed block's `System.Events` blob retrievable and non-empty over **both** the chainHead and legacy transports. Requirement (iii) is explicitly a single transport-and-liveness proof and **not** per-variant evidence — the row's "plus real `System.Events` bytes" phrasing would have over-ratified: all 90 event entries record the *same* blob at the *same* pinned block, so the fixture never asserts any variant was emitted. Scenario-driven emission is the §4.7 suites' job, evidenced through the environment-run contract (SQ-139/SQ-202).
