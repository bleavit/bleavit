## Question

**The supply-chain gate's ecosystem coverage is enumerated, so a third ecosystem stays silent until somebody adds it.** The cargo half fixed exactly this shape by deriving its set from `git ls-files` and failing on any disagreement in either direction. The ecosystem list is the residual copy of the defect that motivated that fix, and SQ-985 exists because a coverage hole reports as silence rather than as absence.
