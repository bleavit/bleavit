## Question

Must the L-1 identity `PositionTotals ≡ Σ Positions` hold *between* partial `sweep_dust` calls (a vault drained over multiple crank calls when a prefix exceeds `ReapBatch`), or is a mid-teardown vault a defined exception?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15**: it must hold — A2 reaps per entry, checked-subtracting each drained balance from `PositionTotals` and removing the total only at zero, so the identity is preserved between calls (no exception); regression-tested
