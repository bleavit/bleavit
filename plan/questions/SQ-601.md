## Question

**11 §11.8.3's treasury console needs per-stream reads, and 02 §7.6 forbids exactly that.** The S16 row cites *"stream storage per 02"*, and 02 freezes no `pallet-futarchy-treasury` storage at all — its §7.6 closing rule is the opposite instruction: *"every treasury consumer MUST bind `nav()` rather than raw `pallet-futarchy-treasury::State`."* But `nav()` publishes only the `stream_remainders` aggregate, while `futarchy_treasury.claim_stream(id)` is per stream and §11.8.3 requires *"claimable now"* displayed per row. So the console's central read is unbuildable as written: freezing the storage contradicts §7.6, and binding `nav()` cannot answer it. Considered and deliberately not decided during the v28 freeze, because unlike the six reads v28 did freeze, this one is not a naming gap — it is two rules that cannot both hold. The likely resolutions are a fifteenth `FutarchyApi` method returning a caller's streams, or a stated §7.6 exception; the first keeps rule 2's *exact chain read* property and the second does not

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07 (contract v29).** 02 §7.6 needs no exception and its text does not move: it forbids binding **raw storage**, and a published runtime-API projection is not raw storage — `nav()` is itself one. `treasury_streams` is that projection, so §7.6's rule is preserved rather than carved into, and 11 §11.4 rule 2's *exact chain read* property is kept
