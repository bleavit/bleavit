## Question

**`sudo.sudo_as` denied (R-1 spec correction).** 06 §3.3's wrapper table lists `sudo_as` as "recursed", but it dispatches the inner call as `Signed(who)` for a caller-chosen `who` — letting the founding multisig forge any signed origin: steal VIT (`balances.transfer`) or impersonate a protocol sovereign (e.g. the welfare settlement account) to drive `ledger.settle_*` directly, defeating 06 §3.1's "SettleAuthority reachable through exactly one path" and D-13's containment (`sudo`/`sudo_unchecked_weight` dispatch as Root, which satisfies no such account check). B1a **denies `sudo_as` outright** (it has no bootstrap use `sudo`-as-Root doesn't cover, 09 §5.3). Should 06 §3.3 formally narrow the `sudo_as` row from "recursed" to "denied"?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — 06 §3.3 splits the sudo row: `sudo`/`sudo_unchecked_weight` recursed, **`sudo_as` denied entirely** (recursing the inner call cannot contain an outer origin forge). Ripple applied to 09 §5.3, 00 D-13 and 14 TH-29.
