## Question

**The keeper cannot complete the market lifecycle after E1.** `keeper/bleavit-keeper/src/planner.rs:661` plans `Market.reap`, which now fails `NotReapable` until `Market.sweep_revenue` has run — so an unmodified keeper stalls every book at Sweep and no POL returns at all. The Cleanup role needs the sweep crank ahead of reap plus a snapshot field for the swept marker. Deliberately not fixed inside E1: `keeper/` is a separate cargo workspace outside that milestone's file ownership, and a half-considered change to crank ordering, dedup and metrics roles is worse than none

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-29** (`9077855`): the planner cranks `Market.sweep_revenue` from the terminal latch at `PRIORITY_MARKET_SWEEP = 125` and gates `reap` on the `SweptMarkets` marker
