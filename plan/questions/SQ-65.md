## Question

POL seeding split count: 04 §10 says "one split funds both branches' books (the mirror branch's seed is the free image of the live one)", but `seed_book` does one `do_split` **per book** (2·`b·ln2` escrow for a decision pair; the mirror branch-USDC is left with the treasury). The spec-reviewer confirmed the per-book reading MATCHES 13 §5.5's commitment arithmetic (`13,863 = 2·10,000·ln2` for the PARAM pair), so the code is consistent with 13 while reading against 04 §10's "one split". Is decision-pair escrow `b·ln2` (one split) or `2·b·ln2` (per-book, treasury reclaims the stray pair)?

## Status

**closed 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification) — already resolved by B1b (`73d9d2c`).** Production does **one** split per branch pair, not one per book: `market_core::seed_branch_pair` performs a single `do_split` and transfers both branch legs to their books (`crates/market-core/src/lib.rs:1154-1215`), wired for the decision pair, both gate pairs and rerun top-ups; per-book `seed` survives only for the unbranched Baseline. 04 §10's “one split funds both branches' books” governs and is implemented. The row's premise was arithmetically wrong: 13 §5.5's 13,863 is the per-book **commitment meter** (08 §3 charges both branches by design), not a pair escrow figure — commitment and escrow are different quantities.
