## Question

**Phase-4 community-schedule creation path is unspecified and possibly foreclosed.** 08 §2.1 (amended) says the community pot's 24-month schedule "is created at Phase-4 arming", but: `vesting.force_vested_transfer` is a D-13 nobody row (denied for ALL origins, forever), the community pot is a **keyless derived account** that can never sign `vested_transfer`, and `sudo_as` is denied (SQ-99) — so no existing mechanism can create pallet-vesting schedules out of the pot. Candidate mechanisms: a treasury-pallet VIT-stream analog, plain scheduled transfers under a distribution pallet/call, or a spec'd runtime-internal dispatch.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-23 (B20).** Added the treasury-owned `create_community_schedule` PARAM leaf: exact Phase-4 arming latch, derived community-pot source, remaining-allocation and minimum-amount checks, claimant-adverse floor, atomic production `VestedTransfer` adapter, and a 4,096 lifetime schedule bound. The new operational event/storage are outside the frozen 02 ingest surface, so the integration contract remains unchanged.
