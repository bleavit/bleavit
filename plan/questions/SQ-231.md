## Question

**Attack-flow inflation of step-5 grading and step-9 `L̂` (S4's surviving finding).** 08 §5.2 defines `L̂` = POL depth + non-POL contest notional — explicitly "the same measured quantity graded against dec.v_min in step 5" — and 05 §5.6 bounds wash flow only inside `C_hold` of the non-gating ManipFloor̂ diagnostic. Attack-generated gross flow therefore both promotes a thin book to decision grade and inflates the `AttackCost̂` that licenses the flip. S4's executed-ledger simulation demonstrates flips at 0.76–0.90×3·InCapPrize (PARAM/TREASURY; CODE/META are saved by their gate books), violating the D-4 sub-3P guarantee in the TH-4 regime. Is a manipulation-resistant depth measure (time-weighted net open interest / capital-at-risk, or a flow-cap-like ceiling inside the gate) intended for steps 5/9, or is this an accepted residual absorbed by the empirical A-2 elasticity? Decides whether the PARAM/TREASURY δ floors can be Phase-0-confirmed and whether V-12 publication proceeds.
