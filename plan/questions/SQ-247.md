## Question

**Does a 05 T13 rerun's extra POL consume the current epoch's `pol.budget_epoch`?** 08 §4.4 scopes shrink-to-fit to "the epoch's qualified slate's total commitment"; a T13 branch-rerun seeds additional books mid-lifecycle whose subsidy is not part of any slate qualification. B10's shrink-to-fit freezes the slate commitment at Seed entry and does not re-charge reruns (status-quo: reruns proceed; their obligations still enter the treasury `pol_commitments` NAV netting). Should reruns instead be budget-charged (and refusable) against the current epoch's meter?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** Ratified: a T13 rerun's extra POL is **not** charged against the current epoch's `pol.budget_epoch`. Shrink-to-fit is evaluated once over the qualified slate at Seed entry and a rerun is by construction not part of any qualification (`pallets/epoch/src/lib.rs:942-944`, `crates/epoch-core/src/lib.rs:856-914`). No second meter is needed because the exposure is **structurally** bounded rather than budget-bounded: `delayed_once` and `rerun` are one-way flags with mutually exclusive producers, so each proposal admits at most one rerun and one POL doubling, capping epoch rerun exposure at 2× the qualified commitment. 08 §4.4 gained the normative paragraph; the obligation still enters NAV netting and the seed fails status-quo if `POL` cannot fund it.
