## Question

**08 §5.3's prize-scaled `pol.b` and δ are normative but unimplemented.** §5.3 mandates `pol.b(class, P) = b_floor(class)·max(1, P/P_ref(class))` and the matching δ slope, and §5.3's ceiling non-bindingness argument depends on that seeding ("a book seeded at floor `b` while `v_min` carries the `2P` scaling is **not** a configuration the normative seeding produces"). The runtime seeds **flat** `Params` values (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:3334-3367`) and `crates/futarchy-treasury-core/tests/treasury_vectors.rs:292-297` explicitly records that the scaled Rust surface is absent. So the shipped configuration is exactly the one §5.3 says its own bound does not cover. Implement the scaling, or amend §5.3 to make the floors normative and the scaling advisory.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (A13, merged with SQ-270)** — the blanket "the runtime seeds flat `Params` values" claim is false at HEAD for TREASURY and, with SQ-173's envelope source, for PARAM/CODE/META too. The `treasury_vectors.rs` comment recording an absent Rust scaling surface is stale and superseded by the production helpers.
