## Question

**Attestor recall with an open challenge strands the challenge.** `attestor.set_members` (values-track recall) can remove an attestor while one of its attestations has an open challenge; the later `resolve_challenge` then fails closed with `NotMember` (the member row — and its stored bond basis — is gone) and the challenge stays open indefinitely. Fail-closed is G-1-safe (no slash against a vanished bond) but the challenger's bond is locked in limbo. Should recalls be refused while liabilities are open, should departing `AttestorInfo` rows be retained until settlement, or should bond/challenge liabilities move to storage that survives recall?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch X · implemented).** The ruling is in code: seating retains a departing member that still carries an unsettled liability as an **inactive `AttestorInfo` row** (`crates/attestor-core/src/lib.rs` `set_members` + `has_unsettled_liability`), so governance authority ends immediately while the slash basis survives until every window and challenge settles. Uses the already-frozen 02 §7.5 `active` field — **no storage reshape, no contract change**. Verification **refuted the row's own framing**: the challenger's bond is *not* "locked in limbo" because attestor bonds are arithmetic-only — no custody exists at all (`pallets/attestor/src/lib.rs` scope-boundary header; zero `fungible`/`Currency` deps in either crate), so the stranded liability was notional. The real harm is different and was under-stated: the challenge could never reach a terminal state, which **permanently suppressed the attestation from quorum** (`attestation_counts` never counts `Open`) and **permanently shielded the attestor from a `false_count` strike** — exactly the residual SQ-248 recorded, i.e. a for-cause recall laundered the offense. Fixed alongside a second defect the row did not name: `set_members` reset `false_count` to 0 for **continuing** members (the in-source KNOWN LIMITATION), so one no-op re-election wiped every strike and 06 §7's second-false ejection threshold was unreachable; strikes now carry forward while `bond` re-binds at seating per 13 §1 (SQ-248). Two try-state invariants added (ejected ⇒ not active; every open challenge has a member row). Failing-then-passing regression tests in `pallets/attestor/src/tests.rs`; the pre-existing test that pinned the defect as intended behaviour was replaced. Real bond **custody** remains SQ-263/SQ-293.
