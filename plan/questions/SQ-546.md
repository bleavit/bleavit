## Question

**A key can be amended to a value that disables its own amendment class, and two are reachable today.** `gate.p_max` publishes no hard minimum (13 §1's column is an em dash; the stored Fixed type floors at 0), the veto is strict `>`, and gate books are universal over all four market-bearing classes — so **five lawful amendments over 20 epochs** reach a state where every proposal in every class vetoes, **including the META amendment that would repair it**. There is no finite-price escape: the κ clamp rounds inward and bottoms at one raw 1e-9 unit, never zero, so `s_adopt > 0` for every reachable series. Independently, `sec.prize.meta` ships with no ceiling on the published argument that over-raising "stalls that class's own proposals **and nothing else**" — but at the 21,256,533 META NAV floor the maximum seedable prize is **669,315.42**, so **one** lawful doubling (600,000 → 1,200,000) stops the META repair proposal reaching Seed and freezes the whole META surface after two epochs. **Ruling needed:** a hard min for `gate.p_max`, and the general rule that no kernel-bounded key may reach a value disabling its own amendment class
