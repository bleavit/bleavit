## Question

**06 §7 challenge-bond basis: live `att.bond` or the challenged attestor's held bond?** "anyone posting a bond ≥ 50% of the attestor bond" diverges whenever att.bond is amended between registration and challenge; the live reading makes contesting in-flight CODE/META artifacts more expensive after a raise (defense-softening direction). B10 remediation implements the conservative stored-bond basis (symmetric with the slash side). Ratify or flip to live.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B2 · ratified).** 06 §7 now states the **held-bond** basis normatively: the challenge floor is 50% of the bond held by the challenged attestor when the challenge opens, both slash sides use the same basis, rounding is up against the forfeiting party, and an `att.bond` amendment binds a member only at next seating; 13 §1's row carries the binds-at-seating note. The row's "symmetric with the slash side" justification was **verified, not assumed** — both legs read `AttestorInfo.bond` (`crates/attestor-core/src/lib.rs:261-267` and `:316-322`), pinned by `pallets/attestor/src/tests.rs:404-497`. Verification also supplied the argument the row lacked: the live reading is defense-softening in **both** directions — after a raise it prices contesting out, and after a cut it makes challenge-spam denial-of-attestation cheap, since an open challenge suppresses its attestation from quorum and `att.bond` is reachable to ×0.5. One residual recorded, not resolved: a challenge open against an attestor who is then recalled becomes permanently unresolvable against them (`attestor-core:316-321` returns `NotMember`), so a for-cause recall shields the attestor from adjudication — harmless only while bonds are arithmetic-only.
