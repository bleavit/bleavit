## Question

**`sweep_dust` can race Sweep and destroy the POL return.** 03 §5.4 makes the ledger dust sweep explicitly independent of the market and routes residual escrow to `INSURANCE`. A book whose `sweep_revenue` has not run by the time `ledger.archive_delay` elapses therefore loses its return to exactly the account E1 exists to stop feeding. The orderings are bounded far apart by design — Sweep is available from the terminal block with no delay, `sweep_dust` only a whole `archive_delay` (up to one year) later — but they are **not locked**, and SQ-511 is precisely the condition that makes the gap reachable. Pinned by `sweep_survives_a_ledger_first_vault_sweep_and_pays_nothing_twice`, which asserts the ledger-first path returns 0 rather than double-paying

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-29** (`a2de0ff` + 03 §5.4a): the race is closed **on-chain**, not merely by keeper ordering — the `MarketSweepStatus` seam makes `sweep_dust` refuse while a seeded book is unswept. Keeper priority is now defence in depth, not the guarantee; the review found the on-chain hole was reachable *deliberately* because both cranks are permissionless
