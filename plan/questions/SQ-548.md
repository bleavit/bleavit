## Question

**`dec.trailing` can lawfully exceed `dec.window`, and the two normative guards disagree at exactly the collision point.** `dec.window`'s hard min (14,400) **equals** `dec.trailing`'s default (14,400); `epoch-core` compares `trailing <= window` and `pallets/market` compares strictly `<`. In the equality band live-params validation passes while every decision-window registration errors, `?`-propagated through the Seed-phase market factory. Worse, `dec.trailing`'s max (28,800) exceeds `dec.window`'s min, so `trailing > window` is reachable — in **three amendments over four epochs** — and fails `EpochParams::validate` outright, halting the epoch crank and breaking try-state. The spec makes `dec.trailing` the final sub-window of `dec.window`, and 13 §5's occupancy screen explicitly *admits* lowering `dec.window` ("the opposite direction is ordinary business"), so nothing refuses the approach. **Ruling needed:** reconcile the two guards, and bound `dec.trailing`'s max at or below `dec.window`'s min. **CORRECTED 2026-08-01 by adversarial review of PR #206, before any ruling was taken.** The original row asserted strict `dec.trailing < dec.window`. **04 §194 and 05 §3.1/§271 define the trailing window as the final `dec.trailing` *of* the decision window — that is containment, `trailing ≤ window`** — and no document states strictness, so `trailing == window` is representable and is **not** a breach. What survives: the reachable `trailing > window` state, and the implementation disagreement between the two normative guards at the equality point
