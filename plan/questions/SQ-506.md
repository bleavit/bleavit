## Question

**The Phase-0 simulation cannot test the revenue hypothesis it is being asked to test.** 08 §10.6 states the central economic hypothesis — POL is paid *to* traders, fees are collected *from* them, so net the protocol is solvent only if uninformed flow of order `V*` exists. The 15 §4.9 simulation cannot falsify it: its flow targets are `dec.v_min × formation_stratum` (`corr(volume, dec.v_min)` = 0.947, r² = 0.90) and nothing responds to fee level, proposal salience or trader population, so a `mkt.fee` sweep would show revenue perfectly linear in the rate with **zero** volume elasticity — a property of the model, not of any market. Four concrete defects sit under it: `ExecutedBook.fees` is computed and **discarded**, so no fee figure appears in the artifact at all; `book.events.clear()` truncates the pre-extension ledger so lifetime volume under-reports 2.5–3.3× for the 51 % of proposals that extend; one Baseline book is instantiated per **proposal** rather than per **epoch**, inflating aggregate volume 4.4 %; and only 24 of 10,000 proposals carry per-proposal flow fields. **Ruling (decide-and-proceed, R-1): the demand-side driver is a Track-S obligation recorded normatively in 15 §4.9, and no self-funding claim may rest on the present model.** 08 §10 is written to that constraint throughout — it uses the sim as a *consistency check* on structural relations (τ, held-capital-to-floor ratio), never as a forecast

## Status

**open** — owner Track S; blocks nothing in E1–E4, blocks any future claim that the crossover is reached
