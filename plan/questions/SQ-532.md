## Question

**`keeper.budget_epoch`'s kernel floor is now ≈ 175× the load it was sized to bound.** 13 §1 sets a hard minimum of 6,000 USDC/epoch, justified as "below it the metered budget cannot cover decision-critical cranks at the 1× rebate bound and A-1 fails silently". Under the SQ-531 basis the *whole trading window* demands ≈ 148 USDC/epoch and the decision-critical window ≈ 34, so the floor cannot bind and the meter provides no real protection — `KeeperBudgetExhausted` is now unreachable in normal operation. Not a solvency problem (the budget is a ceiling, not a spend — 08 §1.1) but a safety rail that no longer rails. Lowering a kernel floor is a CODE change, so it is filed rather than taken

## Status

**open** — needs a CODE change to move the floor, and a decision on whether a meter that cannot bind should be re-sized or retired
