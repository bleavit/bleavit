## Question

**Guardian accountability events are outside 02 §6.** Should `ReviewFailed`/`RecallScheduled`/`ActionRatified` be appended with a 02 §13 version bump and joint sign-off, or remain off-contract chain events?

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C, contract v6) — **the underlying question is answered normatively.** 02 §6 now states its own scope: it freezes the names and field shapes of the events the canonical frontend ingests, is exhaustive for that ingest set, and is *not* an exhaustive declaration of every event a pallet may emit. An event MUST be listed iff (a) the frontend ingests it for an 11 workflow, (b) another architecture doc makes it normative, or (c) it is the sole record of a terminal proposal/vault/cohort transition; anything else MAY ship as a pallet-local diagnostic outside the contract, and §6 now names the shipped diagnostics explicitly so the boundary is never ambiguous. Applying the rule, all eight shipped guardian accountability variants are contracted (`MembersSet`, `ActionProposed`, `ActionApproved`, `ActionRatified`, `ReviewFailed`, `RecallScheduled`, `RecallEnacted`, `PlaybookRegistrationSet`). **The row's 2026-07-17 extension was over-broad and is corrected:** the three playbook lifecycle events were *already* contracted, there is no guardian `IntakePaused` variant (pause-intake rides the contracted `GuardianAction.power`), and upholding a veto emits `ActionRatified`, not a distinct uphold event.
