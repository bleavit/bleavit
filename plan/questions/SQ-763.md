## Question

**10 §9.2 makes its storage shares *"user-adjustable locally"* and §9.3 states a bound the adjustment can walk under, with no rule for what happens then.** §9.2: *"Fixed internal shares (user-adjustable locally): raw samples 60%, candles 20%, events+archive 15%, metadata 5%."* §9.3 bounds `metadataCache` at *"≤ 8 blobs / ≤ 15 MB desktop, ≤ 3 blobs / ≤ 3.75 MB mobile"* and pins *"the current and next-authorized runtime's metadata"* non-evictable. Raising the metadata share is harmless — §9.3's own bound still binds, which F8 enforces with a `min`. **Lowering** it is not: a local preference below what the pinned blobs occupy leaves a budget the pinned set alone exceeds, and §9.3 forbids resolving that by evicting a pin. F8 refuses such a pass (a release configuration that does not fit its own platform budget), which is the fail-closed disposal but converts a user preference into an error. **Ruling needed:** does the adjustability reach the metadata share at all, and if it does, is the floor the pinned set's measured size?

## Status

**open — raised by the R-6 re-review of F8.** The unsafe direction is silently dropping a pin, which the shipped reading refuses. Filed to **B7**
