## Question

**16 §8.4's arming condition is normative and no code enforces it.** `Σ b_ext ≤ Σ pol.b(live)` — external depth bounded at parity with Bleavit's own at switch-on — is stated as an arming condition in 16 §8.4 and is implemented **nowhere**. `b_ext` appears in no pallet and in no migration; the Phase-4 transition (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/migrations.rs`) applies the treasury arming gate and the cap plan and nothing else. Surfaced by an adversarial review of N14's slot-pricing derivation, as a side finding while refuting something else — nothing in the N1…N13 reviews had looked for it, because each checked that code matched the section it implemented rather than that every normative sentence had an implementer. **Why it is the largest item on this branch:** N12 anchored its entire competing-venue arming rung to this condition, ran a ~2 h calibration to measure governance damage *at* it, and recorded its error direction as unsafe — while nothing makes the condition true. So the measurement describes a chain state no mechanism guarantees, and 16 §8.4's mitigation list reads as four mitigations when one of them is a sentence. Pinned by `slot_pricing.check_arming_condition_unenforced`, written as a **tripwire**: it asserts the gap exists and must fail — and be rewritten — the moment an implementer appears

## Status

**open — partially implemented 2026-08-03; the derivable half is in, the half that is not is stated.** Shipped: `LiveExternalDepth` (the external side, in posted cash `Σ 2·b·ln 2` — the same units `LivePolCommitments` stores, so the two sides compare without reconstructing `b`), maintained on `register` and released at the single terminal exit, with a **try-state fold** that recomputes it from `Terms` and is pinned to fail in *both* directions (low re-opens the bound, high denies honest clients); the `ArmingBoundExceeded` error; and the **switch-on check in the Phase-4 transition**, which is 16 §8.4's own wording. It is not vacuous — registration is **not** phase-gated, so hosted questions accumulate through Phase 3 under `phase3.tvl_cap` and can be live at the moment of arming. Guarded on a non-zero external side so arming with no hosted questions cannot be blocked by POL merely being uncommitted; when the external side is non-zero it binds and can refuse the transition, which is the clause doing its job (remedy: let questions terminate or seed POL, then re-attempt). **Deliberately NOT shipped, with a measured reason:** the *continuous* form, enforced at `register` against `Σ pol.b(live)`. `LivePolCommitments` holds protocol subsidy only while decision books are seeded, so the instantaneous sum is zero for most of an epoch and the check would refuse essentially every registration outside Bleavit's own decision windows — **demonstrated, not predicted: 21 of this pallet's 28 tests failed exactly that way** before the comparison was removed, and the mock's empty POL is also the chain's ordinary state between windows. Whether the condition must hold continuously, and against what non-blinking measure (the instantaneous one cannot be it), has no derivable answer yet and is what stays open here. Guessing a measure under milestone pressure is how the unsafe direction gets shipped. Gates: pallet 30/30, clippy clean. Batch **X**
