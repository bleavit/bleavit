## Question

**RULED AND EXECUTED 2026-08-06 — contract v28 freezes `Filings`, `ClosedAt` and `AckRecords` for both registry instances in 02 §7.4.** Per instance, because `pallet-registry` is instantiated twice and the two allocators share no filing-id space. **11 §11.8.6 requires registry state rendered with countdowns and 02 freezes no registry storage — only the events.** *"Registry state (filings, challenge windows, watchtower acknowledgments, slash outcomes) renders in the Advanced area with countdowns"*, and the filing row needs a value-scaled bond and the instance's occupancy bound. 02 §6 freezes eleven registry event rows per instance; 02 §7 has no registry section. An event stream is not one of §11.4 rule 2's three precondition sources, so S19's filing and challenge rows are built on reads the contract does not publish — and the bond is value-scaled, so it cannot be carried as a constant either. Client posture: `UNREADABLE['O-8']`/`['O-9']` declare both `blocking`, and `filingBlocks`/`challengeFilingBlocks` take every figure as a supplied read rather than deriving one.
