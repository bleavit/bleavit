## Question

**`UndecidingTimeout` has no spec home.** The referenda config constant (production `7 * BLOCKS_PER_DAY`) appears nowhere in `docs/architecture/` yet the constants rule gives every non-02 value a 13 row. Surfaced by the B11 Round-B delta audit when the benchmark wasm forked it (7 d production / 14 d bench fixture — the upstream `nudge_referendum_no_deposit` fixture needs one extra prepare period; release wasm unchanged). Is 7 days normative (e.g. tied to the 06 §2.1 prepare period) and owed a 13 row + limit-coverage classification, or an unowned pallet-plumbing knob?

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B6): 13 §3.4 now carries the row (7 d; metadata-visible, hence not "pallet-internal") plus the normative constraint `UndecidingTimeout ≥ max(track prepare period)`. Row's premise corrected — 13 has no every-value-needs-a-row rule (that is AGENTS.md, not spec) and the value is not derived from 06 §2.1. Verified benign: zero margin against the 7-day entrenched track affects only a referendum whose decision deposit was never placed
