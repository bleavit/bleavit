## Question

**05 §5.6 calls `ManipFloor̂` a "mandatory diagnostic" the engine "computes and emits, per decision" — and no Rust implements it.** Surfaced while fixing SQ-544. `grep` over `crates/`, `pallets/` and `runtime/` finds no `manip_floor`, no `C_disp`, and **no `DecisionDiagnostics` type at all**; the only implementations are Python (`reference-model` and `simulation`). So the Phase 3–4 measurement obligation §5.6 places on the values layer — "if published `ManipFloor̂` persistently reads below `3·InCapPrize` … the values layer MUST tighten δ" — rests on a figure the chain never publishes. Nothing is *wrong* on-chain: `attack_cost_hat` (the §5.6 **gate**) is implemented and gates correctly, and this quantity never gates in v1. What is wrong is that a MUST-obligation names an input with no producer, so the obligation is unfalsifiable in production

## Status

**open.** Milestone-sized rather than a fix: emitting it needs a decision-time diagnostic surface (event or view type), which is 02 contract surface and therefore a bump plus joint sign-off, and it needs `futarchy-fixed` `ln` on the decide path whose weight is not budgeted in 13 §5. Deliberately **not** folded into the SQ-544 repair, which is a correction to an existing figure and must stay reviewable as one. Filed to **X** (real code). Its resolution should decide whether the obligation binds on-chain publication or on off-chain recomputation from `TelemetryApi`-grade inputs — the latter is far cheaper and may be the honest reading
