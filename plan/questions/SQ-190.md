## Question

**Decision sanity-band ownership is unspecified.** Pin the versioned source, freeze time and per-book/per-class application so a live mutable adapter cannot change decision-grade eligibility after market opening.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch X)** — **already owned.** The sanity band is a Wasm/kernel constant, so it cannot be moved by a live adapter after market opening, and role selection is derived from the stored book kind. `MetricSpec` component sanity bounds are a separate metric-input concept and do not own market-price eligibility. Nothing is owed.
