## Question

**The expedited CODE lane force-rejects the proposal it exists to ship, has no production writer, and its published latency is unreachable.** (a) Under `PB-LEDGER-FREEZE` — the lane's primary trigger — 06 §6.3's T20 sweep covers every live non-terminal state including `Queued`, and `market.set_frozen(true)` halts `crank_observe` so the mandatory 72 h gate market cannot accrue TWAP: **no lawful trace reaches `Executed`** while the freeze is active. The only walkable arm is a source-less staged `PB-MIGRATION` halt. (b) `Expedited[pid] = true` is unreachable in production — the sole enqueue caller passes a literal `false`. (c) 09 §3.1's "≈ 9–10 days, inside the 14-day freeze envelope" is false at **every** lawful configuration: sweeping all lawful `epoch.length` × onset × {24 h kernel floor, 7 d CODE default} gives a global best of **14 d + 1 block** and a global worst of **82 d** (25 d / 46 d at the 21-day default). The sentence prices four windows, omits the 05 §3.1 pipeline entirely, and substitutes the 24 h floor for a 7 d default roughly 126 days of amendment away. Its own four terms sum to 10 d sequential / 7 d concurrent — never 9. **Unsafe direction:** operators may rely on a freeze that expires before the repair lands
