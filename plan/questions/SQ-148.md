## Question

**`execute()` charged-weight semantics.** 09 §1.2(11) bounds a batch's *declared* weight at kernel `prop.max_weight` (25 % of block) but no doc states how the guard extrinsic's own charged weight must relate to it. B5 implements pre-charge `execute(16) + prop.max_weight` with a `PostDispatchInfo` refund to actuals (never undercharges; the runtime dispatcher currently erases inner post-info, so production refunds fall back to declared inner weights — conservative). Ratify the pre-charge+refund model (and whether the dispatcher should preserve post-info).

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B6): pre-charge + post-dispatch refund ratified in 15 §4.5, with the declared-inner-weight fallback made normative for dispatchers that do not surface inner post-info (verified: the runtime dispatcher does not, and cannot undercharge)
