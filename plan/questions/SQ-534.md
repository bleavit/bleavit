## Question

**`keeper.rebate` is a stored USDC value while the fee it compensates is fixed in VIT, and nothing couples them.** SQ-531 re-derived the rebate as 3× the measured crank fee at 08 §9's 0.05 USDC/VIT placeholder. The fee is weight-fixed in **VIT** (0.0017 VIT, invariant to price); `keeper.rebate` is a **stored USDC** row. Only one side moves when VIT reprices: at ¼× the derivation price the rebate is 12× the fee, at 4× it is **0.75×** — below the fee itself, so cranking becomes loss-making and A-1 fails silently, which is exactly the failure 08 §6.2 says the old 3,000 budget caused. 13 §1 permits a 10× move of `fee.vit_usdc`, and no code updates or screens the rebate when it changes. **I asserted the opposite in three places** — the kernel doc comment, 08 §6.2 and the 13 §1 row all said the ratio held "at any price" because rebate and fee scale together. They do not, and all three are corrected. What SQ-531 did fix is orthogonal and stands: the derivation is anchored to a measured weight, so re-deriving at any price now gives the right answer, where before no price did
