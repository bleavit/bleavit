## Question

`DenyUnsupportedInstructions` admits `SetAppendix`, `SetErrorHandler`, `TransferReserveAsset`,
`DepositReserveAsset`, `InitiateReserveWithdraw` and `InitiateTeleport`, while 09 §6.1 disables all
nine inner-program instructions. Executor tests prove admitted appendix and reserve-deposit paths.
A paid message from Asset Hub, relay or Coretime can therefore make Bleavit execute an inner program
or forward assets, and outbound forwarding reaches the welfare-observing router. Recursive
`DenyTransact` still closes arbitrary dispatch. Ordinary Asset Hub user reachability through its
current filter is not established here; a compromised accepted chain can author the message.
