## Question

**A fail-closed `PhaseFlags` makes the client state a fact about chain state the chain never stated.** INV-FE-12 and §11.5 make an unreadable or undecodable `Constitution.PhaseFlags` fail closed, and `market-reads.ts` implements that by substituting `LEDGER_FROZEN_BIT` — so `tradeBlocks` emits *"the constitution's trading-enabled bit is clear"* and *"a ledger freeze is active"*. Both are sentences about the chain, rendered where a user reads them, and neither was read. The blocking behaviour is right and is not in question; what is in question is the **wording**, and it is the same class as the badged-zero quote this round repaired one field over (V-323) — 10 §2.2 governs a value's status and says nothing about a refusal's prose. Distinguishing the two would need `TradeInputs` to carry a tri-state rather than a `Finalized<boolean>`, which is a shape change this round did not make. **Ruling needed:** must a fail-closed refusal say *this could not be read* rather than asserting the state it assumed, and if so does that obligation live in §11.4 rule 3's expected/actual display or in INV-FE-14?
