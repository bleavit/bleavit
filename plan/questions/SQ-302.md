## Question

**The settlement observer can permanently block a terminal transition it ought to tolerate.** 04 §2 and 03 §5.4 (B4 ratification, *corrected during the R-6 review*) require marker and latch to commit or roll back together — the shipped `with_storage_layer` composition is right, and the batch's first draft had this backwards. The residual is narrower: `observe_proposal_terminal` hard-fails on `ensure!(!ids.is_empty(), TryStateViolation)` (`pallets/market/src/lib.rs:1655`), so a vault with no registered books would roll back its own `void`/`settle_scalar` on every attempt and never settle. Unreachable today — vault creation and first book creation are the same call (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:2072-2091`) — and the rollback direction is the safe one, so this is latent liveness, not a solvency risk. Make the empty-book-set case a legitimate no-op rather than an error, or establish the unreachability structurally instead of incidentally.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch X)** — **row premise disproved.** The failure is accurately described but its premise is not a reachable production state: vault creation is structurally coupled to first-book deployment through both the market storage layer and the enclosing epoch Seed transaction, so no origin can create an orphan vault. The `ensure!(!ids.is_empty())` at `pallets/market/src/lib.rs:1655` is therefore a defensive corruption check that fails closed on a broken vault↔book invariant, not a latent liveness defect. **Kept as-is deliberately** — converting it to a no-op would silently accept the corrupt state it exists to catch (G-1).
