## Question

13 §3.1 mandates that clients bind the phase fractions via pallet metadata constants, but `pallet-epoch` exposes no phase-fraction constants (`extra_constants` has only the contract version/bounds/`TickBatch`) — so neither the keeper nor the future FE has a compliant binding path and both must mirror the kernel grid in code. Confirm the queued B2 contract amendment adds the actual metadata-constant exposure to `pallet-epoch`.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (keeper implementation; stale chain premise).** `Epoch::PhaseOffsets` was already present in the metadata surface; the keeper now decodes and validates the seven ordered fractions, carries them in `ChainSnapshot`, derives all phase boundaries from the live value, and suppresses tick/settlement planning when the constant is absent or malformed. No frontend exists yet to assess, and no contract bump applies.
