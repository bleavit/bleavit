## Question

**The SDK pays collators a second time, ungoverned, and nothing in the spec knows it exists.** `pallet_authorship::EventHandler` is `(CollatorSelection, RuntimeCollatorAuthorship)`, so `CollatorSelection::note_author` runs every block and pays the author **half the `PotStake` pot** — no meter, no origin check, no budget line, no NAV accounting, no try-state coverage, and absent from 08 §10.1. It duplicates 08 §2.4's governed `ops.collators` path. It cannot be unhooked: `note_author` also maintains `LastAuthoredBlock`, which drives candidate kick-out. Inert **by construction rather than design** — nothing funds `PotStake` and 08 §9 burns VIT fees — but it is an ordinary `PalletId` account anyone may fund, at which point it pays per block
