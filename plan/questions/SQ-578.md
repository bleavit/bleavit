## Question

**Should a permanently-undecodable preimage be recorded terminally instead of erroring until expiry?** Split out of SQ-552 rather than decided by a frontend milestone, because the fix is backend and contract-bearing. Today `execute` errors `BadPreimage` and the proposal stays `Queued` until the T15 crank records `Expired` after `grace_end` — safe (G-1: nothing dispatches), truthful about the *outcome*, and silent about the *cause*. Two things argue for a terminal `RejectReason::BadPreimage`: 09 §1.2's own item-7 reasoning already distinguishes contention (retry within grace) from a **permanent** defect, which it says "belongs to item 12: rolling it back there records the failure and bounds the retries, which is the correct treatment for something that will not improve by waiting" — and a malformed preimage is exactly that; and every keeper attempt through the grace window is spent on a mandate that can never succeed. Against: it adds a variant to 02 §4's frozen `RejectReason`, so it is an integration-contract bump (joint sign-off, descriptor + feed + surface-manifest regeneration) plus a runtime change, for an outcome that is already safe and whose cause the client can determine before signing (11 §11.5 row 13 now requires presenting it as unexecutable). Reachability is real, not theoretical: the queue-time check commits to a *hash*, and a hash commits to bytes, not to their validity. Decide with a backend milestone that is already bumping the contract, not on its own
