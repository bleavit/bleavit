## Question

07 §7's registry cranks (`crank_close` "Signed (keeper, rebated)", `reap_epoch`) name no funding line. B9 routes them through the **oracle budget line** (unmetered by the 08 §6.3 keeper meter), by analogy with 07's pallet-table row pinning `crank_round_close`/probe cranks to the oracle line — the registry is a 07-owned component. Confirm, or assign them to the KEEPER meter.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B3): **split ruling — ratified for `crank_close`, refused for `reap_epoch`.** 07 §7 gained a normative *Crank funding lines* note plus the missing `reap_epoch` row, and `crank_close`'s weight cell now names its line. `ack_observed` and `crank_close` are ratified on the **oracle budget line** (dispute machinery; `crank_close` is the registry's analogue of `crank_round_close`, which §13 already funds there). `reap_epoch` is **not**: 08 §6.3 assigns reaping to the metered general tranche, every other reaping-shaped crank in the tree uses `CrankClass::General` (ledger sweeps, market reap, execution expiry, coretime-quote pruning), and the general tranche's 20% cap structurally cannot starve a decision-critical crank whereas the oracle line — which funds reporter/escalation incentives — carries no such reservation. **Row premise corrected twice** by independent second-model verification: the cited precedent is 07 **§13 prose scoped to `pallet-oracle`**, not a "pallet-table row"; and this was never a spec-vs-spec conflict — §7 simply omitted `reap_epoch` entirely, making the OracleLine routing an unsupported code choice rather than a ratifiable reading. → **SQ-297**.
