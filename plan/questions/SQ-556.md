## Question

**Two doc-14 rows are inverted in the unsafe direction, and one of them says the arithmetic *is* the mitigation.** TH-64 (with 14 §4 residual 14 and **03 §5.3a(3)** — three normative locations that agree with each other) prices redemption-fee avoidance at "a factor of ≈ 1,000 against the griefer". Priced from the committed `pallet_conditional_ledger::redeem_scalar` weight it is **133/15 = 8.8667×** at the documented reference rate, and at the exact 08 §9 envelope floor (0.1×) it is **9/10** — the grief pays for itself *inside the lawful envelope*, break-even at 0.005641145 USDC/VIT. TH-11's "deposits make third-party dusting uneconomic" is inverted: 03 §4 charges the `Positions` deposit to the **recipient** on a `transfer` the sender signs, with no consent gate, so **0.662656 USDC** of attacker outlay (own deposits refunded; serial exposure 0.1) locks **6.4 USDC** of a victim's balance and all 64 slots — the victim cannot `split` or trade, and recovery needs a terminal vault plus the archive delay. Bounded only by `Preservation::Preserve`, so it lands on accounts already holding ≥ 6.4 USDC free. Third row, conservative rather than unsafe: 14 TH-16 publishes **10,900** USDC/epoch where 08 §7 derives **18,900** for the same schedule — TH-16 applied the post-fix 10 % slash to the pre-review 109,000 bond base — a 1.73× disagreement between two normative documents, which 14 §1 forbids. **Ruling needed:** TH-64's is a real repair (three documents); TH-11 needs an incidence decision; TH-16 is an R-1 correction
