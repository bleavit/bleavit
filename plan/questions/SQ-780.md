## Question

**10 §9.2's *"never a silent splice"* sits inside the degradation-ladder paragraph, and the harm it names is produced identically by anything else that deletes chart rows.** The clause names coverage metadata among what the ladder never touches: *"holes stay truthful even after eviction — an evicted range becomes a labelled 'downsampled' range, not a hole, and never a silent splice"*. A **schema migration** produces exactly that state without going near the ladder: F8's v1 → v3 upgrade empties `priceSamples` and the three candle tables while `meta.coverage` carries through unchanged, so afterwards `coveredSamples` answers a covered span with an empty array and a table states *"complete within [ranges]"* over nothing. So do `FE-IDX-001`'s partial cousins, and so would any future repair that drops rows. F8 ships the **wide** reading — the migration writes a `ChartDiscardRecord` inside its own upgrade transaction and `checkIndexAtBoot` surfaces it. **Ruling needed:** does the sentence bind every deletion of chart rows, or only the ladder?

## Status

**open — raised by the third R-6 review of F8.** The wide reading is the pessimistic one: it announces a loss the narrow reading would leave unannounced, so nothing has to be unwritten if the narrow reading wins. Filed to **B7**
