## Question

`SigningRequest` carries `prep`, `window` and `account` separately. Injected and raw adapters sign
`request.prep.scaleHex` for `request.account` without requiring `request.prep === request.window.prep`
or binding the account to the proof. The test suite reuses one genuine window with changed bytes and
then a changed account and expects success. F17 fixed the earlier unnamed proof but the adapter
boundary reintroduces the substitution path. It is latent while the composition root exposes no
production submit screen.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-12.** `TxPreparation` binds the signing account and
`GatePassed` freezes `{scaleHex, account}`. Signing requests no longer accept an
independent preparation or account; every adapter derives the exact target from
the window and rejects mutation or override. Cross-transaction/account fixtures
now fail.
