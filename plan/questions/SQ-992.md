## Question

**§8.5.2's chart moratorium contradicts §2.3 and §9.2, and neither was updated.** SQ-989's caveat says *"until it is ruled no surface renders a provider-origin chart"*. But §2.3 carries an **accepted residual risk** row for provider-fed chart manipulation, and §9.2's degradation obligation 3 requires that *"a verified observation and a provider-supplied one are summarised separately at every rung"* — both of which describe a client that renders provider-origin chart data. Either those two sections now describe a state that cannot exist, or the moratorium is narrower than its wording. Nothing renders such a chart today, so the contradiction is latent rather than shipped.
