## Question

**10 §9.1's scan-time aggregation is mandated in one clause and unspecified in four dimensions, and §9.2's ladder already owns one of them.** The sentence: *"Chain-wide `Traded` **is consumed into the candle aggregates as it is scanned** and never stored row-by-row."* It names no table, no resolution, no provenance rule, and does not say whether the folded price is `Traded.p_after` (02 §5), `Observed.o_t`, or both in one series. Two of the four are derivable and F8 derives them: the table must be **`candles1h`**, because §9.2's ladder is `raw → candles1h → candles4h → candles1d` and writing coarser destroys resolution the client held; and the provenance must be the **block's header source**, because §7 requires every row to carry the four-valued origin and §6.5 already derives every other row's label from that one argument. Two are genuinely open. F8 folds **`p_after` only** — §9.1's sentence names `Traded` and nothing else, and §9.1's own row-rate model derives the raw `priceSamples` tier from the observation grid — because merging the two would publish a bar describing neither series (`o_t` is the κ-slew TWAP observation, not the instantaneous price). A second open point falls out of the same clause: a candle is an accumulator with no deterministic primary key, so replay-idempotency (§6.5) has to come from the stored bar's own block span, which **undercounts** a hole later backfilled inside an existing span. **Ruling needed:** which series the aggregate summarises, and whether an undercount on a backfilled hole is acceptable or the bar owes a folded-block record

## Status

**open — raised by the R-6 re-review of F8.** Both open choices ship in the direction that cannot overstate: one series rather than a merged one, and undercounting rather than double-counting. Filed to **B7**
