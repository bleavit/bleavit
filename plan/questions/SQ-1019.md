## Question

§11.8.2's approve row and §11.12 E20 disagree on what gates a guardian approval. The row lists four preconditions (member; pending and unexpired; not already approved; the exact enumerated batch rendered) and names no trigger. E20 — titled *"Guardian approval"* — requires *"allowance meters, trigger-condition status"* in its V-facet and blocks on *"trigger condition not active at B′"* in its F-facet. Both are normative and neither cites the other. The runtime agrees with E20's direction: `propose_action` validates nothing, and `check_and_consume` runs inside `dispatch`, which `approve_action` reaches only at the **threshold** approval — so every dispatch refusal falls on the fifth guardian. B1 is implemented against E20: `approvalBlocks` blocks on an inactive, unread or mismatched condition, and O-3 declares the trigger reads so the check happens at B′. If the row is authoritative instead, B1 becomes a **display obligation only** and the O-3 trigger clauses and `conditionBlocks` should be reduced to rendering
