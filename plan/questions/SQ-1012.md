## Question

**10 §5.2's foreign lattice has the same hole one chain over.** `ForeignMode` is `full | restricted | unsupported | wrong-chain | unreachable`. A pinned, reachable Asset Hub on a supported `spec_version` whose metadata could not be fetched is none of them — `unreachable` says *"could not be reached"* about a chain that answered, and `unsupported` names a version that is in fact supported. Distinct from SQ-1011 because 02 §7.7 owns the foreign verdict and §13 rule 8 forbids folding the two.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-08 (R-1) — and 02 had already named it, which is why nothing in 02 changes.** §7.7's fail-closed sentence reads *"An unavailable **or unprobed** AH surface blocks the funding flow with diagnostics"*. The contract therefore distinguished *could not be reached* from *was never probed* before 10 §5.2 had a name for the second, and the row's premise — that this outcome is *"none of them"* — is right about `ForeignMode` and wrong about the contract. So the ruling is the same one SQ-1011 takes, one chain over: `ForeignMode` keeps **five** arms, all of them claims about the foreign chain, and the unprobed outcome stays outside the mode as `ForeignVerdict`'s own `unestablished` arm, carrying the same `FE-COMPAT-003`. §13 rule 8 is honoured rather than strained — the two verdicts stay separate values produced by separate probes, and what they now share is a *code*, not a verdict. Recorded in 10 §3.1's new state text and §5.2 rather than in 02, since 02 is already correct. No contract bump; §7.7's own paragraph explains why a foreign-surface change could not bump `INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` in any case
