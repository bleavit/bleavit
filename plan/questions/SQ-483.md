## Question

**The 196-market aggregate is sized for concurrently active proposals but counts closed books through the roughly one-year archive delay, so healthy throughput can exhaust it before the first reap.** `Markets` retains closed/reap-pending books until `ledger.archive_delay`; with the default 21-day epoch and typical 31-book creation load, 196 slots fill in about 6.3 epochs while a one-year delay is about 17.4 epochs. Even materially below the five-slot maximum, `create_market` can then return `TooManyMarkets` and wedge T7 although every keeper ran correctly. Contract v7 truthfully freezes the shared aggregate and market-lifetime mapping rule; it does **not** make this capacity adequate. Resolve by re-deriving the cap/delay/archival design together, preserving bounded PoV and POL commitments rather than silently raising one number.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-22 (contract v8)** — live/POL capacity remains 196 while retained rows are independently bounded at 2,240 against the one-year archive horizon; first terminal observation releases the live slot and auxiliary state. Reap discards only fixed protocol inventory, permanent canonical custody prevents future-address poisoning, active/retained telemetry is separate, and saturated reference-host weights plus lifecycle/rollback/`try_state` regressions are green. Joint backend+frontend sign-off recorded in the Decision log.
