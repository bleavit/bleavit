## Question

**Unratified execute semantics.** 06 §2.2 says an unratified execute attempt leaves the proposal Queued until grace expiry; 09 §1.2(4) says the dispatch-time re-check rejects `NotRatified`. The S1 model follows 05/06: ratification Pending keeps the mandate retryable; a referendum concluding Failed rejects immediately via T16.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — 09 §1.2(4) trued to 06 §2.2 and the code: an unratified `execute` **errors and leaves the proposal `Queued`**, retryable through `grace_end`; only the post-grace T16 crank rejects. Ripple: 05 enum comment/producer row/T16/pseudocode/truth table, 06 `NotYetRatified` → `NotRatified`, 11 §11.5 row 5. **Residual to batch C:** frozen 02 §2 still glosses `RejectReason::NotRatified` as "ratification absent/failed at execute", which this ruling supersedes; the one-line comment change rides the v6 bump (raised by the batch-D adversarial review).
