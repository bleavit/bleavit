## Question

**Can a live indexer ever supply a chart row, and if so in what document?** 10 §7's schema requires `priceSamples` and both candle tables to carry `origin ∈ {…, 'indexer'}`, which is explicit that an indexer can populate them. §8.5.2 now rules that an indexer serves **§8.2's document restricted to a range** and no second format — and that document carries `vaults`, `ops`, `balances` and `coverage` only. So as the two sections now stand, no indexer can ever supply the rows §7 says it supplies. Raised by the R-6 review of §8.5 rather than found in use, because nothing renders a provider-origin chart yet.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07** (§8.5.2 + §9.1's schema row) — the third way out: **a provider serves observed facts, and this client owns every derivation.** A chart row whose `origin` is `snapshot` or `indexer` is one the client derived locally from that provider's `ops`, never one the provider served. The badge is unchanged, which is what makes this cheap — §2.3's mitigation is that the row names the third party it came from, and it still does. Extending §8.2's document with the sample and candle tables costs more than it looks: aggregates are a **function** of the movements beside them, so a document carrying both can contradict itself and §8.4 would owe a fourth screen to catch it, and reproduce-by-anyone would start depending on two producers agreeing on a *bucketing* rather than on a serialization of what they observed — two honest producers that bucketed differently would fail `FE-PROV-004` on every pair. Nor is deriving locally a new mechanism: §9.1 already consumes chain-wide `Traded` into the candle aggregates as it is scanned and never stores a trade row. **Caveat, found by the R-6 review of this very ruling and carried openly rather than buried: the resolution is not implementable as `bleavit.snapshot.v1` stands.** Its movement alphabet is 03 §5's complete-set operations and none of the four carries a price, so there is nothing in a provider's `ops` to derive a chart row *from* — the contradiction is narrowed to one question and moved, not removed. **SQ-989** owns the remaining half
