## Question

**09 §5.2 names only the mint step and `DepositAsset{beneficiary}`, but neither is the complete local surface.** Two further shapes are reachable and are now covered by the implementation, ahead of the text: (a) `WithdrawAsset` fills holding from the sending chain's local sovereign account with **no** mint at all, so a per-account cap refusal on the subsequent deposit strands the holding in a remote-keyed trap; (b) `DepositReserveAsset{dest}` credits `dest`'s local sovereign account *before* sending onward, making it a second metered deposit leg that `DepositAsset` alone does not describe. Both were found by adversarial review of the SQ-129 barrier and both are now bound (`runtime/bleavit-xcm/src/barrier.rs`), with the bound computed over the whole holding rather than the minted amount. `TransferReserveAsset` is deliberately excluded — it moves value through `transfer_asset`, which `CappedInflows` does not meter, so it can never produce a cap refusal. Should §5.2 be amended to name "every locally-metered deposit leg" and "every local USDC holding source" rather than the two specific instructions?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (ratified as implemented)** — §5.2 now binds **every local USDC holding source** and **every locally-metered deposit leg** rather than naming `WithdrawAsset`'s absent mint and `DepositAsset{beneficiary}` alone, with the bound computed over the whole holding. `DepositReserveAsset{dest}`'s local sovereign credit is named as the second deposit leg, and `TransferReserveAsset`'s deliberate exclusion is recorded with its reason (it moves value through `transfer_asset`, which the capped transactor never meters, so it cannot produce a cap refusal). The implementation was already stricter than the text.
