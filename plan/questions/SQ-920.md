## Question

**The local index's disclosure surface has no 11 §11.2 inventory row, so its placement is this client's choice rather than the specification's.** 10 §6.3 obliges the client to render coverage, holes and their provenance; §6.5 obliges *"N events pending decoder"*; §9.2 obliges the downsampled label. All three are obligations on the **client**, and 11 §11.2's inventory — the one list `app/tests/screens` binds in both directions, and whose constraint 1 requires every screen to be *present, reachable and complete* — contains no row that owns them. F25 therefore renders the boot disclosure as shell furniture on every route, which is what 10 §3.1 makes it by placing `StorageOpen` in the boot machine rather than behind a screen, and deliberately mints no S-number: inventing one would put a screen in the client that no document lists, and the inventory test would then be binding the client to itself. The read-path half has the same gap one level down — §6.3 says *"charts render holes as visible gaps with an explainer"* and no inventory row owns the explainer. **Ruling needed:** does §11.2 gain a row for the local index's own state, do §11.12's E3/E8/E9 rows own the placement as a global surface, or is it left to each screen that reads history?

## Status

**open — raised by F25.** No unsafe direction while it stands: the surface renders on every route, which is the superset of any placement a ruling could choose, and it states only facts about local storage. Filed to **B7**
