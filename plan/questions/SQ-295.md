## Question

**07 §5.4's "the only admissible call is `oracle.adjudicate`" is false in the deployed runtime.** The `OracleResolution` origin also gates `resolve_challenge` on **both** registry instances — `runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:3837` (`AdjudicationOrigin`) vs `:3941` (`ResolutionAuthority`, inside `registry_config!`, applied at `:3955`/`:3956`), both resolving to `pallet_origins::EnsureOracleResolution`. The track admits three calls with incompatible shapes (`adjudicate` carries `value`/`spec_version`; `resolve_challenge` carries only `uphold: bool`). Either enumerate the registry calls in §5.4 or route registry resolution off the track. Deliberately **not** doc-trued in B3: enumerating them would legitimize SQ-294's unconstrained call.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-25 (batch X) — doc-trued, and the deferral reason is now discharged.** The row deliberately refused to enumerate the registry calls in §5.4 because doing so "would legitimize SQ-294's unconstrained call". SQ-294 lands with it and constrains that call, so enumeration is now a disclosure rather than a widening. **Ruled: §5.4's "only" scopes to the oracle reporting game, not to the track.** The `OracleResolution` track carries two distinct disputes with deliberately different shapes — `oracle.adjudicate` (grid-bounded value, round ladder, `(component, epoch, spec_version)` key) and §7's `registry.resolve_challenge` (a `uphold` verdict plus the committed evidence hash, one round, no recomputable quantity). Routing the registry off the track was considered and rejected: it would need a new custom origin and a new 06 §3.1 variant for a call whose electorate, thresholds and snapshot semantics are the ones the oracle track already provides, and 06 §2.3's pre-cohort conviction snapshot is exactly the protection a registry verdict on a money-settling epoch needs. Both calls are now enumerated in 06 §3.2's `OracleResolution` column with the constraint note, and §5.4 states the scoping explicitly; a values-track filter MUST admit exactly these two. The classifier half shipped 2026-07-21 and its regression (`registry_resolve_challenge_carries_its_real_oracle_resolution_authority`) still pins the runtime side.
