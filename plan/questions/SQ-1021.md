## Question

`operatorRowsFor(instance)` has no production caller and `operatorGate` always resolves the incident-instance row, so the gate cannot detect a milestone filing gated on incident surfaces: a preparation built against `MilestoneRegistry.Filings` would be verified against `IncidentRegistry.Filings` and pass. `OperatorSurfaceCall` is instance-free by construction (`registry.file` / `registry.challenge`), which is why the binding cannot be made. Should `OperatorSurfaceCall` carry the instance, so the call-to-row map names the allocator the filing really acts on?
