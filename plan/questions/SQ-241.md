## Question

**02 §12 `ReleaseChannel` reserved flag bits under a future schema bump.** 02 §12 freezes bits 0–2 and says "others reserved zero", while allowing future fields to be appended beyond offset 168 with a schema bump. May bits 3–31 of `flags` ever become non-zero under `schema > 1`, or are they frozen zero forever? Decides whether frozen-layout readers must hard-reject (O5's current behavior — isolated so only the ReleaseChannel metric family degrades) or mask reserved bits.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B5 ratification; settled in doc 12 — no 02 edit, no contract bump).** 02 §12 is already complete on the writer side (bits 0–2 fixed, bits 3–31 reserved zero, extension only by appending beyond offset 168 under a schema bump); what it never stated is **reader** behaviour on a violation, and reader obligations are doc 12's subject matter — so the ruling lands in 12 §3.1 and `INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` stays at v5. Substance: read together, those two 02 rules make the reserved bits **frozen zero for the life of the layout** — a future flag is a new appended field, never a redefinition of bits 3–31 — so a non-zero reserved bit is a record no conforming writer produced. Readers therefore **reject rather than mask** (masking would silently accept an unauthenticated writer's record), rejection MUST surface as degraded/unknown and never as healthy (for §3.2 neither "no newer release" nor "no security flag"; for §6.3 the series go absent, never zero), and readers MUST accept values **longer** than 168 bytes — which the shipped O5 reader already does. Nuance recorded: the degraded family also includes `bleavit_chain_pending_upgrade_age_blocks`.
