## Question

The `Rounds` bound is stated three ways — 07 §13 "≤ 16 × 4 = 64", 02 §3 `open_oracle_rounds → BoundedVec<_, ConstU32<192>>`, 13 §4 "16 × 4 × 3". With per-version (triple-key) games ~2 versions can overlap a `(component, epoch)` across a MetricSpec activation boundary, so live rounds can exceed the core's `MAX_ROUNDS = 64` (the 65th `report` hits `RoundLimit`). Which figure is normative, and must `MAX_ROUNDS` rise?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (user-delegated): live-rounds bound = 16 components × 4 epochs × 2 concurrent versions = **128** (one live round per game; the 3-round ladder is sequential within a game). `MAX_ROUNDS`/`MAX_COMPONENT_VALUES` 64→128; 02 §3's cap of 192 already accommodates (no 02 change). 13 §4 amended.
