## Question

Coretime renewal quotes carry no validity interval and cannot lapse on their own; the bounded quote slot (8) could be clogged by periods a keeper never renewed. A9 adds `prune_coretime_quote` (B4-driven) and rejects zero-price quotes, but the validity-window semantics (when does an open quote expire?) are 09 §4/§6 / B4 XCM territory.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (triage sweep — code-verified).** B12 supplied the missing validity semantics: inclusive-TTL freshness via `ops.ct_quote_ttl`, in-place supersession, and expiry-only permissionless `prune_coretime_quote` — so an open quote lapses on its own and the bounded slot set cannot clog. Rides the SQ-245/SQ-246 rulings (2026-07-18).
