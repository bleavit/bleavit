## Question

**Is the INSURANCE account inside NAV?** 08 §1.1 lists INSURANCE among the treasury's lines ("inflow-only except by TREASURY decision"), but `BudgetLine` has no `Insurance` variant, no treasury call spends it, and 13 §4's line enumeration omits it. Consequently `NavView.total` (treasury-core's own arithmetic) **excludes** the insurance custody while `NavView.insurance` reports it — the 02 §4 field set reads as an account decomposition of `total` (11 §11.8.3 renders it as such) but does not sum to it. Excluding it understates NAV, which is the conservative/fail-static direction for spending gates, so B2 ships that and does not touch A9's solvency math. If insurance is in NAV, A9 needs a `BudgetLine::Insurance` + custody sync; if not, 08 §1.2 should say so and 11 §11.8.3's decomposition should exclude it.

## Status
