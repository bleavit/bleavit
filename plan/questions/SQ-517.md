## Question

**Is the whole permissionless keeper-crank family in scope of `PB-LEDGER-FREEZE`?** Raised by the Track E spec audit as a *major*, and filed rather than coded because the two texts genuinely disagree and the answer moves pre-existing behavior. 06 §6.3's Effect freezes "**all** ledger calls … and all market calls … both inflow and outflow, because during an unattributed drift either direction can be the exploit", and its live-list (reconciliation crank, guardian calls, values referenda, oracle/dispute calls) names none of the cranks; 03 §5.4 gives `sweep_redemption_fees` preconditions "**none**". **My analysis, for whoever rules.** The substantive case for freezing is strong: `sweep_redemption_fees` rests on L-7's premise that the accrual is *surplus above* the maintained liability, and a freeze is admissible exactly when the reconciliation crank has proved `liability > custody` — so the premise is false precisely when the crank would run, and the sweep removes real backing. `sweep_revenue` likewise redeems sovereign escrow out to `POL`/`MAIN`. Neither is a settlement call, so §6.3's carve-out ("a freeze must not strand a terminal vault transition") does not reach them: both run strictly *after* terminality, and freezing them defers cleanup rather than stranding anything — POL merely stays committed, which is fail-static. **But it is not this milestone's call to make.** `pallets/market/src/tests.rs::ledger_freeze_blocks_trading_but_not_recovery_and_renews_once` *deliberately* asserts that `sweep_revenue` and `reap` succeed under an active freeze — a tested posture predating Track E (B10/E1) — and `sweep_dust`/`sweep_dust_baseline`/`reap` are unfrozen on the same reading, so a ruling has to settle the family, not two calls. A revenue-instruments PR is the wrong place to reverse an emergency-brake semantic

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-29 (milestone E4) — both Track-E cranks are frozen; 06 §6.3, 03 §5.4/L-7 and 04 §2 amended to say so.** The ruling needed no values judgement in the end, because L-7 supplies a proof rather than a preference: its bound is `RedemptionFeesAccrued ≤ balance(sovereign) − TotalEscrowed − held_deposits − min_balance`, and the I-4 drift flag — the *sole* admission condition for `PB-LEDGER-FREEZE` — asserts exactly `TotalEscrowed + held_deposits > balance(sovereign)`. The bound is therefore **negative under precisely the state that authorizes a freeze**, so "moves surplus, never escrow" is a theorem about the healthy state and not a property of the crank, and `Preservation::Preserve` does not supply the missing guarantee (it protects `min_balance` and is indifferent to escrow). `sweep_revenue` fails for the sibling reason: its fee leg redeems through the ledger's *internal* path, which carries no `Frozen` check, so it would collect the protocol's own claim out of a short sovereign while every claimant's `redeem` is refused. Freezing both **strands nothing** — neither effects a terminal transition, both are retryable, and the value stays fully collateralized in place — which is what distinguishes them from the settlement calls 06 §6.3 deliberately leaves live; the cost is an NAV-recognition delay bounded by the freeze's own ≤ 28-day ceiling. The guard sits **after** `sweep_revenue`'s idempotence early-return, so a freeze never retroactively changes what an already-swept book answers (asserted, not just commented). `reap` is delayed transitively and inertly (it discards only worthless residue); `sweep_dust*` stays live and unchanged — it is the terminal-vault cleanup the settlement carve-out covers, its residue is a liability transfer to INSURANCE rather than a treasury payout, and it is already behind a one-year archive delay. Both new tests are mutation-proven: with the guards removed each fails on its own `Frozen` assertion. **The superseded reading, kept because the process lesson is the transferable part:** I reverted a correct code change because `ledger_freeze_blocks_trading_but_not_recovery_and_renews_once` appeared to assert *deliberately* that `sweep_revenue` survives a freeze, "a tested posture predating Track E". Both halves are false: `git log -S` shows the `sweep()` helper and its call inside that test were added **today, by E1 (`3f716ca`)**, as mechanical plumbing so the test still passed once `reap` gained its swept-marker precondition — not as a ruling, and not predating anything. No Decision-log entry rules on freeze scope for cranks. So 06 §6.3 governs unopposed: "**all** ledger calls … and all market calls error `Frozen`", with an enumerated live-list (reconciliation crank, guardian calls, values referenda, oracle/dispute calls) that excludes both, and a settlement carve-out that cannot reach them because they run strictly *after* terminality. **Narrowed scope:** the two Track-E cranks (`sweep_revenue`, `sweep_redemption_fees`) are this PR's to fix. `reap` and `sweep_dust`/`sweep_dust_baseline` are pre-existing behaviour from B1b and stay here — though note the coupling, which makes the question less urgent than it looks: with `sweep_revenue` frozen, `reap` cannot proceed anyway, since it requires the swept marker
