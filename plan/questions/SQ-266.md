## Question

**"Effective POL vs floor" granularity (12 §6.3 Liquidity-floors row).** 08 §4.3 mandates the Baseline line MUST NOT compete with proposal subsidies, yet a summed comparison lets `POL_BASELINE` overfunding mask `POL` underfunding (and vice versa). B13 ruling (this session, decide-yourself delegation): the two series carry a `component` label (`pol` = Pol line vs Σ live-book commitments; `baseline` = PolBaseline line vs the standing next-Baseline `seed_headroom(pol.b_baseline)`) so the rule expr compares per component and masking is impossible. Whether doc 05's per-book "undisturbed" check additionally needs per-book telemetry granularity is left open.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B6): per-component comparison ratified in 12 §6.3 (match on the label, never aggregate). The open per-book half is ruled out — 05 §5.2's "POL undisturbed" is an on-chain decision-grading predicate, not a monitoring threshold
