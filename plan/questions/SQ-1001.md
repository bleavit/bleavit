## Question

**10 §9 publishes exactly one per-row byte model and `proposalsArchive` is the one table it does not cover.** §9.1 states *"~120 B effective per row (Dexie overhead included)"* and labels it a modelling assumption; §9.2 charges daily candles at *"`books × 120 B/day`"* and events at the same figure (*"a hundred attributed rows a day costs ~12 KB/day"*). The two tables §9 does **not** model are the two the client measures instead — `metadataCache` rows carry their own `bytes` and §6.5's raw blobs are weighed by `pendingRawBytes` — leaving `proposalsArchive`, whose rows §9.2 creates by compaction (*"`events` for settled+reaped proposals → compacted into `proposalsArchive` summaries"*) and whose size is dominated by a `summary` string the client writes and nothing bounds. The client charges it §9's single published figure, which keeps compaction monotone (many event rows become one archive row, so the measured events share cannot rise when the ladder runs) but under-states the share if summaries grow, and the events share is the one §9.2 calls *"the binding constraint"*. §9 should either publish a figure for an archive row or bound the summary; a bound is the safer of the two, because a modelled size for an unbounded string is a measurement of nothing.
