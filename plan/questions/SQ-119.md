## Question

08 §1.4's event list writes fields where they exist (`KeeperBudgetExhausted { epoch, spent }`) but lists `KeeperBudgetLow` bare; A9 declared (and B9 emits) `KeeperBudgetLow { remaining: Balance }`. Does the bare listing freeze a field-less event, or may it carry the diagnostic payload? Keep `remaining` (useful, pre-existing A9 surface, 02 freezes neither keeper event) and amend 08 §1.4 at the next 08 touch, or drop the field?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** `remaining` kept. 02 freezes neither keeper event — 02 §6's frozen event schema has no `pallet-futarchy-treasury` row at all — so this was never a contract question. 08 §1.4 now writes `KeeperBudgetLow { remaining: Balance }` with its semantics (metered budget left when the 80 % threshold is crossed) and records that both keeper events are diagnostic, read by no on-chain decision, and amendable in 08 without a contract change. Consumers already depend on the field (`deploy/runbooks/RB-KEEPER.md:74-75`).
