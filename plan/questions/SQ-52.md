## Question

Are per-line per-epoch budgets (`keeper.budget_epoch`, `pol.budget_epoch`, `ops.*`) enforced at `fund_budget_line`, at `spend`, or by each line's consuming mechanism (keeper rebates B9, POL seeding A3/A8)? A9's `fund_budget_line` accepts any amount ≤ `MAIN` (an allocation decision), relying on the consumers to enforce per-epoch spend.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** 08 §1.1 gained a normative *Where per-line budgets bind* paragraph: `fund_budget_line` is an allocation act under a passed TREASURY decision, deliberately unmetered beyond `MAIN` solvency and the §1.4 custody sync; each line's per-epoch budget is enforced by its consuming mechanism (keeper meter for `KEEPER`, shrink-to-fit for `POL`), never at funding. The design is safe — every debit is bounded by the line balance and the §1.3 rolling meters, so no unbacked claim is reachable. Surfaced while verifying: `ORACLE`, `REWARDS` and `ops.collators` have concrete normative budgets with **no consumer implementation at all** (proposer rewards and `collator.comp_epoch` are unpaid) and no owning milestone — logged as SQ-298.
