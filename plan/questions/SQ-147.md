## Question

**What mechanism satisfies 15 §4.5's "CI gates on > 10 % weight regressions"?** B5 implements a deterministic committed-artifact diff gate (worst-case totals vs the merge-base) — regressions are only visible when weight files are regenerated; nothing forces regeneration when benchmarked code changes (the `benchmark-smoke` job proves executability, not calibration). Is the diff gate sufficient, or must CI force regeneration-and-compare on benchmarked-source changes (machine-noise caveats apply)?
