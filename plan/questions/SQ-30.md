## Question

05 §4.6 requires p5/p95 winsorization over the 12-point trailing sample but names no percentile-estimator family; conventional estimators produce different bounds, conflicting with 15 §4.4's bit-identity mandate. Which estimator is normative? The model currently uses inclusive `(n−1)·p` linear interpolation behind a `SPEC-NOTE`; the convention must be pinned before `pallet-welfare` implements normalization.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (same batch): inclusive linear interpolation ("type-7": rank `1 + f·(n−1)` on the ascending sample, interpolated between bracketing order statistics) is normative — 05 §4.6 now states it, evaluated on-chain on the FixedU64 1e9 grid with the interpolation product rounded down per §4.4; never vacuous on the 12-element sample (nearest-rank would degenerate to min/max, explicitly not meant). Matches the model's implemented estimator — its SPEC-NOTE can drop at next touch
