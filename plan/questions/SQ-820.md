## Question

**10 §9.2 and §6.3 have no vocabulary for a tier emptied outright, which is why a repair's own record could be written to a channel nothing reads.** §9.2 offers *"downsampled"* — *"an evicted range becomes a labelled 'downsampled' range, not a hole, and never a silent splice"* — and it says what it means: the resolution **survives**, one rung coarser, which is why `DownsampledRange.resolution` cannot be `'raw'`. §6.3 offers *"hole"*, which means nobody ingested these blocks. A schema migration, `FE-IDX-001`'s partial cousins and any future repair produce a **third** state — *ingested, still covered, no longer held at any resolution* — and neither term describes it: labelling it *downsampled* claims detail that is gone, and turning it into a hole claims the blocks were never seen. SQ-780 asks **whether** the no-splice clause binds a deletion outside the ladder; it does not ask **which label** applies when nothing coarser survives, and that gap is exactly why F8's `ChartDiscardRecord` was invented as a third, initially unread channel. **Ruling needed:** does §9.2 gain a third label for a tier emptied outright, and if so what may a surface say about those blocks?
