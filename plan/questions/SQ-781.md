## Question

**`candles1h` is one key space for two different price series, and 10 §7 publishes no key that separates them.** §9.1's scan-time aggregate writes `[bookId, sourceKey, openAt]` rows folded from `Traded.p_after`; §9.2's ladder folds `priceSamples` — `Observed` prices — into the **same table under the same key**, and `mergeCandle` then merges the two bar by bar. SQ-762's disposal (*"one price series rather than a merged one"*) therefore holds inside `tradeCandles` and is undone at the storage layer, where nothing distinguishes a trade-derived bar from an observation-derived one. It is latent only because nothing writes `priceSamples` yet (SQ-782). **Ruling needed with SQ-762:** is `candles1h` one series per book or one per price source — and if the latter, does the key gain a series discriminator or do the two tiers get separate tables?
