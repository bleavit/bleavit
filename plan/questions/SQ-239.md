## Question

12 §6.3 binds alert rows to runbooks but assigns no owner per row; §6.1 assigns owners per service. O4 derived a default rule (Monitoring coordinator as first responder for protocol-domain alerts, dedicated §6.1 program rows keep their owner; rationale in `deploy/runbooks/README.md`) — is that the intended reading, or should 12 bind an owner/funding line per alert row?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B5 ratification; derived rule ratified and extended to the case it did not cover).** 12 §6.3 now states it normatively: the table assigns runbooks, not owners; an alert's owner is the owner of its §6.1 row; protocol-domain alerts have no dedicated program row and fall to the **Monitoring coordinator** as first responder under §6.1's Monitoring row. The row's claim that "the checker enforces" the rule is corrected — it enforces §6.1 **row-pair membership** only, and nothing forces a protocol-domain runbook to the monitoring coordinator. Verification also surfaced a case the derived rule left undetermined and the ruling now fixes: RB-BOOTNODE spans two §6.3 domains mapping to *different* §6.1 rows (Bootnodes → Bootnode program coordinator; Served-state window → Infrastructure coordinator), so a spanning runbook takes its primary row's owner and MUST name the other in its escalation path.
