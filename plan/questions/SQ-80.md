## Question

06 §3.2 names a `welfare.activate_spec` call on the metric track, but `welfare-core` models MetricSpec activation **implicitly** via `activation_epoch ≥ current+2` (register-time) with no separate activation state, so A7 implements `register_spec` only.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — ruled: **implicit scheduled activation**. `activate_spec` struck from 06 §2.1/§3.2; 05 §4.4 pins that registration *is* the activation decision (immutable stored `activation_epoch`, no pending state, fail-closed on tie, genesis lead of 1 because epoch 0 is the pre-launch sentinel). Rationale: a two-phase model creates a registered-but-never-activated liveness trap; a stored future epoch cannot fail to arrive.
