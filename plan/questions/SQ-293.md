## Question

**Oracle bonds and stakes are numeric bookkeeping only — no value is ever escrowed, forfeited or distributed.** Confirmed by two independent verification passes. `pallet_oracle::Config` exposes no `Currency`/fungible/hold/collateral type at all (`pallets/oracle/src/lib.rs:220`); `report` and `challenge` compute a bond, store it in `RoundState.bond`/`cumulative_*_bond` and emit an event without touching balances (`crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs:470`, `:525`); escalation just doubles the number (`:716`); `adjudicate` records an offense and emits `Adjudicated` with no transfer (`:821`); the "slash" only emits `ReporterSlashed` (`:1164`), and watchtower ejection likewise moves nothing (`:642`); settlement drops the round and the stacks vanish with it (`:1040`). Reporter stake (§3), watchtower stake (§4) and the §5.5 40/60 forfeiture are therefore all notional, and a losing **challenger** has no penalty path whatsoever. The pallet admits the gap in its own header — "Economic custody … is a B-track runtime concern" (`pallets/oracle/src/lib.rs:61`) — but it was never wired. §13's `Σ held bonds == Σ open-round bonds × sides` try-state check is consequently absent (`pallets/oracle/src/lib.rs:1310`; `crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs:968`). `pallet-registry` is the counterexample showing this is unfinished integration rather than a frame-free-core limitation: it holds real USDC via `Config::Collateral` → `ForeignAssets`, transfers bonds into its sovereign account and pays winner + INSURANCE (`pallets/registry/src/lib.rs:193`, `:849`, `:864`) — now I-28. **Currently masked, not mitigated:** production returns `Balance::MAX` for `StakeAtRisk` while SQ-174 is open, so `round_bond`'s checked multiplication overflows and refuses any exposure-bearing report — the game fails closed by accident of an unrelated open row, and any round that *does* open has no custody.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (stale — verified against HEAD)** — every claim in the row is false at HEAD. Registration stakes, reporter/watchtower stakes and round bonds are physically held and released or slashed; terminal settlement pays the honest party and routes the 40/60 remainder to INSURANCE; `try_state` checks custody balances against active liabilities and cumulative bonds — the §13 check the row called absent; and reporter/watchtower ejection carries an explicit custody disposition. The A12 kernel slice recorded here in 2026-07-23 completed the work; the row was never closed out. `StakeAtRisk` returning `Balance::MAX` remains a separate defect (SQ-174).
