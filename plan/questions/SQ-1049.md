## Question

**`MaxScoredMarketsPerAccount` is anchored to `MaxLiveMarkets` alone, but an account can hold positions in more live books than that.** The bound is 196, from `MaxLiveMarkets`. 13 §4 separately carries `MaxLiveExternalMarkets` = 128, and `market.buy` serves `BookKind::External` through the **same** call path and therefore the same accumulator, so the reachable live-book count per account is up to **324**. The error direction is safe — a fill past the bound records no score and never rejects the trade, so the effect is silent under-crediting rather than a hazard — which is why this is a sizing question and not a defect. Does the score bound intend to cover external books, and if so is the anchor `MaxLiveMarkets + MaxLiveExternalMarkets`?

## Status

resolved 2026-08-10 — **neither: external books are not scored at all, and the anchor was right for a reason nobody had written down.** Asked as sizing, it is not. [16](../../docs/architecture/16-hosted-question-service.md) §6.5 states plainly that a client controlling a majority of its own named attestors can move the settled value, declines to repair it, and bounds the residual: *"the blast radius is that question's own escrow, minus forfeited bonds, with Bleavit's ledger instance and every Bleavit market untouched."* Scoring an external book **breaks that stated bound** — the same client would convert an accepted, self-funded residual into a claim on the genesis `incentiv` pot, risk-free at up to `snapshot_bond / rate_headroom` per epoch, defended by neither the bond (it never loses) nor the rate coupling (there is no offsetting loser). Two further grounds agree independently. The pot buys Bleavit's own decision liquidity — 08 §2.6 justifies the program by [05](../../docs/architecture/05-welfare-and-decision-engine.md) §5.2's `L̂` rising, and an external book contributes nothing to `L̂` while its client already pays `svc.fee_bps` for the venue. And rule 3 is **unimplemented** for external books: it needs `settled_value`, an external book settles through the separate service-ledger instance, and §2.6 names no source for it there. Ruled: the accumulator skips `BookKind::External`, decided at the `buy`/`sell` call sites where `book.kind` is already in scope, so the exclusion costs no storage read. `MaxScoredMarketsPerAccount = MaxLiveMarkets` therefore becomes exact rather than coincidentally safe. 08 §2.6, 13 §4 and the design updated; implementation routed to TR5, which already reopens this accumulator
