## Question

08 §6.3's decision-critical tranche list ("decision-window observations, `decide`, `settle_cohort`, `tick`, `snapshot`") is treated as exhaustive by B9, so the unlisted keeper cranks — `welfare.record_daily_gate` (a decision *input*, 05 §4.7), `execution_guard.execute`/`reap_prequeue_ratification` (01 §4.2 names "execution" a keeper crank; arguably decision-critical since it finalizes an adopted decision), `futarchy_treasury.execute_coretime_renewal` (09 §4 "keeper-rebated"), and every reap/dust crank — rebate from the ≤20% **General** tranche. Conservative for the reserved tranche, but confirm none of these belongs in the ≥80% decision-critical reservation.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** 08 §6.3 gained a normative closed-list bullet: exactly the five named crank families draw the ≥ 80 % reservation; every other sanctioned permissionless crank (`execution_guard.execute`, `expire_failed_execution`, `reject_stale`, `welfare.record_daily_gate`, `execute_coretime_renewal`, `prune_coretime_quote`, all reap/dust sweeps) draws the ≤ 20 % general tranche, `ORACLE`-line cranks sit outside the meter entirely (coheres with B3's SQ-116/SQ-121), and privileged non-keeper entry points are unrebated. Row text corrected: `reap_prequeue_ratification` is not a dispatchable at all, but an `EnqueueAuthority`-gated helper. The bullet states honestly that the general tranche is a **partial subsidy by construction** — out-of-window demand exceeds it ~17× — so `execution_guard.execute` and `record_daily_gate` are unrebated in expectation. Promoting those two would cost ≈ 6.2 USDC/epoch against a ≥ 9,600 USDC reservation; left as General (the G-1 direction) and flagged as an available refinement.
