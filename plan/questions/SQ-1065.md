## Question

Configuration enforces three unique display names but neither distinct normalized URL origins nor
operator identities. The integrity verdict fails only when at least two resolutions diverge, while
12 §5.2 says the monitor alerts on any mismatch and §6.3 says 2-of-3. Three aliases can collapse to
one provider, and one compromised gateway is explicitly tested as healthy.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-12.** Gateway configuration requires stable operator IDs and
pairwise-disjoint normalized origins. Any resolver or route-byte dissent is an
integrity failure; majority agreement is used only as a deterministic lag-clock
anchor. Config, monitor, Prometheus threshold and release runbook agree.
