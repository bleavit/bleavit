## Question

**§8.5.2 says the `ops` are *"what layer-3 ingests"*, and nothing ingests them.** The sentence justifies keeping the replay's referential checks for a page. It is not true of this codebase: `mint.ts` produces `coverage` and `balances` rows only, 10 §7 defines no movement table, and no consumer reads a page's `ops` at all. Meanwhile `balances` — the field that **is** minted into layer-3 — has no referential check on a page, so a row may name a vault or a branch the document does not declare. The argument was applied to the right principle and the wrong field. Either §7 gains a movement table, or §8.5.2's sentence takes a different subject and `balances` gains the referential check.
