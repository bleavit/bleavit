## Question

**Is an unfinished spot re-derivation a refusal or a disclosure?** The pass is bounded by a client-side work ceiling, and two live configurations reach it with nothing wrong with the file: a device more than the ceiling behind the document's newest covered block, and a document with more **reachable** covered blocks than one pass will ask about. §8.4 states the depth limit as **disclosed** and gives `FE-PROV-003` three causes that are all statements about the document, so F9 now **admits** on the ceiling with `reach: 'ceiling'` and refuses to badge the rows `sampled` — INV-FE-15's second option (present and labelled) rather than its first (absent with an explanation). That is a choice. The opposite reading is defensible: §8.4 mandates re-derivation *"for the covered blocks that fall inside light-client-reachable depth"* — every one of them — and a pass that stopped early did not do what was mandated. **What §8.4 wants when the client's own work budget stops the walk with no adverse finding is the question**, and a ruling toward refusal restores the deleted `spot-check-incomplete` finding class and its `incomplete-check` remedy (with the *"try again when this device has caught up"* sentence corrected, since it was false for the second configuration). SQ-770 is upstream of this: if *spot* is a **sample**, a bounded pass is the mechanism working as specified and this question dissolves
