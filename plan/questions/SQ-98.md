## Question

Ledger-freeze force-reject: mechanism, reason code, and scope. `epoch-core::tick` force-rejects **every** live non-terminal pre-Executed proposal to `Rejected(ProcessHold)` + vault-void-at-par under `self.ledger_frozen` (`decide_with` likewise returns `ProcessHold`). This is G-1-safe (06 §6.3 "resolve to status quo"; D-1 par-void; the literal T10→T21→measure-REJECT path is unworkable while `market.set_frozen(true)` halts trading), but three texts point elsewhere: (1) 06 §6.3 frames the effect as "decision windows overlapping a freeze **fail decision-grade (staleness)**" — a decide-time `NotDecisionGrade` (T10) scoped to windows that overlap, not a T20 force-reject over all live proposals; (2) 09 §1.2(10) blocks `execute()` during an active PB-LEDGER-FREEZE, implying `Queued` items **wait out** the bounded (≤14+14 d) freeze rather than being destroyed; (3) 05 §1.3's `ProcessHold` producer map lists T20 only as "force-reject under **VOID/stale-epoch**" and a ledger freeze is not obviously a "VOID condition". **Pre-existing** — this behavior predates PR #56; the dead-man fix only *removed* dead-man from the tick force-reject set and deliberately left `ledger_frozen` (the dead-man/ledger asymmetry). Either narrow the trigger to decision-window proposals and let `Queued` items wait, or amend 06 §6.3 / 05 §2.1 to bless a freeze-wide T20 `ProcessHold`+void.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (code fix).** The divergence is closed: `decide_with`'s ledger-freeze arm moved out of the decision chain and now shares `tick`'s exact predicate (`self.ledger_frozen || self.stale_process_hold(pid)`), force-rejecting to `Rejected(ProcessHold)` with a par vault void. Both cranks therefore land on identical ledger state; previously a race decided whether Accept-branch holders recovered ½ (par void) or nothing (Reject-branch resolution). Par void is also the only self-consistent choice: the freeze halts `market.crank_observe`, so `reject_to_measurement` would have sent proposals to await a score the freeze itself prevents producing. `dead_man_armed` and `guards.process_hold` were deliberately left in the decision chain (the 05 §4.8 dead-man asymmetry is intentional). Precedence note: the freeze check now precedes the static guards, so a freeze forgoes the 10 % `!preimage_ok` slash — accepted, and conservative in the G-1 direction (no confiscation during a machine-checked solvency emergency; cf. the SQ-191 ruling that confiscation requires a verified culpable act). Spec trued: 06 §6.3 now states the T20 `ProcessHold` + par-void disposition explicitly — the old "fail decision-grade (staleness)" wording implied `NotDecisionGrade`, which forfeits 10 % of bond under §4(3) and would fine proposers for an operational emergency; 05 §2.1 T20 and §1.3 now name the freeze as a producer and note the tick/decide equivalence. **The sweep *scope* question the row also raised is not settled here** — whether `Queued` items should wait out the bounded freeze per 09 §1.2(10) rather than be force-rejected is split out as **SQ-286**. **Known modelling gap (deliberate, recorded not fixed):** `models/tla/proposal/Proposal.tla` binds T20 to the `Tick` action only, so the amended 05 §2.1 T20 row (which now also names `decide()`) is ahead of the model. Loosening the binding was tried and reverted — the model carries no freeze variable, so the edge is unreachable there, and admitting `Decide` for T20 makes a Decide/Trading→Rejected/ProcessHold step match **both** T10 (`ProcessHold` ∈ `T10Reasons`) and T20, breaking the `Cardinality(matches) = 1` clause of `TransitionTableExhaustive`. Disambiguating requires carrying the vault outcome in the transition record (T20 voids; T10 resolves into measurement), which is a modelling change owed its own pass. The reason for the binding is now commented in the model itself.
