## Question

03 §5.5 enumerates the D-3 `do_*` internal API but names no `create_vault`/`create_baseline_vault` call or its origin, though vaults must exist before POL seeding. Which call creates a vault, and under which authority?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — 03 §5.5 now names `create_vault(pid, spec)` and `create_baseline_vault(epoch)` as the `MarketAuthority`-gated vault-construction pair that runs at book deployment, before POL seeding (`pallets/conditional-ledger/src/lib.rs:1005-1026`).
