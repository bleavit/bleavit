## Question

Execution depth of a Chopsticks "dry run" (15 §4.7: "every upgrade and every playbook, including PB-LEDGER-FREEZE entry/expiry and the coretime exemption"): the producer's per-scenario pass = fork boot from the bound raw spec + byte-exact injected-state verification + two produced blocks + live `:code` binding before/after; the normative .md card sequences remain manual (most card assertions are gated on unwired surface per the chopsticks README matrix). Does that qualify as the suite having "run" for 15 §5, or must card assertions execute before evidence may name the scenario?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (O6).** Verified real *and* worse than stated: `run_chopsticks` attested only boot/injected-state/blocks/`:code` binding, and `--include-gated` was **absent from `report_only_reasons`**, so a release-tier run could emit evidence naming a scenario whose card had never run. Fixed: every scenario card now carries a machine-readable ` ```card-assertions ` block — one entry per numbered step, each with exactly one of `execute` (a program the runner runs over the live Chopsticks JSON-RPC), `blocked_on` (the concrete unwired surface), or `discharged_by: try-state`. The runner parses and executes it, records the `card` check **only** when every assertion ran, and `validate_release_evidence_completeness` now refuses evidence that names any card-requiring scenario lacking it; `validate-environments.py` enforces the block's shape as a structural gate. All 10 cards encoded from their own prose and NOTE(B7) blockers; each still carries `blocked_on` steps, so the suites stay gated — but mechanically, and the gate expires on its own once the surface lands. 17 new tests.
