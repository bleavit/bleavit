## Question

**`gate.v_min` is class-dependent but reading rule 6 omits it from the four-suffix enumeration.** A single Balance key cannot represent its class-derived default. A8 retains four live bounded suffix records; S3's strict extractor now mirrors that runtime reality and classifies all four as genesis-seeded `param-bounds`, with generated amendment-bound coverage and no waiver. Amend rule 6 or rule that it is one key.
