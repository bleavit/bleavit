## Question

**Nothing audits `app/`'s two lockfiles, and Dependabot is currently reporting two advisories in exactly them.** `tools/ci/supply-chain-gates.sh` audits precisely two files — the root `Cargo.lock` and `keeper/Cargo.lock` (lines 85–86, and its `assert_lockfile` calls) — because `app/` keeps its own cargo workspace, excluded from the root one, and its JavaScript tree is pnpm rather than cargo. So `app/Cargo.lock` and `app/pnpm-lock.yaml` are outside every supply-chain gate this repository runs, and the SQ-219 argument that motivated the GHSA-only leg applies here with more force: that leg exists because RustSec is a strict subset of the GitHub Advisory DB, and here there is **no** leg at all. Found 2026-08-07 not by the gate but by a `git push` printing GitHub's own notice, which is the tell — a coverage hole reports as silence. The two live alerts are `@opentelemetry/core` GHSA-8988-4f7v-96qf (patched 2.8.0, arrives under Lighthouse in `tools/render-budget`) and `glib` GHSA-wrw7-89jp-8q8g (patched 0.20.0, arrives under the Tauri shell). **Neither containment claim is verified** — that is the first thing the answer owes, because both packages are confined by workspace membership rather than by anything mechanical, and 10 §10.1's firewall is about *client* packages, not about what a build tool may drag in. Three questions: does the gate grow to cover `app/`'s cargo workspace on the same pinned `cargo-audit` + `osv-scanner` pair; what audits `app/pnpm-lock.yaml`, which nothing does today; and does a build-time-only dependency get an annotated exception like the root's stable2606 set, or must it be patched like a shipped one?
