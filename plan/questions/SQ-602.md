## Question

**The insurance target `T_ins` has no published surface, so 11 §11.8.3's insurance classification cannot be a chain read.** §11.8.3 requires `INSURANCE` presented as a sized reserve against its target and never as income. `NavView` (02 §4) carries `insurance` and no target, and `insurance_target()` is a pallet-internal helper rather than a `FutarchyApi` method — so any client that classifies must fabricate a `Verified<T>` for a derived value, which is the INV-FE-1 defect `claimableNow` was repaired for. Until a surface publishes it the client must carry an `unestablished` arm and refuse to classify, which is fail-closed but leaves §11.8.3's requirement unmet rather than met conservatively. Appending `insurance_target` to `NavView` is lawful under 02 §13 rule 3 (view types may gain trailing fields) and would cost one contract bump

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07 (contract v29).** `insurance_target` appends to `NavView` as a **trailing** field under 02 §13 rule 3, with the existing field order untouched — reordering would forfeit the rule that makes the append lawful
