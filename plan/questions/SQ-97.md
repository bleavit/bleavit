## Question

09 §1.2(5) states the execute-time attestation check as record "presence/unrevoked/unchallenged", but I-19 / 06 §7's bonded 2-of-3-over-≥3-active-members regime implies **live quorum**; a post-queue signer recall/ejection drops effective quorum without revoking the record. A11 fails closed by re-checking `has_quorum` at execute too. Confirm the intended post-queue quorum-loss semantics at a future 09/06 touch.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-23 (B19, contract v10).** Queue admission still requires the live roster quorum. Execute/recovery uses `has_record_quorum`: only matured, unrevoked, settled records count, and for-cause departure atomically revokes the affected records before the live member is removed.
