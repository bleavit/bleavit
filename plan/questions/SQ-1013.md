## Question

**11 §11.9.1's Asset Hub compat row needs two smoldot chain handles and no document says so.** §11.9.1 requires both an AH balance read and an AH descriptor-compatibility verdict. PAPI's smoldot provider binds one `Chain` to one JSON-RPC connection and removes the chain on disconnect, so the two obligations cannot share a handle. 10 §9.3/§9.4 budget three chain specs and the memory line assumes one Asset Hub client. The implementation attaches a transient second handle relying on smoldot's internal de-duplication — so a lookup rather than a second sync, and **unmeasured**. Either §9.4 names the cost or §11.9.1 says the probe and the read share one connection by some means this client does not have.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-08 (R-1) — §9.4 names the cost, and it is not the cost the question expected.** Both halves were read off `smoldot@3.3.2`'s own published contract in the lockfile-resolved package rather than assumed (V-365, V-366). **First**, the transient handle is a *lookup, not a second sync*: smoldot *"will automatically de-duplicate chains if multiple identical chains are added"*, and callers are *"strongly encouraged to trust smoldot and not attempt to de-duplicate chains yourself, as determining whether two chains are identical is complicated and might have security implications"*. So §11.9.1's *"same smoldot instance, additional chain"* already covers the probe's handle, and the alternative the row floated — one connection serving both obligations — is the design the vendor warns against. **Second**, the cost the row called *"unmeasured"* is **CPU, not memory**: *"Smoldot tries to distribute CPU resources equally between all active `Chain` objects of the same client."* One probed chain halves the reader's share for as long as the handle lives. §9.4's memory rows are therefore right as they stand and the **mobile CPU** row was the silent one; it now carries a named compatibility-probe line, its steady-state figure is the no-probe figure, and the obligation is that a probe handle is released when its verdict is produced. 10 §4.1 states both rules normatively, including the one the connector already implements — releasing a probe stops **every** chain it added, since a factory adding relay + para and releasing one leaks the other and §3.2 re-probes on every `CodeUpdated`. Only the measured share stays open, under FE-P4 with the other CPU/memory rows
