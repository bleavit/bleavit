## Question

**Multi-limit 13 §2 rows: which enforcement unit is normative?** 15 §4.6 says "for **every** rate limit, meter, and bound, one integration test…" while I-22 (15 §1) says "a **registry key** without a matching test fails CI" — for a §2 row declaring several independent numeric limits (`MinTrade`/`MaxTrade`; `prop.max_calls`/`max_bytes`/`max_weight`; PB-LEDGER-FREEZE's duration + one-renewal caps; the registry/attestor floors row; the crank-batch row) the two sentences imply different granularity (per-bound vs per-row). S3 adopted **per-bound** (each constituent materializes as its own coverage key with its own error/behavior token; the I-4 drift-flag admissibility clause of PB-LEDGER-FREEZE stayed a gate, not a bound). Confirm per-bound as the normative unit or amend one of the two sentences.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B6): per-bound ratified as the enforcement unit in 15 §4.6 and the I-22 row; the PB-LEDGER-FREEZE I-4 drift-flag clause named normatively as a gate, not a bound
