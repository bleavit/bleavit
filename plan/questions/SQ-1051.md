## Question

**The score mixes plain USDC with branch-conditional USDC and credits at par, and 15 §4.1 states the anti-farm property "in USDC" without saying which.** A buyer spends plain USDC; a seller is paid in branch USDC, which merges to plain only up to their mirror balance. Rule 2 credits proceeds at par regardless. Before settlement a branch unit is worth strictly less than par, so a score summed across branches is not obviously denominated in one thing. This decides whether the 15 §4.1 property TR8 must prove at 10⁶ cases is **well-defined across branches** — an invariant stated in ambiguous units cannot be proven in any. Raised by the TR4 implementer as the deepest of its four questions, and it is.

## Status

resolved 2026-08-10 — **plain USDC, and asking the question exposed a defect rather than an ambiguity.** A conditional market pays a buyer in two currencies and only one survives: the wrapper splits `cost + fee` of plain USDC across both branches, so the buyer leaves holding the scalar position **and `cost` of mirror-branch branch-USDC**, while a sale is paid in traded-branch branch-USDC. `received` and the mirror leg are worth par in exactly opposite states, so the single rule `received − spent` had to be wrong in one of them — and it was wrong in the common one. [04](../../docs/architecture/04-markets-and-pricing.md) §6.2 guarantees as **G-3** that a buyer in the losing branch *"loses only fees when its branch is annulled"*, because the mirror leg redeems at par. The old rule scored that buyer at `−(cost + fee)`: the whole notional, against a realized loss of `fee`, about 1/333 of it at the `mkt.fee` default. Roughly half of decision-market trading sits in the branch that does not realize, so **the program would have debited the bonds of accurate traders** for positions the protocol had already made whole — and every conservation identity and per-call assertion holds while it happens. Ruled: `MarketScore` gains **`mirror_principal`** (the sum of book-side `cost` over buys) and rule 4 gains three arms — realized `received − spent`; annulled `mirror_principal − spent`, discarding every `received` credit because those are annulled-branch units worth nothing; proposal VOID drops the entry at zero, since no forecast was tested and §6.2 gives VOID its own delta. The annulled arm is **exact**, not conservative: it reduces to `−Σfee`, which is G-3 restated. 08 §2.6 and 15 §4.1 amended (the suite must generate all three arms and assert the annulled case at exactly `−Σfee`, since `−(cost + fee)` is also negative); code routed to TR5 obligation 7
