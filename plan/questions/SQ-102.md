## Question

`INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` (=3) is bound to `RuntimeVersion.transaction_version`. 02 §13(2) requires the contract version as a primitives re-export + constitution metadata constant (both present), but overloading the SDK's extrinsic-**format** version with contract semantics means a purely additive contract bump would spuriously signal an extrinsic-format break to wallets/tooling. Decouple (`transaction_version` starts at 1 with native semantics)?

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C, contract v6) — **decoupled, in doc and code.** `transaction_version` now carries its own native baseline of 1, the `INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` alias is removed, and the equality test is replaced by one asserting the counters are independent; 02 §13 states the independence normatively. Safe only pre-genesis — the SDK forbids this counter ever decreasing after genesis. **Two row claims corrected:** it is stale at "=3" (the contract was at 5), and `transaction_version` denotes *dispatchable compatibility* embedded in signed-transaction validity, not the extrinsic **format** version.
