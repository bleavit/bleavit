## Question

**10 §3.1 gives `CompatUnavailable` the renderable surface of `WorkerFailed`, but that state is reachable with a perfectly healthy transport.** A probe can fail while verified reads remain available, so borrowing a transport failure's surface may suppress reads the client can still prove. Does *"the renderable surface is `WorkerFailed`'s"* forbid rendering verified reads, or only signing? INV-FE-12 settles the signing half and nothing settles the reading half.
