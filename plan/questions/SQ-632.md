## Question

**§8.4's 400 MB import ceiling is an *input* bound and §9.4 budgets *memory*, and on a phone the two cannot both hold.** §8.4 caps an import at *"≤ 400 MB uncompressed, ≤ 4 M rows, streamed"*. Admission is not a pass over bytes: the file text, `JSON.parse`'s tree, the parsed model and the digest pre-image are resident together, so peak working memory is a multiple of the input — and §9.4 budgets a mobile tab **350 MB steady-state in total**, with §9.2 capping its whole local store at **75 MB**. A 400 MB import therefore cannot fit any mobile budget this specification states, and the way it fails is a dead tab rather than a refusal, which is the one outcome §8.4's quota sentence exists to prevent. F9 ships the conservative correction: the byte bound is a **required** field of the import request with no default, chosen by the caller from its own device budget and **capped** by §8.4's figure (a caller may narrow, never loosen), and the duplicates this client controls are removed (the canonical-form comparison streams, the pin is taken over the bytes already held). The multiple itself is **unmeasured**, and measuring it is the other half of the answer.
