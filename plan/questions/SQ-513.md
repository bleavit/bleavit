## Question

**08 must rule: does the seed-time `POL` line debit replace the NAV commitment obligation, or compose with it?** Raised by the Track E spec audit. 08 §8 step 5 mandates the line debit; §1.2/§3/§4.4 keep "POL commitments of live books" in `obligations()`. The code composes them deliberately, making live-book NAV drag **1.5×** the §3 commitment for a branched proposal and **2×** for the Baseline — a figure no §3/§4.1/§10.1 arithmetic computes. Direction is safe (understatement, fail-static) but it silently tightens §4.4 shrink-to-fit and the §4.2 arming gate. Supersedes and subsumes SQ-510, which described the same defect from the E1 side
