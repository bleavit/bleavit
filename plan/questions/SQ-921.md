## Question

**`FE-IDX-002` has a third candidate emitter, and it is the one whose recovery is unlike the other two.** SQ-604 asks for the code's definition against a **per-range invalidation** — a range the chain disagreed with — and SQ-783 adds F8's **whole-tier migration discard**. `readCoverageRepair`/`sanitizeCoverage` produce a third: a stored coverage entry that no longer parses, dropped on the read path per §6.3's *"corruption of one range invalidates that range, not the index"*. Nothing disagreed and no tier was emptied; a record in IndexedDB stopped being readable, which is 11 E9's IndexedDB-corruption row scoped to one range rather than to the whole database — and E9's recovery is `FE-IDX-001`'s reasoned rebuild, which is not what happens here. The three recoveries are different: an invalidated range is re-fetchable from layer 2 (§6.2), discarded chart depth re-accumulates, and a dropped coverage entry becomes a hole that forward ingestion never closes. 10 §9.4 requires *"fixed user copy + expert detail + documented recovery per code"*, so one code for three failures is one recovery sentence for three. **Ruling needed with SQ-604 and SQ-783:** one code, two, or three?
