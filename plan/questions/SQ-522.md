## Question

**`pallet_epoch` weights understated on `main`, and three milestones of drift had accumulated inside the gate's documented advisory window.** Found while regenerating for SQ-518 at the committed 50x20 fidelity. On the merge base `af2e5a0`, `pallet_welfare.rs` declares `Welfare::Snapshots (r:61 w:60)` while `pallet_epoch.rs` declares `(r:21 w:20)` — the same storage bound, the same runtime, two committed weight files disagreeing. `pallet_epoch`'s benchmark is not seeding a smaller fixture: `seed_welfare_retirement_history` reads `BenchmarkSnapshots::iter_keys().count()`, which the assembled-runtime helper populates *at its retained capacity*, so the fixture scales with `MAX_SNAPSHOTS` and `r:21` is simply the pre-MAX-01 bound of 20. Regeneration also picks up **A14** (`Welfare::BlockProductionEpoch` and `CollatorAuthorshipEpoch`, `r:0 w:2` each, absent from the committed file) and **E1** (`Constitution::Params` 41 -> 43 and 34 -> 36 for the `ledger.rdm_fee`/`ledger.min_split` overlay, plus `RedemptionFeesAccrued`). Direction is **understatement**, the one direction 15 §4.5 says weights must never err in. **Why no per-commit gate saw it, stated from the code rather than the doc:** `regenerate-weights.py` buckets a drift as hard only when `matches or not has_components` — `matches` being run fidelity equal to the committed header's. These epoch functions are component-bearing (`Estimated: 183055 + n * (30754 +/-57)`), and the `benchmark-smoke` job runs `--steps 2 --repeat 1` against a 50x20 header, so every one of these deltas landed in **advisory** and printed without failing. That is the *documented* tradeoff, not a defect in the gate — a 2-point fit can lose a linear term outright, so hard-gating component functions at low fidelity would produce false failures. The consequence is the part worth recording: the hard gate for these is the release-blocking `Component weights at committed fidelity` job in `release.yml`, which runs only at tag time, so this drift would first have surfaced as a **failed release** rather than a failed PR

## Status

**resolved incidentally 2026-07-29 (milestone E4) — the corrected weights ship with SQ-518, and the fix was not optional.** Regenerating `pallet_epoch` for SQ-518's seam necessarily re-measures the whole pallet, so the accumulated drift came with it; excluding it would have meant hand-editing generated weight functions, which the 15 §4.5 generated-weight purity gate forbids outright. `check-weight-regression.py` passes: none of the epoch deltas crossed its 10 % worst-case limit against the merge base, so no acknowledgement was required for them. **What stays open is the question, not this instance** — whether a cheap per-commit run should hard-gate the *storage* dimensions of component-bearing functions at their component high, since reads/writes at a fixed fixture are structural rather than statistical and do not have ref_time's 127 % spread. That is a change to the gate's own design and belongs to whoever revisits SQ-490's regime, not to a revenue-instruments PR
