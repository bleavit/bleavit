## Question

**A proposal has no human-readable label anywhere on this chain, and the client carried a `title` field for one until a decoder had to produce it.** `Proposal<AccountId>` (02 §7.1) and `ProposalSummaryView` (02 §4) both carry `payload_hash` and no free text; 11 §11.2's S2 row lists `Epoch.Proposals`, `proposal_summaries()`, `decision_stats()`, `Preimage.PreimageFor` and `Referenda.ReferendumInfoFor`, none of which is a source for a name. So S2's list and detail identify a proposal by its id and its commitment, which is true and is also the least legible thing a governance screen could show. Three dispositions, and only the last is free: the payload preimage decodes to calls, so a client MAY render a **derived** description of what a proposal does (a `derived-local` value computed from a finalized read, with no new chain surface — but a decoder per call family, and a wrong summary on a governance screen is worse than none); or 02 gains a bounded label field on `Proposal`, which is a contract bump with joint sign-off and a genuine spam surface, since the field is attacker-controlled text on a screen; or the spec states plainly that a proposal is identified by its commitment and that legibility is the caller's problem, which is what the client now implements. **Nothing ships wrong today** — F7b's repair renders the `payload_hash` under an `Identifier`, so no invented value reaches a badge
