## Question

04 §4 ulp semantics: is "each `exp2`/`log2` call ≤ 2 ulp of 64.64 (≈1.1e-19 relative)" 2 raw 2⁻⁶⁴ units absolute or ~2⁻⁶³ relative? The readings coincide only in [1,2); exp2 with integer part ≥ 1 cannot meet raw-absolute after the left shift, and log2 measures 2.64 raw ulp on wide inputs while passing relative. Also: no explicit error bound is stated for the price function (crate test infers 8 raw ulp).

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (user-delegated batch; see Decision log): ulp = 2⁻⁶⁴ fixed; exp2 bound is kernel-absolute ≤ 2 ulp ⇔ full-range relative ≤ 2⁻⁶³ (absolute full-range unattainable and not meant); log2/ln ≤ 2 ulp absolute full-domain; marginal price ≤ 8 ulp absolute added (04 §4); current exp2 (10.11 ulp) and wide-input log2 (2.64 ulp) remain non-compliant — M2 fix stands
