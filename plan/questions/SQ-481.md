## Question

**The pre-mint per-account inflow gate measured the beneficiary cap against the full pre-fee holding, not the post-fee amount `deposit_asset` actually meters, so USDC-fee-paying inbound programs were wrongly rejected.** The barrier bounded each target by `entering_usdc + reclaimed_usdc`, but `CappedInflows::deposit_asset` records only the USDC left in holding after `BuyExecution`/`PayFees`. A program paying its fee in USDC via `PayFees` — or a fee-only program depositing zero — was refused even when the real cumulative meter would stay within `phase3.dep_cap`. Fixed for straight-line execution by subtracting the USDC a single top-level `PayFees` provably commits before every reachable deposit and never refunds (`deposit_fee_reduction` in `barrier.rs`). The subtraction is a conservative *lower* bound on removal: it is omitted for `RefundSurplus`, a deposit before the fee, a non-USDC fee, and — after the SQ-482 follow-up — any local deposit inside `SetErrorHandler`/`SetAppendix`, because fallback control flow can reach that deposit before the fee removes anything. This keeps the gate from admitting an over-cap deposit (R-7). `BuyExecution` never contributes: the executor refunds its unspent portion straight back to holding.

## Status

**RESOLVED 2026-07-22.** `DenyOverCapInflows` bounds each target against the provably post-fee holding; the initial 5 regressions cover 2 admit-cases and 3 straight-line R-7 cases, and SQ-482 adds the sixth fallback-control-flow regression. SQ-129 over-cap regressions remain green.
