## Question

**11 §11.8.1 states a disclosure obligation for unreadable preconditions and §11.8.6 states none, though O-8 has three of them.** §11.8.1 requires a client to *"render both errors explicitly"* and to *"not present registration as unconditionally available"*. `registry_core::file` refuses on `WindowClosed`, `TooManyLiveEpochs` and `MilestoneTargetUnset` with no readable half for any (SQ-1031), and one of those bounds is **`MAX_LIVE_EPOCHS = 4`** — reachable in normal operation, unlike the `TooManyReviews` bound of 128 that a *stated* disposition was justified against. Should §11.8.1's sentence generalise to every operator row?
