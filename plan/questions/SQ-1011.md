## Question

**10 §3.1's boot machine has no edge for a `CompatCheck` that could not complete.** Its three exits — `Ready`, `ReadyRestricted`, `ReadOnlyIncompatible` — are each a claim *about the runtime*. A client that reached `CompatCheck` and could not read `spec_version`, or could not pull a compat surface at all, has none of them: synthesising `restricted` with every surface disabled would say *"this surface is absent from this runtime"* about surfaces nothing examined, which is the fabrication the coverage refusal prevents one layer up. Implemented as a fourth arm (`unestablished`) that fails closed and carries a named reason, inventing no UX. §3.1 needs a state or an explicit ruling that this collapses into `SyncDegraded`.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-08 (R-1) — a state, and explicitly not `SyncDegraded`.** 10 §3.1 gains `CompatUnavailable` under a new code `FE-COMPAT-003`: non-terminal, signing disabled, **no** surface named as disabled because none was examined, retrying into `CompatCheck` on the `SyncDegraded` backoff. Collapsing it into `SyncDegraded` was refused on §3.1's **own precedent**, three paragraphs down: the `system_health` rule already forbids reading a broken introspection path as a peer count, because that *"would send the user to a peer-diagnostics panel for a fault that is not theirs"*. A compat probe that could not complete is the same shape of fault. **The lattice stays at three modes**, which is the half worth recording — the implementation's `unestablished` arm was right and the ruling ratified it rather than replacing it. Each of `full`/`restricted`/`read-only-incompatible` is a claim *about the runtime*; this is a claim about the *client*, so a fourth mode would have made every consumer of `CompatMode` handle a value that is not one. §3.2's table therefore keeps three rows and now says why. Doc changes: 10 §3.1 (diagram + normative list), §3.2 (the mid-session rule — a previously established mode MUST NOT survive a runtime change the client could not examine), §5.2, §9.4 (taxonomy range `FE-COMPAT-001..003` + the code's own row), and 11 E4. No 02 change and no contract bump: `CompatMode` is a frontend concept owned by 10
