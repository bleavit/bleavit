## Question

**What does *"holds a pair"* mean in §11.6 step 1 — the displayed holdings, or the holdings after step 1a?** Step 1 offers the 100 %-recovery `merge` *"**only** when the user holds an **Accept+Reject branch-USDC** pair"*. Step 1a then says the UI *"MUST … route the user to whichever of step 1 or step 2 their **post-merge** holdings actually reach"*. `void-recovery.ts` takes the post-merge reading: `parPair` is computed over branch-USDC **plus** the branch-USDC that consolidation would mint, so `mayOfferParMerge` is true for an account holding LONG+SHORT on both branches and no branch-USDC at all. That maximizes the stated recovery, which step 3 requires — and it offers a `merge` button for a pair the account does not hold yet, so the call reverts until `merge_scalar` has run. The reverse reading refuses the action to a user whose holdings really do reach par, and understates nothing but offers less. **Ruling needed:** is step 1's *holds* evaluated on the displayed holdings or on the decomposition's post-consolidation ones, and if the latter, must the action be sequenced rather than offered?
