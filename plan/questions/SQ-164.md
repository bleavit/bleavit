## Question

**T15 vs T16 precedence at grace end (CODE/META).** An unratified queued proposal at grace end satisfies both T15 (Expired) and T16 (`NotRatified`). The S1 model gives the specific T16 reason precedence over generic expiry; 05 §2.1 should state the precedence.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — 05 §2.1 gains a normative **grace-end precedence** rule (T16 before T15) plus the T15 timing-cell and diagram qualifiers. A known terminal cause must not collapse into generic expiry: same refund, different `DecisionRecord`, and the archive is the only place the reason survives.
