## Question

**Which capability should `sweep_insurance` require?** SQ-207 wired it to `Capability::TreasurySpend`, matching the 06 §3.2 row it was added to and every other treasury outflow. But the sweep is an **inflow** that "takes no budget line by design" (08 §1.4), and 08 §1.3 warns specifically about classes that acquire the treasury-spend capability row — "any class that acquires the treasury-spend capability row of 06 §3.2 … MUST be brought under the same decide-time aggregate check". Reusing `TreasurySpend` means a future class granted it inherits the ability to sweep INSURANCE as well as to spend. Reuse (shipped), or a distinct capability?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (A13, PR #146):** selected a dedicated `Capability::InsuranceSweep`. `sweep_insurance` remains Treasury-domain and reachable only through a passed TREASURY decision, but disabling ordinary `TreasurySpend` no longer disables or grants this INSURANCE→MAIN custody-return path; PARAM/CODE/META remain refused. The trailing enum variant preserves existing SCALE discriminants, and no 02 surface changed. Runtime capability-toggle coverage and the exhaustive Rust gate are green.
