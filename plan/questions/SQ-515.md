## Question

**PT-2 and PT-6 run at a single rate, which 15 §4.3 says is not a discharge.** "Every sequence-based suite of §4.3 MUST be run at **both** `ledger.redeem_fee = 0` and a non-zero rate … A suite run at one rate only is **not** a discharge of its obligation." PT-1/PT-4/PT-5, PT-3 and PT-7 do run both; PT-2 and PT-6 do not. For PT-6 the both-rates run also delivers §4.3's literal "byte-identical to the zero-rate run" comparison, which is currently only implied — and that comparison is the machine form of "the fee never leaked into the emergency path"

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-29**: both bodies now run at both rates through `sequence_rates()`. PT-2's `rate > 0` precondition was hoisted to assert `registry_redeem_fee() > 0` once — it guards 13 §1's seed, not the loop variable. Resolving it surfaced a **defect in the mandating text itself**: 15 §4.3 named the *zero*-rate leg load-bearing for I-2/PT-2 while giving the reason that proves the opposite. Corrected under R-1 (Decision log)
