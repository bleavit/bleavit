## Question

**Ledger-vault vs market-book reap ordering needs a normative rule.** Both reaps are permissionless and unordered; the ledger reap removes the terminal markers the market's POL-obligation predicate keyed on, which could resurrect a settled book's obligation and wedge its slot (spec-review major). B10 remediation latches settlement-observed terminality on the market side. Pin the ordering/marker-retention rule in 03 §5.4/04 §2.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** B10's `SettlementObservedAt` latch verified to close the resurrection path under **every** interleaving: the three terminal ledger transitions each write the latch inside the ledger's own storage layer, the sweep cannot run before `terminal + ledger.archive_delay`, `pol_obligation_live` no longer reads ledger state at all, and try-state enforces the binding in both directions. 04 §2 gained the normative *Reap ordering* rule and 03 §5.4 the companion statement that terminal markers are swept state and must never be another pallet's predicate, with idempotency and the G-1 no-fail requirement made normative. Recorded residual: `observe_proposal_terminal` hard-fails on an empty book set, unreachable today but a G-1-shaped coupling the rule now forbids.
