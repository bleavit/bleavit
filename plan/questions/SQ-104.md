## Question

`authorize_upgrade` domain vs matrix, and the migration/xcmp/collator Root-admin surface. B1a projects `system.authorize_upgrade` → `InternalRoot` (denied for every origin incl. `FutarchyCode`, contradicting 06 §3.2 row 3's FutarchyCode ✔) because its dispatch is guard-internal Root (I-10, 09 §2.1(3)); and it classifies migrations `force_*`/xcmp admin/collator `set_*` as `Public` (Root-gated in-pallet → sudo-reachable bootstrap admin), but 06 §6.2 assigns migration-cursor control to the **EmergencyPlaybook** set, which would fail `ensure_root` in-pallet — the PB-MIGRATION lane needs an origin bridge. Also: how is the Phase-3→4 upgrade authorized while the guard (A11) is absent, given 09 §5.3 gives sudo that power but the InternalRoot projection denies it? Overlaps main's SQ-93 (A11 apply-mechanism).
