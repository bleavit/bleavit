## Question

**Doc 10 never says whether a snapshot carries a chain binding, and the obvious reading refuses every pre-upgrade snapshot — which is the case §6.4 assigns snapshots.** §8.2 and §8.4 describe snapshots entirely without a binding; §13.1 mandates one — genesis hash, `spec_version`, `INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION`, gated by **exact equality** — for the three **handoff** formats, which describe *one block* and are unreadable under a runtime the client cannot decode. A snapshot is the opposite shape: §6.4 gives it *"deep history beyond 30 days … by design, not by omission"*, so it necessarily predates the current runtime, and under exact equality the **first runtime upgrade refuses every snapshot ever published** — reported as `FE-PROV-003`, whose recovery then advises checking a download that was never damaged. F9 ships the conservative correction: **`genesisHash` equality only**, because 10 §7 gives one local database per chain identity so a foreign-chain document would file another network's history under this one, while the two version fields stay on the document as advisory. What 10 owes is whether §8 wants a binding at all, which fields are compared, and whether a version difference is an advisory line or a refusal.
