## Question

**RULED AND EXECUTED 2026-08-06 — contract v28 freezes `ExecutionGuard.PendingUpgrade` in 02 §7.4 and `System.AuthorizedUpgrade` in §7.6, and corrects doc 11.** Two measurements settled it. The authorize/apply pair is in `frame_system`, not `cumulus-pallet-parachain-system`, which carries no such item on the pinned SDK line — so §11.8.4 step 1 named a pallet that cannot answer it, the V-169 class. And the stored struct carries a fourth field, `target_spec_version`, which doc 11's three-field spelling omitted. **02 freezes neither `ExecutionGuard.PendingUpgrade` nor `ParachainSystem.AuthorizedUpgrade`, though 11 §11.8.4 step 1 cites *"02 names"* for both — and doc 11's spelling of the stored struct is missing a field.** Step 1 tells the client to read *"the authorized code hash from `parachain-system` storage, and `PendingUpgrade { hash, authorized_at, applicable_at }` from the execution guard … ([02](docs/architecture/02-integration-contract.md) names)"*, and step 4 makes `now ≥ PendingUpgrade.applicable_at` a precondition that MUST be **read, never recomputed** (D-14, SQ-552). Neither item is in 02 §7 or in `tools/release/surface-manifest.json`, so S17's two central reads have no frozen surface and 10 §5.2's compatibility lattice **cannot fail on them** — a runtime upgrade that moved either leaves the classifier reporting `full` while the crank silently breaks. **The framing an F17 audit first proposed is wrong and is corrected here:** 02 §6 and §11.8.4 do *not* contradict each other on `applicable_at`. 02 §6 describes the **event** `UpgradeAuthorized { code_hash, authorized_at }` and says an event consumer derives the third value; §11.8.4 describes the **stored** field, which `execution_guard_core::PendingUpgrade` really holds. The genuine defects are the two above plus a third: the struct has **four** fields (`hash`, `authorized_at`, `applicable_at`, `target_spec_version` — `crates/execution-guard-core/src/lib.rs:189`) and doc 11 writes three, omitting the version the upgrade targets. Client posture meanwhile: `UNREADABLE['O-6']` declares the gap `blocking`, so S17's submit control is closed with the reason named rather than offered on an unread condition.
