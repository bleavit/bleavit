## Question

**`trs.proposer_reward` is ~40 % of the launch cost base and has no derivation anywhere in the specification.** Found by decomposing `C` after E7's cuts: at the 08 §10.1 PARAM ceiling the REWARDS line is **43,482 USDC/yr — 39.8 %**, exactly level with `ops.collators` and larger than every other row combined. The key appears in precisely three places in the whole doc set (the 13 §1 row, a restatement in 08 §1.1, the §10.1 cost row) with no rationale, anchor or derivation — the same shape as `collator.comp_epoch` before SQ-536, which proved 4× too high. **It is not the same answer, and that is the finding.** Under 08 §7.1 a rejected proposal is refunded *in full* ("rejection is information"), so the `intake.slash_pct` risk attaches only to **non-decision-grade** outcomes; EV per submission is `reward·P(adopt) − 0.1·bond·P(non-DG)`. At the committed Phase-0 artifact's rates (PARAM adopts 2.32 %, forms decision-grade 40.56 %) that is **−47.8 USDC**, against a break-even adopt rate of **11.89 %**. So the evidence points **downward-unsafe**, not toward over-payment: a cut to the 13 §1 hard-min ×0.1 would put the break-even at **118.9 %** — unprofitable at *any* adopt rate, even universal adoption. **The artifact cannot settle it either way**: its population is adversarial by construction (informed/noise/arbitrage + five doc-14 manipulator strategies), so 2.32 % is a population average and not an honest proposer's expectation

## Status

**open — filed, deliberately not acted on.** R-2 escalation is a conjunction and both halves hold: the error direction is unsafe (too low ⇒ nobody proposes ⇒ no futarchy and no revenue) **and** no evidence anchors an honest-proposer adopt rate.
