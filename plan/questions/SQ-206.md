## Question

**`ParamView.max_delta: u128` cannot represent a non-absolute max-Δ rule.** 13 §1's max-Δ column carries three kinds (absolute, percent, factor); 02 §4 projects them onto one `u128` scalar with no stated semantics. `MaxDelta::Factor` is asymmetric (`[ceil(v/f), v·f]`), so *any* single scalar is lossy: B2 ships the conservative `min(upward, downward)` (R-7 — never render an inadmissible amendment as admissible), which under-reports the upward allowance, e.g. `exec.lock.code` (100,800, ×2) reports 50,400 though 201,600 is admissible. Should `ParamView` carry exact `min_next`/`max_next` (or the tagged rule) instead of one scalar? That is a 02 §4 contract amendment.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C, contract v6) — `ParamView` gains trailing `min_next` and `max_next`: the exact inclusive interval of admissible next values, obtained by intersecting the record's `min`/`max` with its max-Δ rule, with factor rounding exactly as the admission code does (`ceil(value/factor)` down, saturating `value × factor` up) and no-rule yielding the full `[min, max]`. `max_delta` is retained and normatively defined as the conservative symmetric projection `min(upward, downward)`. The row's example verified exactly: `exec.lock.code` = 100,800 ×2 projects to 50,400 while 201,600 is admissible — under-reporting the upward allowance, never over-reporting it (R-7-safe, but a real functional gap for 15 I-6 and 11's constitution dashboard).
