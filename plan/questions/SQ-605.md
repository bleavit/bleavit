## Question

**10 §6.3 mandates three per-range integrity checks that the light client cannot perform at the depths the ranges describe.** The section says *"cursor integrity checks (hash-at-edge, genesis binding, spec-version-at-edge) apply per range"*, and §4.2 says just as plainly that **smoldot exposes `chainHead` only, with no `archive_*` methods**, so a block hash at an arbitrary past height is not obtainable — which is the whole reason the three-layer model exists. The genesis check is answerable at any time; the other two are answerable only inside the pinned window, which is minutes of chain. **F8 resolves the shape half under R-1** (this session): `CoverageRange` as §6.3 declared it two paragraphs earlier carried a span, an origin, a provider id and a timestamp and **no field any of the three checks could read**, so all three were unimplementable and `invalidateRange` had no detector that could call it; a `RangeEdge` (genesis hash, block hash at `toBlock`, `spec_version` at `toBlock`) is added to 10 §6.3 and to the implementation, recorded in the Decision log. **What is not resolved is the reachability**: F8 treats *cannot check* as **keep the range** (an unreachable chain, a pruned block and a syncing client all mean *cannot say*, and dropping on that empties the index during ordinary offline use), so in practice only ranges whose edge is still inside the pinned window are ever checked, plus the genesis binding everywhere. **Ruling needed:** say so in §6.3 — either the checks are best-effort with the reachable subset named, or a provider-served header is admitted as evidence for the hash check (which would be layer-2 evidence about layer-3 metadata, a boundary 02/§2.2 do not currently address)

## Status

**open — the field-shape half amended under R-1 this session; the reachability half is a real ruling.** The unsafe direction is the *other* one: making an unperformable check invalidate would delete honest history whenever the network is poor, so the conservative reading is in force and is written into the code and the amended section.
