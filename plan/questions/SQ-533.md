## Question

**The ORACLE-line rebate path has no meter at all — six permissionless cranks draw on it bounded only by the funded balance.** 08 §6.3 puts every sanctioned keeper crank on one of two metered tranches, then exempts oracle-funded cranks: they "sit outside this meter entirely and consume neither tranche". 07 §7 agrees — the line "carries no per-tranche reservation". But no *substitute* bound is specified anywhere: `ack_observed` (oracle rounds and registry filings), `crank_close`, `crank_round_close`, `crank_reserve_probe` and `recompute_proof` all pay `keeper.rebate` from `BudgetLine::Oracle` with no epoch cap, no exhaustion latch and no `KeeperBudgetLow`-equivalent event. The only limit is whatever governance funded, and 13 §1 marks the `ORACLE` line `[VERIFY — sized in Phase-2/3 ops planning]`, so today that is unsized-times-unbounded. Two aggravating details: `recompute_proof` accepts a 256 KiB payload (`orc.max_proof_bytes`) and is rebated at the same undifferentiated O(1) rate as a trivial crank; and `wt.max` = 16 watchtowers may each claim a rebated acknowledgment per round. SQ-531 makes the magnitude small — 0.000255 per crank rather than 0.09 — but small-times-unbounded is still unbounded, and the KEEPER line has a latch precisely because the same shape was judged unacceptable there

## Status

**open** — found by the E5 code-outflow audit. Needs either a per-epoch ORACLE meter mirroring §6.3's, or an explicit argument in 08 §6.3 for why this line is safe unmetered when `KEEPER` is not
