## Question

Does 08 §1.3's `Streams` bound (13 §4) count CONCURRENT open streams or LIFETIME streams?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (code): concurrent — a terminal (cancelled / fully-claimed) stream is reaped at the bound (the treasury has no crank, so eviction is lazy at `open_stream`); 13 §4 rationale reads "128 open streams"
