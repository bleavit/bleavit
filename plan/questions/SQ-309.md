## Question

**A stuck migration cursor makes the chain permanently inherent-only, and an extrinsic recovery lane cannot run.** A live or stuck `pallet-migrations` cursor makes `frame-executive` reject every non-inherent extrinsic, including guard, guardian, sudo and migration-control calls; `ForceUnstuck` would expose the half-migrated layout.

## Status

**RESOLVED 2026-07-23 (B16, PR #140 merged).** The pre-authorized inherent path ships with a mandatory paired recovery artifact, Operational qualification/pinning, exact cursor retirement, relay-Abort restoration, installed-code identity proof and a recovery profile with zero SDK MBMs. Missing/mismatched/unrepairable recovery remains locked. B15 supplies the first admissible primary MBM: a finite read-only ledger backfill with a release-specific runtime-local restart repair tested at every cutpoint; unknown layouts remain prohibited rather than falsely “repaired.”
