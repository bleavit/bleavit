## Question

**Three of `registry_core::file`'s five refusals have no readable half, and §11.8.6's O-8 row names none of the five.** The row lists the bond, the USDC headroom, the epoch's filing count, the frozen `spec_version` and the evidence hash. `file` also refuses on `WindowClosed` (`now > Epoch::filing_window_end(epoch)`), on `MilestoneTargetUnset` (`Epoch::milestone_target(epoch, spec_version) == 0`, Milestone instance only) and on `TooManyLiveEpochs` (`FilingCount`'s key set at `MAX_LIVE_EPOCHS`). No frozen surface publishes the filing-window end, the milestone target, or that bound, so a filer is walked to a **bonded** signature the runtime reverts. The two readable refusals are closed as of 2026-08-08: `AlreadyFinal` reads `ClosedAt[epoch][spec_version]`, frozen at contract v28 and written by `close_epoch` in the same call that pushes the terminal aggregate the core tests, and `InvalidClass` is made unrepresentable by typing `FilingInputs` per instance, since `validate_class` admits `S1|S2|S3` for Incident and `Scope(_)` for Milestone. `points` remains outside the model because nothing in `file` gates on it, but a form that cannot express it cannot encode the call. Should 02 freeze the filing-window end and the milestone target, and should O-8 carry all five refusals the way §11.5's `execute` row carries the guard's fourteen?
