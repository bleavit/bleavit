## Question

**12 §6.3 *Ownership* does not determine an owner for four alert domains.** The paragraph gives two rules that disagree. *"An alert's owner is the owner of its §6.1 row"*, and *"Protocol-domain alerts have no dedicated program row and fall to the Monitoring coordinator"*. Doc 12 tabulates no domain → §6.1-row mapping, so whether **Epoch progress, TWAP, Relay finality and Upgrades** have a dedicated program row is unstated. Applying the fallback literally moves all four to the Monitoring coordinator and away from the roles their runbooks name (Keeper coordinator, Release operations lead). F15 adopted the reading that each runbook already carries its domains' §6.1 owner — licensed by the paragraph's closing claim that the Bootnodes / Served-state-window pair is *the* live instance of a divergence — and `deploy/ops-handbook/README.md` states that it is a reading. Settling it moves accountability for four alert domains
