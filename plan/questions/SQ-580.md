## Question

**11 §11.3 mandates a client read that the frozen contract does not publish.** The section says "Multisig via `Multisig.as_multi` with approval state read from `Multisig.Multisigs`; proxies supported as call wrappers under the same precondition system". The runtime **has** all three pallets (`Utility` 24, `Proxy` 25, `Multisig` 26), but doc **02 mentions none of them — zero occurrences** — and `tools/release/surface-manifest.json` carries no `multisig` or `proxy` entry among its 43 storage rows. So `CRITICAL_SURFACE` has nothing to probe, and the consequence is worse than a missing feature: a runtime upgrade that moved `Multisig.Multisigs` would leave 10 §5.2's classifier reporting **`full`** while the multisig approval path silently broke — the compat lattice cannot see a surface it was never told about. Same family as SQ-577 (10 §5.2 names calls; 02 freezes none) and SQ-552 (both documents cite a contract test nobody wrote): a client obligation with no frozen surface behind it. **Ruling needed:** admit `Multisig.Multisigs` (and whatever the proxy wrapper path reads) to 02's frozen surface with the contract bump that implies, or scope multisig/proxy out of the canonical client. Not coded around: the P-row clause type can only cite a generated `SurfaceId`, so inventing one would mean hand-listing a frozen surface, which app-code rule 7 forbids and which is the defect that rule exists for
