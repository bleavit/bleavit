## Question

The stale-epoch force-reject bound (`StaleEpochBound`, 05 §2.1 T20 / 13 §2) has no spec-defined anchor. A8 latches a high-water proposal-id snapshot when the persisted next phase boundary is overdue by `StaleEpochBound` (epoch-staleness, **not** a per-proposal lifetime); affected proposals (id ≤ cutoff) force-reject, later submissions are immune.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B1 ratification, delegated ruling).** 05 §2.1 gained a normative *T20 stale-epoch anchor* note ratifying the shipped mechanism: `StaleEpochBound` measures **epoch staleness, not per-proposal lifetime** — the overdue margin on the persisted **next phase** boundary — and trips a latched high-water proposal-id snapshot, so `id <= cutoff` force-rejects and later submissions are immune. The note also pins two behaviours the row omitted and code-verification surfaced: the latch is **suppressed while the dead-man is armed/paused or during recovery** (a stalled clock the dead-man already owns must not also be charged to epoch staleness), and it **self-clears** once the drain completes, making it a bounded drain rather than a permanent mode.
