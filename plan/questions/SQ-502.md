## Question

**The `P` pillar has no producers, no normalization kernel, and one component needs a bounded-state sketch design.** 05 §4.3's three `P` components are the only welfare components whose raw form is an unbounded series rather than a ratio already in [0,1], so all three depend on 05 §4.6's normalization — winsorize at the p5/p95 of the trailing 12 finalized epoch values, `log1p` for heavy-tailed series, min–max to [0,1], constants frozen at epoch open. **None of it exists:** `MetricSpec.prior_bounds` is stored and round-tripped and read by nothing, and no percentile, winsorization or min–max code is in the workspace (V-43, re-verified 2026-07-26). §4.6's cold start additionally wants 12 declared pseudo-observations per component at genesis, sourced from Phase-2 shadow-run data that does not exist yet and which 15 requires published with the genesis artifacts. The qualified-users component is harder than the other two: a filtered distinct count is not what a plain HLL answers, and the bounded structures that do answer it (bottom-k/KMV sampling carrying a per-sample day mask) are grindable by an adversary who chooses account keys — so it needs a threat-model read and a 14 row, not just an implementation. Scope: ~~the normalization kernel with reference-model parity and differential vectors~~ (**done 2026-07-26, PR #188** — frame-free kernel, type-7 percentile on the 1e9 grid, `log1p` over the existing `futarchy_fixed` `ln` rather than a second logarithm, bit-identical to a Python side written from spec text and cross-checked over 40,000 randomised cases with 0 mismatches; 05 §4.6 and 15 §4.4 amended to fix the `log1p` grid, bound-transform and zero-width-range refusal); `PriorBounds` genesis values; the fee and settled-value accumulators; the sketch design; and only then a genesis `MetricSpec` set that can produce a complete snapshot. **A blocker surfaced by #188 and folded in here rather than raised separately:** §4.6 says the constants come from `Snapshot(e−1)` history, but `Snapshot.components` holds **normalized** values (§4.4 pins them to [0,1]) while §4.6 winsorizes the **raw** metric. §4.4 names raw counters as a pipeline stage and no section gives them a storage home, so the trailing-12 *raw* history the kernel consumes is stored nowhere — and `Snapshots` is a frozen 02 §7.4 read surface, so putting it there needs a contract bump. The kernel takes `finalized: &[FixedU64]` from its caller and is correct either way, but **production wiring is blocked until the raw series has a home**. Also worth a look before the fees producer ships: `prior_bounds` is `FixedU64`, capping a genesis pseudo-observation at ~1.84e10.

## Status

**open** — successor to SQ-181, split out so A14's seven natively-bounded components are not gated behind it. Registration is *not* gated: `register_spec` never consults the producers, so the conforming v1 set can be registered first and snapshots fail closed until the producers land.
