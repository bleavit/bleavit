## Question

**Are protocol custody accounts metered under `phase3.dep_cap`?** Treasury-origin trap recovery deposits to the treasury protocol account are recorded in the per-account cumulative meter (test-pinned; status-quo-safe). Once cumulative recoveries exceed the 20,000 USDC default, further protocol recovery refuses until a phase gate raises the cap. Intended, or should protocol accounts be exempt (cf. the 08 §8 MaxPositions/deposit exemption precedent)?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-24 (verified in merged B15, `ba27dfa`).** The ruled implementation is live: the runtime's canonical `ProtocolAccounts` predicate bypasses only the per-account `phase3.dep_cap` meter, while the global TVL cap remains enforced; the inflow/escrow admission, accounting and try-state paths agree. `pallet-inflow-caps` is **14/14** and the production Treasury protocol-keyed trap-recovery regression is green. No contract, network or further Rust change applies.
