## Question

**07 §6.3's bond-coverage admission rule is enforced nowhere, and `MetricSpec` cannot express its input.** §2(5) makes admission of an attested component conditional on §6.3's rule `(2^R_max − 1) · orc.bond_bps ≥ Δs_max` — the rule that makes a lie cost more than it can move, and the stated basis for the 150 bps hard floor. `can_admit_attested_component` exists (`crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs:1310-1321`) but its **only callers are its own unit tests**; `register_metric_spec` (`crates/welfare-core/src/lib.rs:404-487`) performs no coverage check, and `MetricSpec` (`:152-171`) has **no `Δs_max` field**, so the rule is unimplementable against the current struct regardless of where it is called. 07 §2(5) also requires ≥ 3 reporters and ≥ 2 watchtowers at admission — verify whether that half is enforced. Either wire the rule (adding the mandatory `Δs_max` MetricSpec field 05 §12.4-equivalent already assumes) or record why admission is unguarded. Surfaced by SQ-296 verification; not previously rowed. **Scoped 2026-07-25, and the row's open questions now have answers.** (a) The §2(5) half is enforced **nowhere**: `register_metric_spec` validates version/activation/weights/epsilon/source-pillar/discipline flags only, `pallets/welfare` has no oracle dependency at all, and `orc.n_min` (13 §1, default 3, K floor 3) has **zero consumers in the whole tree** — its only occurrences are its genesis seed and a doc comment. So both halves of 07 §2(5) are unguarded, not just the coverage rule. (b) **Units are basis points**, fixed by 07 §6.1 ("is evaluated in basis-point space") and §6.3's worked example (0.10 s-shift ⇒ 1,000 bps against 1,750 bps coverage) — which is exactly what the shipped `can_admit_attested_component` already assumes. (c) **07 §6.3's citation is dangling**: it calls `Δs_max` "a mandatory MetricSpec field per 05 §12.4-equivalent", but 05 has no §12.4 and its §4.4 field list omits the field entirely, so 05 must be amended before the code can claim to follow it. (d) §6.3's "the 150 bps hard min keeps the left side ≥ 10.5%" is arithmetically true only at `R_max = 3`; 13 §1 admits `orc.rounds ∈ [2,4]`, and at (2, 150) coverage is 450 bps. Evaluate at live params (as the shipped helper does) and read 10.5% as illustrative. (e) Enforcing this makes attested components **un-registrable at genesis** — no reporters exist yet — which is the correct fail-closed reading of 07 §3 but will break genesis presets and several fixtures at once; budget for that rather than weakening the gate. Wiring `orc.n_min` also gives it its first dispatch consumer, so the limit-coverage registry row moves `param-bounds → dispatch-limit` with a marked test.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-25 (contract v14)** — both halves of 07 §2(5) are now enforced at `register_metric_spec`, the only place a frozen spec is ever written. `MetricSpec` gains the trailing `delta_s_max_bps` the rule was unimplementable without; welfare-core takes an injected `AttestedAdmission` (seats + the live coverage rate) so the genesis build and a live dispatch take the identical path, per the SQ-82 discipline. **`orc.n_min` gets its first consumer of any kind** — its limit-coverage row moves `param-bounds → dispatch-limit` with a marked past-limit test. The §6.3 coverage derivation was **single-homed** in `oracle_core::coverage_bps` first: it already had two copies with *opposite* correct failure directions (admission must refuse an unreadable ladder; the registry's filing bond must fall back to the tightest lawful ladder, never zero), so the function returns `Option` and each call site states its own direction. **The row's scoping expectation was wrong in the safe direction and the correction is the finding:** the gate does not apply to *some* spec sets, it applies to **all** of them — 05 §4.3 makes every A-pillar component attested and §4.4 requires the A weights to sum to 1, so no valid MetricSpec is free of attested components. Seating the oracle is therefore a precondition of the *first* MetricSpec, which is 07 §3's stated bootstrap order and is now normative in §2(5). No genesis preset registers a spec, so nothing regressed. Live-parameter halves are deliberately **not** re-checked in try-state — a lawful amendment or a reporter's legal exit would become a chain-halting violation; that drift is SQ-495, still open.
