## Question

**Does 10 §3.1's terminal `WrongChain` state permit destroying INV-FE-11's *"always-available"* verification panel?** The terminal screen unmounts the whole tree and renders the two genesis hashes, on the stated grounds that every other surface would be describing a chain the client cannot identify. That argument does not reach the verification panel, which describes the **bundle** — release TXID, source commit, per-file hashes, descriptor metadata hashes, chain-spec hashes — and is exactly what an operator needs when the diagnosis is *release pins X, chain answered Y*. INV-FE-11 calls the panel *always-available* and 15 §6 pairs it with the boot identity check as the enforcing components of one invariant. Latent today: `VerificationPanelView` is exported and rendered by nothing. Recommended reading: *terminal* bars any path forward into the app and does not license the client to stop identifying itself, so the terminal screen should carry the panel's content.
