## Question

**Does §8.4's *"deterministic spot re-derivation"* mean every reachable covered block, or a sample of them?** The bullet reads *"deterministic spot re-derivation for the covered blocks that fall inside light-client-reachable depth"* and names no rate. The bullet directly below it gives live indexers an explicit one — *"1-in-16-page row re-verification"* — and 14 TH-49's residual-risk arithmetic is computed from that number, so the omission on the snapshot side is conspicuous rather than obviously deliberate: the word *spot* suggests a sample and the phrase *"for the covered blocks that"* reads as all of them. F9 implements it as **exhaustive within the window**, which is the conservative reading (it can only compare more, never fewer), bounded by a work ceiling derived from §4.2's ~256-block pruning depth. What a ruling changes: if it is a sample, the rate is a release constant that needs the same derivation §8.3's thresholds got and TH-50's residual-risk statement needs the same arithmetic TH-49 already carries; if it is exhaustive, §8.4 should say *every* and the ceiling needs a stated relationship to the reachable window rather than a factor of two
