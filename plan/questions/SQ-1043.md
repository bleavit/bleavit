## Question

**10 §3.1 fixes the backoff endpoints and never states the growth law.** It writes *"backoff 1s→60s"* and requires retrying *"for as long as the probe keeps failing"*. Doubling with a clamp at 60 s is what shipped, on this edge and on `SyncDegraded`'s, and the clamp is required rather than merely permitted — an unclamped doubling would exceed the published ceiling and stop retrying inside any real session. But two implementations of *"the same 1 s→60 s backoff"* can still differ. Should §3.1 name the law?
