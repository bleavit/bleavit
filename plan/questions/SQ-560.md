## Question

**A-3 does not hold at genesis, by construction — the signable electorate is 100 % insider.** 01 §2.2 A-3 says "the values-layer electorate is not majority-captured; its supermajorities and delays make capture slow and visible, not impossible". Deriving 08 §2.1's allocation with a `signable_at(phase)` classifier: **350M VIT is directly signable at genesis** (200M founding + 150M ecosystem/ops), and the other 650M sits in three keyless `PalletId` pots — so external opposition is **zero**, not merely outvoted. Founding stake alone supplies exactly the 20 % starting support the **`entrenched`** track requires; the combined 35 % clears all six tracks with no external aye, which makes 09 §7.1's Phase-3→4 "values ratification of the arming upgrade" insider-ratified by construction. Maximum conviction adds zero lock cost at genesis (32 weeks = 224 days, inside the one-year founding cliff). The six-track table itself differentials clean against the runtime, so this is an allocation/timing property rather than a governance-config defect. **Second, smaller gap:** 06 and 13 publish support percentages and **never name the denominator**; the runtime picks `MaxTurnout = VIT_TOTAL_SUPPLY`, which appears in no normative text — a smaller base makes capture easier, a larger one makes ratification unreachable. **Ruling needed:** name the support base in 06/13; and decide whether A-3 needs a stated genesis qualification. **CORRECTED 2026-08-01 by adversarial review of PR #206, before any ruling was taken.** The "100 % insider-controlled" conclusion **assumed a colluding bloc**. 08 §2.1 specifies allocations and custody but identifies neither common control over the founding beneficiaries nor the ops multisig membership, and does not establish that vested balances vote — while A-3 is about *capture*, which requires coordination. Coalition membership, vesting-vote eligibility and the turnout base are now explicit scenario inputs. **Two halves remain unconditional and are the durable findings:** 06 and 13 never name the support denominator, and **no externally signable stake exists at genesis** (the other 650M sits in keyless `PalletId` pots)
