## Question

**Guard-record reaping has no assigned producer for never-queued ratified proposals or retained non-requeued reruns.** R2 makes epoch's idempotent terminal callback the universal owner; confirm that ownership in the lifecycle text.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B1 ratification, delegated ruling).** 06 §2.2 gained a normative *guard-record reaping ownership* paragraph confirming the shipped design: the epoch pallet's terminal callback is the **single owner**, deliberately universal rather than queue-scoped so it also covers the two stranded cases — a proposal **ratified but never queued** (whose `RatificationRecord` would outlive it) and a **retained, non-requeued rerun**. The paragraph makes **idempotency normative** rather than incidental, which code-verification showed is load-bearing: the guard also self-cleans on its own terminal paths and several epoch call sites invoke the callback directly, so double invocation is a routine occurrence, not an edge case.
