## Question

09 §1.4 lists `utility.batch`/`force_batch` in the SafetyFilter-recursed wrapper set, but their best-effort semantics (inner failure → `Ok` + `BatchInterrupted`, partial commit) defeat the 09 §1.2(12) atomicity requirement (a partial payload could commit while the guard records success and T18 never runs). A11 defines + models the constraint that only atomic `batch_all` is admitted; **B1a's concrete SafetyFilter MUST reject `utility.batch`/`force_batch`** in guard payloads.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (triage sweep — code-verified).** The guard seam rejects both best-effort wrappers **recursively**, including when nested inside `batch_all` (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/classifier.rs:786-791` in `collect_guard_domains`, reached from `safety_filter` and re-run at dispatch). Pinned by `guard_rejects_best_effort_wrappers_and_admits_atomic_batch_all` (`tests.rs:1629`) and the model-level `d1_best_effort_batch_wrappers_are_rejected_by_the_guard_model` (`pallets/execution-guard/src/tests.rs:900`). The ordinary base call filter deliberately still admits `batch`/`force_batch` for signed extrinsics — a different seam, pinned separately in the S5 inventory.
