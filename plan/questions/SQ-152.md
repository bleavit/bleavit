## Question

**Privileged wrapper admission criterion is inconsistent.** The explicit S5 acceptance criterion requires `contains_for` to reject every wrapper around a privileged leaf for all eight class origins, but 06 §3.3 says utility batch/with-weight/sudo recurse and the current core admits same-origin compositions (for example ConstitutionalValues admits `utility.batch(ForeignAssets.create)`). Is same-origin recursive composition legal, or must every privileged leaf be bare?

## Status
