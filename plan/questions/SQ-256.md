## Question

**`SlotsShrunk` and `PendingOutflowSyncFailed` are outside 02 §6's canonical event surface.** 08 §1.4/§4.4 mandate SlotsShrunk (emitted by the decision engine — B10 lands it in pallet-epoch with {epoch, requested, funded, dropped} as slot counts); the guard gained the diagnostic PendingOutflowSyncFailed. 02 §6's FINAL lists omit both — an additive 02 bump needs the joint backend+frontend sign-off. Also ratify counts-vs-USDC-amounts for requested/funded.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C, contract v6) — `SlotsShrunk { epoch, requested, funded, dropped }` is frozen in 02 §6 with its shipped shape, and the units question is ruled: **`requested` and `funded` are proposal-slot counts, not USDC amounts** — `epoch-core` derives them from qualified-entry and surviving-entry counts, with USDC deciding only which entries fit. `PendingOutflowSyncFailed` is ruled the other way under the new §6 inclusion rule: an operations alarm with no frontend workflow, named in §6 as deliberately off-contract and owned by 12 §6.3. 11 gains the `SlotsShrunk` dashboard requirement 08 §1.4 already pointed at. No production code needed.
