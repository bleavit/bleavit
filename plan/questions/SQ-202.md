## Question

Tiered subsetting of 15 §5's gating rule: 15 §4.7 names keeper-loss, dead-man, and the XCM reserve-transfer suites among "the backend's own suites", but 04 (~8 h real finality stall), 08 (43,200 real relay blocks ≈ 3 days), 09 (63-day soak, SQ-128) are physically infeasible per tag, and 03/07 are gated on unwired surface (B9 topology; SQ-101/B4). The B7 producer encodes a release/g1 tier split (validator-pinned: 04/05/08/09 = g1) and refuses evidence while any release-tier suite remains gated — which suite subset satisfies "ran against exactly those artifacts"? Extends SQ-139.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B5 ratification; tiering ratified as an explicit, bounded narrowing).** 15 §5 now admits a declared **release tier** against which the gating rule is satisfied, with the remainder in a **phase-gate tier** proven against 09 §7.1 — and says plainly that this *narrows* the gating sentence, since §4.7 names the dead-man and XCM reserve-transfer suites among the backend's own suites and the release tier does not carry all of them. Admissible only under four conditions, all matching shipped behaviour: manifest-declared membership pinned by the environment validator (04/05/08/09 = g1); a release-tier suite that does not run and pass **blocks evidence emission** and never degrades to a pass; every exclusion recorded inside the evidence with its reason — promoted to a **frozen, assembly-validated field** after the adversarial review caught that condition (3) otherwise elevated exactly the class of field the neighbouring paragraph forbids consumers to depend on; and evidence admissible only when stamped with the release tier (newly enforced — see SQ-139). Membership is a claim about execution cost, never importance. Noted: G1 passing drills 04/05 did **not** re-tier them, and `--include-gated` remains the documented operator override.
