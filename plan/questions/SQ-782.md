## Question

**10 §9.1 and §9.2 size a raw `priceSamples` tier and no sentence anywhere assigns it a producer.** 02 §5 freezes the minimal client ingest set as `Traded` **and** `Observed`; §9.1's row-rate model is built on observations (*"1 per `mkt.obs_interval` = 10 blocks per trading book"*, 891.43 rows/day primary and 1,440 hosted) and §9.2 gives that tier **60 %** of the cap, publishes its depth at four book counts, makes it the ladder's **first rung** and names it in a `MUST`. F8 scans `Observed` and discards it: `priceSample()` has no caller outside tests and the block scan attaches a payload only to `Market.Traded`, so the tier is permanently empty in production. **Ruling needed:** does the row-rate model oblige the client to ingest `Observed` into `priceSamples`, or is the raw tier a Phase-2 surface whose budget was sized ahead of its producer?
