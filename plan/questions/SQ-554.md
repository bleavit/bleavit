## Question

**The reserve probe is funded to the unit with no buffer, and its runway check is never re-run after arming.** 08 §10's probe line is 52.5 USDC/epoch = 913.125 USDC/year against a cadence demand of exactly **365.2 envelopes for 365 probe-days** — the displayed `≈52`/`≈913` are cost-table roundings, and treating them as the allocation produces a shortfall rather than a margin. The first-arm runway (2 failures + 3 recovery passes = 5 envelopes = 12.5 USDC) is checked at arming only: **one** lawful amendment takes `res.recover_thr` 3 → 255, requiring **257 envelopes = 642.5 USDC**, and nothing re-runs the check (`res.fail_thr` → 255 wants 645; `res.probe_amount` reaches `u128::MAX`). PB-RESERVE is **not** absorbing, contrary to the audit — no unhealthy state can *arm* a refill, but a decision armed before the flag still executes `fund_budget_line`. Watchtowers have the mirror problem: the quorum floor is screened only at registration, so from the minimum two-seat set **one** ejection loses quorum, or one lawful amendment raises quorum 2 → 3. Two §4 delegations are dangling — doc 06 defines no watchtower recall, and doc 05 defines neither `entity_ref` nor the promised no-two-seats rule. **Ruling needed:** amendment-time re-verification of the fail/recovery runway; the buffer question reopens once the line is actually sized. **CORRECTED 2026-08-01 by adversarial review of PR #206, before any ruling was taken.** The zero-headroom result is **informational, not a funding failure**: 08 §10's probe row is cost-estimate arithmetic and 13 leaves `ops.reserve_probe` `[VERIFY]`, so equality between routine demand and a cost basis proves neither that the line is funded nor that it lacks a buffer. The amendment-time gap and the watchtower quorum finding are unaffected
