## Question

**The SQ-481 fee reduction ignored XCM fallback control flow and could re-open SQ-129's mint-then-trap path.** `deposit_fee_reduction` subtracted a top-level USDC `PayFees` whenever no top-level deposit preceded it, but the scanner also treated deposits inside `SetErrorHandler`/`SetAppendix` as local targets. If an earlier instruction or `PayFees` itself failed, the pinned executor ran that fallback without the later fee having removed anything; the fallback could therefore attempt the full pre-fee holding after the gate admitted only the reduced amount.

## Status

**RESOLVED 2026-07-22.** Any locally-metered fallback deposit now disables fee reduction, so the pre-mint bound remains the full holding. Executor-level regression `caps_group_pre_mint_gate_ignores_fee_when_error_handler_can_deposit_full_holding` failed pre-fix after mint with `FailedToTransactAsset(\"USDC inflow cap exceeded\")` and now fails at the barrier with no trapped-assets event.
