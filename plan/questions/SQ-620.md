## Question

**11 §11.5's P-13 still says the round bond is *"recomputed and displayed"*, which SQ-598 concluded no client can do.** SQ-598 established that `B_1 = max(orc.bond_floor, ceil(orc.bond_bps × StakeAtRisk / 10,000))` is not computable from any frozen surface, because `StakeAtRisk(c, m)` sums escrow over every cohort whose frozen MetricSpec consumes the component (07 §6.1) and 02 publishes no such view. The P-13 row was never amended, so the normative text and the ruling disagree — and that disagreement reached the client as **two implementations**: the row table declared a cohort-escrow read plus a headroom check against the recomputed amount, while `oracle-reporting.ts` declared the bond structurally uncomputable and blocked only on the floor. Worse, the table's escrow clause cited `storage.epoch.cohorts`, and `CohortInfo { epoch, proposals, status }` carries no escrow field at all — a clause reading a map that cannot answer it, which typechecks because a `SurfaceId` says nothing about what the item holds. Client posture: the escrow clause is deleted, P-13 cites SQ-598's refusal, and `reportBlocks` consumes P-13's clause list as its work list rather than re-deriving one.

## Status

**resolved 2026-08-07 (contract v29), and the answer changed rather than being corrected.** Once the chain publishes the bond, P-13 is neither recomputed nor floored — it is **read**, exactly as the challenge side already reads `OracleRoundView.bond`, with the disclosure that it is priced at the current block and fixes at submission. The escrow clause stays deleted, and `oracle-reporting.ts`'s floor-only block is replaced rather than kept beside it
