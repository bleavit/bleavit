## Question

**Does §6.3 oblige a covered read for every tier the client holds, or only for the tier a screen names?** The rule binds *"every history query"* and says nothing about which stores must be **reachable** through one. Measured, that gap has a consequence rather than being academic: the package's only covered read served `priceSamples` — the tier SQ-782 records as having no producer in production — while `candles1h`, which `storeWriter` fills from §9.1's scan-time aggregate on every block carrying a fill, had no covered path at all. So the disclosure machinery pointed at the permanently empty tier and the only way to draw a chart was `db.candles1h.toArray()`, which is the bare-rows reading §6.3 forbids, reached by the one route left open. F8 adds `coveredCandles` and a gate that fails on any chart-tier reference outside a covered query, which is the strict reading. **Adjacent to SQ-821 and distinct:** SQ-821 asks what **shape** the answer takes (a field on `CoveredResult`, a container, an obligation on consumers); this asks **which reads must produce one at all**. **Ruling needed:** is the obligation per query, per tier, or per rendered surface?

## Status

**open — raised by the fifth R-6 review of F8.** The strict reading is shipped, so a narrower ruling removes a gate rather than requiring code. Filed to **B7**
