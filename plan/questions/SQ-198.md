## Question

**Canonical MetricId table 02 exposure:** the frozen v1 id assignments (05 §4.3 + 13 §3.4 mirror, `futarchy_primitives::metric_ids`) exist so chain/FE "agree without discovery" — the FE reads oracle rounds and component views keyed by `MetricId`. Does the table belong in 02 (contract bump + joint sign-off), or is the 05/13 double-home + primitives mirror sufficient?

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C) — **closed; the 05/13 double-home is sufficient.** 05 §4.3 owns MetricId semantics, 13 §3.4 mirrors the registry, and `futarchy_primitives::metric_ids` is the executable mirror pinned by a unit test. A third normative copy in 02 would violate the two-homes rule (constants live in 02 and 13 only); v6 adds a non-normative pointer beside `MetricId` instead.
