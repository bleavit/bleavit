## Question

**Is the mint-step global cap defined over issuance-increasing mints only?** `TrapRecoveryInflows` bypasses `mint_admissible` for pallet-xcm's issuance-neutral trapped-imbalance reconstruction (deposit leg stays metered; over-cap recovery re-traps; safety test-pinned). 09 §5.2 is silent on the exemption. Pin "the mint-step gate binds issuance-increasing mints" in 09 §5.2 or require cap checks on reconstruction too.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B2 · ratified).** 09 §5.2 now pins the mint-step gate to **issuance-increasing** mints and states the trapped-imbalance reconstruction exemption. Issuance neutrality was **proven in the pinned SDK sources, not asserted**: the trap forgets the imbalance (`pallet-xcm-29.0.0` `src/lib.rs:3910`) and the claim mints a duplicate then drops it (`:3953-3968`), net zero — so the trapped holding is already inside `total_issuance` and re-applying the cap would double-count. Refusing this row would have been the dangerous choice under R-7: the trap entry is consumed **before** `mint_asset` and the error arm only logs, so a cap check at reconstruction would silently destroy recoverable user funds whenever issuance sits near the cap. The deposit leg stays metered and double-claim is impossible (the trap counter is decremented before the mint).
