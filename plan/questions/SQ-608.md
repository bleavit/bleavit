## Question

**10 §9.2's *"downsampled"* label has no provenance, so with per-source chart rows one span can be coarse for one source and raw for another and the label can only lie about one of them.** §9.2 requires an evicted range to become *"a labelled 'downsampled' range, not a hole, and never a silent splice"*, and this session's R-1 amendment adds that a coarse **row** carries one origin (the ladder degrades resolution and may not relabel a source). The **label** was left provenance-blind, and once §7 keys chart rows per source that is no longer a detail: a client that ingested a book itself and also imported an indexer's series can evict one and not the other, and a single label then either overstates the degradation (claiming the whole span is coarse when the verified rows are still raw) or understates it. F8 ships the blind label, which is the conservative direction — it never claims *more* resolution than is stored, only less — and merges labels across sources. **Ruling needed:** either key the label by source in §9.2, or state that the label is a property of the *span* and that a surface must read the rows' own `resolution` to answer per source. Two related questions fall out of the same amendment and are recorded here rather than filed separately, because one ruling settles all three: whether *"names its distinct sources **rather than** counting its gaps"* forbids a surface that states both (F8's badge states both), and whether §9.2's published order binds the sequence of *steps* the quota manager reports or only the chart rungs among themselves — event compaction and the chart rungs consume separate shares, so the order changes nothing about what is destroyed and everything about the step list §9.2 calls *"deterministic and user-visible"*

## Status

**open — raised by the R-6 review of F8's repair pass.** No unsafe direction: the shipped label is the pessimistic one and the badge states more rather than less. Filed to **B7**
