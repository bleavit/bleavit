## Question

**Is a snapshot "overdue" while the dead-man pause blocks its epoch's close?** The schedule-derived deadline keeps running during a pause, but the paused clock prevents epoch close and `record_snapshot` legally requires epoch < CurrentEpoch — an over-long pause would latch a snapshot cause no crank can clear, making recovery unreachable (no origin can clear bit 6). B10 remediation suppresses snapshot-overdue evaluation for epochs whose close is blocked by the active pause. Ratify.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B1 ratification, delegated ruling).** 05 §4.8 gained a normative *snapshot-overdue under an active pause* paragraph ratifying the B10 suppression. The ruling states the reachability argument explicitly — evaluating the trigger against an epoch whose close the pause itself blocks would latch a cause no crank can clear and no origin can reset — and pins the suppression **narrowly**, matching the shipped predicate rather than the row's looser wording: it applies only while the clock is paused **and** the outstanding snapshot's due epoch is at or after the current index. An epoch that had already closed before the pause is **not** suppressed and may still re-arm the detector. Trigger 1 is unaffected.
