## Question

05 §4.7 is silent on `record_daily_gate` cardinality: the extrinsic is repeat-tolerant (a duplicate `(epoch, day, spec_version)` OR-merges identical flags — a state no-op). B9 keeps it repeat-tolerant but rebates only recordings that actually change state (first write of the day, or genuinely new flags), closing the general-tranche drain both reviews flagged. Should duplicates instead be rejected outright on-chain?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** Repeat-tolerance ratified; duplicates MUST NOT be rejected. 05 §4.7 gained a normative *Recording cardinality* paragraph: flags OR-merge so a duplicate `(epoch, day, spec_version)` is a **state** no-op, late/duplicate submissions from independent keepers are the expected operating mode, and rejecting them would convert a benign race into a failed dispatch the losing keeper must special-case. Rebate eligibility stays state-change-gated so duplicates cannot drain the meter, and the sample marker is required to stay distinct from the breach `day_bitmap`.
