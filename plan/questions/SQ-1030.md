## Question

**A guardian approval reaches the fifth signature on two maps 02 freezes no surface for.** `approve_action` reads `Guardian.PlaybookRegistered[id]` **after** counting that approval and refuses with `PlaybookNotRegistered` (`pallets/guardian/src/lib.rs`), and `dispatch` refuses re-activation of `PB-LEDGER-FREEZE` while a record for it sits in `Guardian.ActivePlaybooks` (`PlaybookAlreadyActive`, `crates/guardian-core/src/lib.rs` — that playbook renews only through a values referendum, 06 §6.3, while every other one renews in place). Both refusals fall on the threshold approver and 02 §7.4 freezes neither map, so the client cannot evaluate either and O-3 carries two `blocking` unreadable obligations that close S15's approve control outright. A control that can never open is one nothing exercises end to end, which is the state `check-unreadable-obligations.py` exists to keep temporary. Should 02 §7.4 freeze `Guardian.PlaybookRegistered` and `Guardian.ActivePlaybooks`? `TooManyActivePlaybooks` on the same `ensure!` needs nothing — `dispatch` upserts by id, there are six `PlaybookId` values and the bound is six, so it is unreachable.

## Status

**RESOLVED 2026-08-09 — contract v30 froze both maps, and the client reads them on both flows.** 02 §7.4 now carries `Guardian.PlaybookRegistered` and `Guardian.ActivePlaybooks`, so the two `blocking` obligations became ordinary clauses on `O-3` **and** `O-4`. Both forbidden readings 02 §7.4 states are enforced rather than noted: registration has no path into the trigger evaluation, and presence blocks `PB-LEDGER-FREEZE` alone, with the five lawful renewals asserted individually. The propose side was closed in the same round — `propose_action` validates nothing and `check_and_consume` runs at the fifth approval, so a propose flow blind to `PlaybookNotRegistered` spends the scarcest signatures the protocol has on an action the chain will refuse, which is the reason `proposalBlocks` already blocked `PB-MIGRATION`. S15's approve control opens again
