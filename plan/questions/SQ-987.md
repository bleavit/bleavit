## Question

**§8.5.2's read-path hole is named but not closed.** The paragraph opens *"a source that answers `GET /chain` and fails every `GET /range` can never be disabled by anything at all"* and closes with a rule reaching only the wrong-chain sub-case. A source that answers `/chain` and `503`s every `/range` is still undisableable: probes keep succeeding, §8.5.2 keeps liveness off the ladder on purpose, and sampling over zero pages is `inconclusive` rather than a mismatch. Which sentence is normative — the hole statement, or the rule that does not close it? A third state (`unreachable` runs counted separately from the probe ladder) would close it without letting read volume drive auto-disable, which is the property §8.5.2 protects.
