## Question

**Does §8.4's *"400 MB"* import ceiling use doc 10's own 10⁶ convention, when that convention is scoped to §9?** §9 states it once and explicitly: *"MB means 10⁶ bytes **throughout §9**, and KB 10³"*, with `tools/ci/check-frontend-budgets.py` re-deriving §9.2's depth tables and §9.4's size budgets under it. §8.4's quota sentence sits outside that scope and states no unit, and the two readings differ by ~19 MB (400 × 10⁶ = 400,000,000 against 400 MiB = 419,430,400). `import-quota.ts` takes the **conservative** reading — the smaller bound, so a caller can never admit more than either reading permits — and that stands until this is ruled. What makes it worth a row rather than a shrug: §9.4 budgets a mobile tab 350 MB of memory in total under the 10⁶ reading, so the ceiling and the budget are already compared across a scope boundary (SQ-632), and a convention that holds *throughout §9* and nowhere else invites exactly the silent 5 % grant §9's own note was written to prevent
