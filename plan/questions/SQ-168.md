## Question

**PT-3's `[E − holders, E]` "holders" term is ambiguous** for one account holding several independently-rounded instrument classes. Implemented per terminal instrument family, with the full mixed D-1 schedule tested separately.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — both PT-3 texts now bound by `[E − r, E]` where `r` counts **independently floored residual redemptions per residual `PositionId`**, under a pair-first schedule, with exact pairs contributing 0. An account count was never a sound proxy: one account holding several instrument classes loses dust once per class.
