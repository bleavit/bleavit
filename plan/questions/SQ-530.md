## Question

**Four fail-soft treasury paths persisted deferred `MAIN` credit without discharging it, inflating NAV cumulatively.** `load()` folds `PendingMainCredit` into `main_usdc`; `persist()` was the only place that killed the counter afterwards. `pay_collator_compensation`, the zero-pay rebate branch, `do_keeper_rebate` and `do_proposer_reward` all write `State::<T>::put(state)` directly — deliberately, because they must not propagate a `checked_state` error — and every one dropped the discharge. The next `load()` therefore folded the same credit a second time. **Direction is the unsafe one**: NAV over-states custody, so `trs.cap_proposal`·NAV (hence the §5.2 security-sizing gate), `pol.budget_epoch`·NAV and the 08 §4.1 arming floors are all computed on capital the treasury does not hold. It compounds — `credit_main` runs in the fee handler of every USDC-paying extrinsic and `do_keeper_rebate` inside most cranks. `do_try_state` cannot catch it: no invariant ties `main_usdc` to real MAIN custody

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-30 (E5)** — one `put_folded_state` seam does both writes, so the discharge is structural rather than a rule to remember; all five call sites routed through it. Regression `a_rebate_payout_does_not_double_count_deferred_main_credit` reproduced it (counter survived at 1,000 USDC) and passes after
