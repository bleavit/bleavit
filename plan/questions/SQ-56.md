## Question

Are the 08 §4.1 minimum-viable-NAV arming floors FROZEN derived constants or LIVE-derived from `pol.budget_epoch` + `pol.b`? A9's `Treasury::floor` returns the 08 §4.1 literals; if live-derived, lowering `pol.budget_epoch` would make the loud 08 §4.2 gate pass below the true floor. 13 §5.6's re-derivation-trigger list omits both keys (supports "frozen"). Related to SQ-39 (08 §4.1 rounding).

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B4 ratification).** Ratified as **frozen**, but only together with the rider that makes the freeze safe. Code confirms frozen (`crates/futarchy-treasury-core/src/lib.rs:1050-1058` reads no `Params`), and 02 §13 v5 pins two of the values into `NavView.class_floors`. The hazard the row half-saw is real and inverted: `pol.budget_epoch` has min `Perbill(0)` and `max_delta: None`, and `pol.b.*` max `u128::MAX`, so one META decision can lower the budget or raise `b` and push the true floor **above** the frozen literal — the unsafe direction. 08 §4.1 gained a *Freeze and re-derivation* paragraph and 13 §5 item 6 gained the five POL keys as `RederiveBudgets` triggers. Live impact today is the FE distance-to-floor signal only, since the §4.2 gate is still unwired (SQ-180).
