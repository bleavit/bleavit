## Question

**Where does an attestor's bond come from, and may seating fail for want of it?** Blocks the attestor leg of **SQ-263**/**SQ-293**. Unlike the oracle's reporter/watchtower registries — permissionless self-registration, so "hold the stake at `register_*`" is unambiguous — the attestor registry is **values-elected**: `attestor.set_members` takes a bare member list (`pallets/attestor/src/lib.rs:205`) and is the only seating surface. Wiring real custody therefore forces a semantic choice the spec does not make: (a) seating **holds** `att.bond` from each nominee, so a values referendum that names an underfunded account **fails at enactment** — real custody, but a funding accident can block a lawful recall or roster change, and the failure lands on the values track; (b) nominees **pre-post** a bond via a new permissionless call and seating admits only pre-bonded accounts — preserves values-track liveness, but adds a call and a pre-bond state 02 §7.5 does not describe, and lets an attacker starve the roster by refusing to bond; (c) the bond stays arithmetic and enforcement moves elsewhere — the status quo this row exists to end. 06 §7 says "bonded registry, values-elected" and 13 §1's `att.bond` row says the bond "binds at seating", neither of which resolves the consent question. R-2 forbids inventing it. **Ruling needed before SQ-263's attestor leg can be implemented;** the oracle leg is unblocked and can proceed independently.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-23 (B19).** Option (a): `set_members` atomically holds each nominee's live `att.bond`; underfunded seating fails the entire values enactment and leaves the previous roster/custody unchanged. Continuing seats adjust their hold; departing seats release only after liabilities and records are gone.
