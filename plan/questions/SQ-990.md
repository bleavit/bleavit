## Question

**Does §8.4's second sampling arm survive the `balances` ruling?** §8.4 re-verifies a live indexer's rows *"against live chain state where the referenced object still exists, **or against the self-ingested overlap window**"*. With §8.5.2 (2026-08-07) ruling that a page's `balances` are state at the page's last block, the second arm has nothing to compare against — 10 §7's local index holds `events`, `priceSamples`, candles and `proposalsArchive`, and **no balances table**. So either the overlap arm is dead for indexers, or it compares something other than `balances`, or §7 gains a table. This is the arm that would make SQ-986's reading (i) workable, which is why it is not merely tidy-up: the overlap window is exactly the region where a page's `toBlock` is re-derivable.
