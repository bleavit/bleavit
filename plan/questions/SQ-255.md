## Question

**Dead-man resume: 05 §4.8 "open decision windows extend day-for-day" vs 05 §5.4 step-2 ProcessHold and market window registration.** decide() terminally rejects in-flight proposals during engagement per §5.4's pseudocode (pre-existing A8 reading), and shifted `decide_at` schedules need their registered market decision windows re-registered to the shifted boundary or sealing wedges (adversarial major). B10 remediation extends the registered windows to the shifted boundary on resume. Which straddle semantics does §4.8 intend?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B1 ratification, delegated ruling).** 05 §4.8 gained a normative *straddle semantics on resume* paragraph: "open decision windows extend day-for-day" binds the **registered market decision window**, not merely `decide_at`. On resume the pause duration shifts `decide_at` (Trading/Extended) and `maturity`/`grace_end` (Queued/Suspended/FailedExecuted), and each affected proposal's registered window is **extended in place** to the shifted boundary — code-verification corrected the row's wording here: the mechanism is an in-place end shift that refuses already-sealed windows, not a re-registration. The §5.4 tension is resolved by scope: step 2's `ProcessHold` governs proposals decided *while engaged*; the extension governs windows surviving to a post-resume decision.
