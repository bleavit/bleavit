## Question

**Does 08 §4.3 mandate instantiating a Baseline book in an epoch with zero qualified proposals, or only that the Baseline's funding is independent of `pol.budget_epoch`?** 08 §4.3 stated the Baseline TWAP "must exist even in an epoch with zero qualified proposals", while the runtime creates the book lazily from the first qualified proposal's `open_markets` call. **Resolved 2026-07-24:** the standing logical Baseline obligation and its dedicated funding line exist independently of proposal shrink-to-fit, but physical Baseline provisioning is conditional: a zero-qualified epoch has no book/vault/mapping or charge. This is consistent with 05 §5.3's carry fallback and §7(6)'s explicit absent-vault no-op; the one-book capacity envelope remains a conservative bound for epochs that open markets.
