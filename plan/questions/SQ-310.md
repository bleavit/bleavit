## Question

**Does T12's rerun opening deserve its own window, or does it correctly inherit `grd.review_dl`?** 05 §2.1 T12's trigger is \"guardian review window closed\" and no such window is defined anywhere in the doc set; the runtime binds it to the same `deadline_epoch` that governs the 06 §5.4(3) slash. That coupling is what made 06 §5.4's ordering paragraph false in all three of its clauses. The SQ-45(c) ruling separates the two by one epoch and repairs **two** of the three clauses — the rerun does then precede the deadline, and the deadline does govern slashing and recall only — but the first clause stays false, because T12 would still fire at `action_epoch + grd.review_dl` rather than at `action_epoch + 1`. Closing that needs a purpose-built window, which is this row. A shorter purpose-built review window would return the proposer's rerun sooner without shortening guardian exposure.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-23 (B18)** — purpose-built `GuardianReviewWindows` opens T12 at the first Seed of `action_epoch + 1`; `GuardianReviewDeadlines` remains the separate accountability/slashing horizon. Migration, strict-boundary tests and runtime T12 integration are green.
