## Question

**15 §4.6's `ledger.redeem_fee` obligation contradicts itself.** Its first bullet assigns the key the `param-bounds` treatment `mkt.fee` and `gate.v_min` already carry; its second demands a `// limit-coverage:` **marked** test. The gate rejects a marker on any non-`dispatch-limit` key (the SQ-495 `orc.bond_bps` precedent), so the two cannot both be satisfied. The substantive both-direction obligation *is* discharged, by a named unmarked test
