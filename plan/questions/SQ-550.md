## Question

**Two of the four published Phase-0 false-pass rates cannot be distinguished from failure by their own sample.** The rates carry no interval and 15 §4.9 states no minimum denominator. Exact one-sided 95 % Clopper–Pearson upper bounds from the committed artifact's integer counts: **PARAM 0/168 → 1.767 %** and **META 4/711 → 1.283 %**, both **above** the < 1 % gate (TREASURY 0.688 %, CODE 0.638 %). PARAM's denominator is small precisely because the δ floor was raised ×2.5 during calibration — the lever shrinks the denominator that measures it. The remedy is sampling, not a failed gate: `required_n` gives PARAM 299 / TREASURY 473 / CODE 473 / META 1,568, and the current deterministic generator yields 1,786 PARAM decidable-harm observations at 10⁵ proposals. **`inference.py` computes all of this and is wired into nothing** — making a bound gate-relevant is a normative 15 §4.9 change. **Ruling needed:** a minimum per-class denominator or an explicit confidence-bound reading, and whether Phase 0 re-runs at ≥ 10⁵
