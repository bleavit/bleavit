## Question

**Is 11 E17's `R: retry AH sync` satisfied by a control that joins an attach already in flight, or must a retry be an independent attempt?** 11 §11.9.1 and 02 §7.7 both make the Asset Hub surface fail closed with diagnostics and name retry as the recovery action, and neither says which of the two a retry is. The distinction has teeth: joining is right for a *slow* attach and wrong for a *wedged* one, and the client cannot tell them apart. F27 implements — and this row asks the spec to ratify — a renewed wait while the attempt is live, and a fresh attempt once it has been abandoned at its bound, with the abandoned attempt detaching its chain and closing any transport it goes on to open.
