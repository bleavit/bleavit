## Question

**08 §2.6 says a debit "suspends the participant until they top up", and does not say what suspension suspends.** TR4's accumulator gates scoring on `bond > 0`, so a **sub-minimum** top-up resumes scoring while the `suspended` flag stays set — the flag and the behavior it names come apart. Nothing reads the flag today, so nothing is wrong yet, and that is exactly why it needs settling before TR5 attaches a penalty to it: a penalty on a flag that a partial top-up already escaped would be laundered by the cheapest available action.

## Status

resolved 2026-08-10 — **suspension suspends scoring, and the accumulator must read the flag.** The admission condition is a **conjunction**, not either half: a nonzero bond **and** no suspension. Neither side implies the other. TR3 clears `suspended` only at the minimum bond `enroll` demands, so a sub-minimum top-up leaves a suspended account holding a nonzero bond — the balance gate alone resumes scoring while the flag still says suspended. And a voluntary `withdraw_bond` retains the record at zero bond without ever setting the flag, so the flag gate alone would score an account with no bond behind it. The conjunction is stated rather than derived because the earning cap is **not** the backstop here: a zero cap makes the reward *round* to zero, which is an accident of the floor rather than a refusal, and it is the same argument TR2's review already retired once. 08 §2.6 amended; the code change is TR5 obligation 6, alongside obligation 4's warning that any penalty attached to `suspended` inherits a free reset
