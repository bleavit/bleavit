## Question

07 §5.4 freezes "the only admissible call is `oracle.adjudicate(round_id, verdict)`", but the shipped dispatchable (per 07 §13's own call list and 02 §7.2's triple key) is `adjudicate(component, epoch, spec_version, value, reporter_wrong)` — the triple is the round key and the verdict decomposes into the settled value plus the reporter-slash boolean. Should §5.4's shorthand be amended to the shipped signature (doc-internal inconsistency), or is a literal `(round_id, verdict)` call intended? RB-ORACLE cites the shipped shape and flags the divergence.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-21 (batch D)** — 07 §5(4)/§13, 06 §2.1/§2.3/§3.2 and 11 §11.7.5 now carry the shipped `oracle.adjudicate(component, epoch, spec_version, value, reporter_wrong)`; the RB-ORACLE divergence notes are retired. Two row claims corrected: 07 §13 listed only the call *name*, and `reporter_wrong` is a reporter-**fault finding**, not a slash (the first offense slashes nothing).
