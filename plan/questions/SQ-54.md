## Question

08 §2.3 caps issuance at `iss.inflation_cap × supply-at-window-start`. Earlier A9 used the **live** `iss.inflation_cap` against a **snapshot** supply, so a mid-window down-amendment could make the I-7 `try_state` envelope fail release-blocking.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-15** (code, PR #51 Codex round): the trailing-window meter (SQ-50) removes the snapshot; `try_state` now checks against the **fixed kernel ceiling** `ISS_INFLATION_CAP_BPS` (2%, the down-only bound), so a mid-window cut can't retroactively break the invariant — no release-block
