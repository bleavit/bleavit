## Question

**`Referenda.ReferendumInfoFor`'s terminal variants carry no tally, and two documents promise one.** Raised by adversarial review of the contract-v24 batch (2026-08-04) and verified against the real metadata: `tally` occurs **exactly once** in the decoded value, inside `Ongoing`. `Approved`/`Rejected`/`Cancelled`/`TimedOut` carry the decision block and two optional `Deposit`s; `Killed` carries only the block. But 11 §11.7.2's read table says "terminal entries carry final tally", and 02 §7.6 repeated it until this row was raised (now corrected in 02). **So the FE-14 governance surface is specified to display a figure the storage item cannot supply** — a screen promised data that does not exist, which is the same shape as a client refusing an action the runtime would accept. **Ruling needed:** where a decided referendum's tally comes from — the `Referenda` events (which are inside smoldot's pinned-block window only, so historical referenda would have none), a separate read, or 11 §11.7.2 drops the claim and the screen shows the outcome without the numbers. The third is the only option that needs no new surface, and it is a product decision rather than a mechanical one
