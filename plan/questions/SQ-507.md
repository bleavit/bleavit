## Question

**Eight `ops.*` budget lines are unsizeable from any evidence in this repository.** `ops.bootnodes`, `ops.rpc_archive`, `ops.oracle_evidence`, `ops.watchtowers`, `ops.monitoring`, `ops.arweave`, the `broker.renew` price component of `ops.coretime`, and the `ORACLE` line all carry `[VERIFY — sized in Phase-2/3 ops planning; ops-gated]`. What each MUST fund is specified as a **count** (12 §6.1: ≥ 8 WSS endpoints across ≥ 4 operators on each of two networks, ≥ 4 RPC + ≥ 2 archive serving 30 days of state, ≥ 2 bonded watchtowers, ≥ 2 keeper operators, ≥ 2 monitor operators, 3-of-5 ArNS custody), but an exhaustive sweep of `docs/`, `deploy/`, `tools/`, `zombienet/`, `keeper/`, `crates/` and `runtime/` found **no hardware, CPU, RAM, disk, bandwidth, headcount or unit-cost figure anywhere**. **Ruling: they stay `[VERIFY]`.** Inventing a number is the fabrication R-2 forbids, and the error direction is unsafe — understating `C` is what would let the project claim self-funding it has not reached. 08 §10.1 states the derivable subtotal as a **floor** and brackets it with two stated overlays (+0.5M, +1.5M/yr) so the sensitivity is visible. Separately unresolved: the coretime **bulk-period length** appears nowhere, so renewals-per-year cannot be derived either
