## Question

**Should Bleavit price congestion?** SQ-528 was fixed with `ConstFeeMultiplier` at 1.0 — exactly the model 08 §9 documents, fee proportional to weight with `fee.vit_usdc_rate`'s bounded [0.1×, 10×] envelope as the only governed variation. The alternative, `SlowAdjustingFeeUpdate`, is what most production parachains run and is a genuinely better spam deterrent under load. It was **not** adopted because it is a policy addition rather than a defect fix: it introduces a second, unbudgeted source of fee movement that no spec section owns, that `keeper.rebate`'s derivation would have to track, and that needs its own `TargetBlockFullness` parameter and 13 §1 row. Also unresolved and adjacent: `WeightToFee = IdentityFee` prices **`ref_time` only**, so `proof_size` — the dimension a PoV-bound parachain actually congests on — is never charged at all (`record_snapshot` carries 645,546 B of proof, ≈ 12 % of the PoV budget, and pays nothing for it)

## Status

**open** — deliberate deferral, recorded so the non-adoption is a decision rather than an omission
