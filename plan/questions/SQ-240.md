## Question

**12 §6.3 table 1 "Proposal state" row — which queue is "queue at bound"?** The row's runbook is RB-INTAKE, and 05 §T1 ("Intake phase only; queue < 64") plus 14 TH-16's "queue telemetry" mitigation both point at the 64-entry intake queue; the execution queue (bound 32) is a different lifecycle stage. O5 bound the alert to the intake queue (`Epoch.IntakeProposals` vs its bound) after the spec review flagged the original execution-queue binding as incoherent with the runbook; execution-queue occupancy stays covered by the 80% storage rule. Confirm the intended queue, or amend the row to name it.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B5 ratification; intake queue confirmed, row amended to name it).** Verification confirms the alert reads `Epoch::IntakeProposals` against `Epoch::MaxIntakeQueue`, matching RB-INTAKE, 05 §T1 and 14 TH-16. Rather than merely confirming, the cell now reads **"intake queue at bound"** so the ambiguity cannot return, and 12 §6.3 records that the execution-guard queue (bound 32) is a different lifecycle stage covered by the Storage row's 80%-of-bound rule. Synced through RB-INTAKE frontmatter/body and the rule's `threshold` annotation. Recorded nuance: two distinct pallet constants share the name `MaxLiveProposals` (13's row scopes Epoch's post-qualification non-terminal `Proposals` map; the execution guard has its own), so the spec text names the bound rather than the 13 row.
