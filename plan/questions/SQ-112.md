## Question

The 09 §6.1 fee row mandates a `WeightTrader` "selling execution for DOT or USDC at governed rates", but 13 §1 has no parameter rows for these rates (`fee.vit_usdc_rate` is the tx-payment side, owned by 08; stable2603 `Weight` is 2-dimensional, so each asset needs a ref-time and a proof-size rate). B4 ships the `TraderRates` seam; B1a cannot bind it to `pallet-constitution::Params` until 13 §1 owns keys/defaults/bounds for them. Which keys (e.g. `xcm.trade_dot_ref`/`xcm.trade_dot_pov`/`xcm.trade_usdc_ref`/`xcm.trade_usdc_pov`), what defaults, what class?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-17** — 13 §1 owns `xcm.dot_per_sec`/`xcm.dot_per_mb`/`xcm.usdc_per_sec`/`xcm.usdc_per_mb` with exact tested metadata; the runtime's `ConstitutionTraderRates` reads `Params` live and backs the two-dimensional `GovernedWeightTrader`.
