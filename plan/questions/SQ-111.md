## Question

`phase3.tvl_cap` base mismatch: 09 §5.2 defines it over "total USDC held by the ledger + treasury sovereign accounts", but the XCM-leg enforcement point sees deposits crediting *user* accounts, which move neither. B4's seam guidance compares **total local USDC issuance** (strictly tighter, status-quo-safe: issuance ⊇ ledger+treasury holdings). Under the literal base no user deposit could ever breach the cap at the inflow leg — which would make 09 §5.2's "enforced … at the XCM inflow leg" vacuous. Confirm the issuance base (and amend 09 §5.2's wording) or define what the inflow leg should compare.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-17** — amended 09 §5.2 uses total local USDC issuance; the runtime adapter reads real `ForeignAssets` issuance and `pallet-inflow-caps::mint_admissible` enforces the live constitution cap without writes.
