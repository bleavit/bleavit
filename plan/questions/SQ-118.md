## Question

08 §6.3's "decision-window observations" classification needs the owning pair's decision window (final 72 h, `dec.window`), which `pallet-market` does not own — the window is epoch/proposal state (05/A8). B9 adds an `InDecisionWindow` Config seam to `pallet-market`; the runtime binds a pending **always-false** adapter (⇒ every observation rebates as General) until the open B1a A8 wiring lands and `pallet-epoch` can answer it. Conservative for the reserved tranche but under-rebates the decision-critical observation load (the 20% general cap starves observation rebates at scale). Bind with the A8 runtime wiring.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (triage sweep — code-verified).** The pending always-false adapter is gone: `RuntimeInDecisionWindow` is a real `Contains<MarketId>` reader bound at `type InDecisionWindow` (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:2712-2713, 2767`), so decision-window observations rebate against the reserved ≥80 % tranche as 08 §6.3 intends rather than starving in the General cap.
