## Question

Guardian playbook/timing semantics the frame-free core can't fully model. (a) **Per-playbook expiry:** 06 §6.2 gives each playbook a distinct expiry (PB-DEPEG ≤ 1 epoch, PB-MIGRATION "until migration resolves", PB-ORACLE-VOID one-shot, others ≤ 14 d), but the core applies one uniform 14-day (`HOLD_MAX_BLOCKS`) cap + pure time-expiry — a *safe* under-approximation (never longer than spec) but not exact; exact bounds need `epoch.length`/trigger-flag context only the pallet can read. Must the guardian *pallet* enforce per-playbook expiry, or is the uniform cap acceptable for v1? (b) **PB-LEDGER-FREEZE renewal** (06 §6.3): is "14 further days" measured from the renewal block (as implemented: `expiry = now + 14 d`) or from the original expiry, and where does "auto-lift early when the I-4 drift flag clears" live — guardian pallet (not modeled; expiry is time-only) or the ledger pallet? (c) **Review-deadline boundary** (06 §5.4): "within 2 epochs" is implemented as slash-when `epoch > dispatch_epoch + 2` (inclusive through the end of epoch e+2) — confirm the intended inclusive/exclusive edge.

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-23 (B15/B18)** — (a) the one-shot trigger is no longer used as `void_in_flight` (B15/SQ-233), (b) renewal is measured from the renewal block and the bounded expiry rules are enforced, and (c) the review failure comparison is strict `current_epoch > action_epoch + grd.review_dl`, with the benchmark and boundary regressions updated.
