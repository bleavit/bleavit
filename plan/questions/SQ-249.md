## Question

**07 §6 in-flight binding of the oracle bond schedule and R_max.** Do `orc.bond_floor`/`orc.bond_bps`/`orc.rounds` bind per game at round-1 creation (B_r = frozen B_1·2^(r−1), ladder length fixed at open) or read live per escalation? Live reading lets a legal amendment retroactively under-collateralize an admitted metric (adversarial blocker) and breaks try-state on legally reachable states. B10 remediation freezes {B_1, R_max} per game at open. Ratify the freeze (07 §13's freezing language currently covers StakeAtRisk only).

## Status
