## Question

**13 §5's pinned PoV measurement for `settle_cohort` is stale.** 13 §5 item 1 quoted the superseded `settle_cohort(5)` estimate 359,385 B (the old 35,266-B slope). A fresh pinned 50×20 benchmark measured the current generated estimate `183,055 + n × 30,754`, i.e. **336,825 B at n = 5**; the normative doc, runtime PoV comment and exact proof-size regression now agree. The 768-KiB worst-case bound remains unchanged, and no production behavior or contract surface changed.
