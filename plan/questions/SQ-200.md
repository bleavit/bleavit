## Question

**05 §3.3 retention arithmetic vs the 20-snapshot window:** "reap is a precondition for pruning snapshot e−20" read with cohort e reaping at e+3 (snapshots through e+2 live) implies ≥ 22 retained epochs — unsatisfiable at `MAX_SNAPSHOTS` = 20 without a free slot. The B4-residual seam implements cutoff `current − 19` (removes exactly ≤ e−20, retains 19, one slot free — regression-tested; the initially-shipped `current − 20` cutoff deadlocked settlement at ~epoch 21 and was the focused re-review's blocker). Which is authoritative: the e−20 index (⇒ raise `MAX_SNAPSHOTS` ≥ 22) or the 20-deep window (⇒ restate the sentence's arithmetic)? Does "e" mean the cohort epoch or the settling epoch?

## Status

**resolved 2026-07-20 (B1 ratification, delegated ruling).** 05 §3.3's retention sentence was replaced with normative arithmetic. Ruling: the **20-deep window governs** (`MAX_SNAPSHOTS`, 13 §4 — raising it is a storage-bound change with PoV cost), and the ambiguous "snapshot e−20" phrasing is restated against the **current epoch index at prune time**: pruning removes every snapshot with index `<= current − 20`, i.e. the cutoff is **`current − 19`**, retaining 19 and leaving exactly one free slot for the epoch's own record. The doc now states outright why the alternative is unsafe — a `current − 20` cutoff retains a full window and permanently jams `record_snapshot` (which requires strict spare capacity), deadlocking settlement into the dead-man. The "does `e` mean the cohort or settling epoch" ambiguity is removed by anchoring on the current index.
