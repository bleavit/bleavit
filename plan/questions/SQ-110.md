## Question

09 §5.2's "excess inbound reserve transfers fail politely, funds remain on Asset Hub" is only partially realizable at the XCM layer. B4 enforces the **global** cap at the *mint* leg (`ReserveAssetDeposited` → `mint_asset`), so an over-`tvl_cap` transfer fails on its first instruction with nothing minted and nothing trapped locally — but the sender's USDC has already moved into our sovereign account *on Asset Hub* by then (the reserve leg runs there first), so "remains on Asset Hub" means "sits in our AH sovereign account pending recovery", not "stays with the sender". The **per-account** cap is only checkable at the deposit leg (beneficiary unknown at mint), where a rejection traps the minted holding — recoverable per the amended 09 §6.1 trapped-assets row. Is trap-plus-self-scoped-recovery (+ FE pre-check, 11) the accepted "polite" failure?
