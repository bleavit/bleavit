## Question

**Guardian accountability saturates to zero and nothing refuses a zero-bond member.** 06 §5.4's fixed 50 %-of-seat-bond slash gives the ladder **50,000 → 25,000 → 0 → 0 VIT**: after two failed reviews a member's marginal slash is zero forever, and `ensure_member` is the only gate at `propose_action`/`approve_action`, so a fully depleted guardian keeps proposing and approving. Lifetime coalition accountability caps at 12,500 USDC at the documented `[VERIFY]` 0.05 USDC/VIT (the whole 7-seat stack is 17,500 USDC; 1,750–175,000 across the lawful rate envelope). Separately, 06 §6.3's unqualified "total freeze duration ≤ 28 days" holds **per activation**, not incident-wide: two fresh post-expiry activations have a 56-day aggregate budget and no incident-level activation count exists. And the "7-day decision fits inside the first expiry window" parenthetical is literally true but incomplete — submission-to-enactment on the guardian track is **11 days** (1 prepare + 7 decision + 1 confirm + 2 enactment), leaving **3 days** of renewal slack, not 7. Recall latency of a captured coalition is 74 days at defaults and 221 days at the lawful maximum, and if values governance rejects recall the spec supplies **no finite removal bound**. **Ruling needed:** name the slash basis, add a membership floor tied to remaining bond, and scope the freeze bound. **CORRECTED 2026-08-01 by adversarial review of PR #206, before any ruling was taken.** The "depletes to zero in two failures" claim silently assumed a **fixed original-seat-bond** basis. 06 §5.4(3) says "slashed **50 % of bond**", and on the live-held-bond reading the ladder is 50,000 → 25,000 → 12,500 → … and **never reaches zero**. The finding is therefore the *ambiguity of the basis*, plus the composition that survives either reading: `ensure_member` is the only gate at `propose_action`/`approve_action`, so nothing refuses a bond-depleted member
