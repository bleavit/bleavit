## Question

**Does the oracle bond base-unit product round up?** `B_1 = max(orc.bond_floor, stake_at_risk × orc.bond_bps / 10,000)` floors the base-unit product (pre-existing); the B10 remediation made the Perbill→bps conversion ceil but left the base-unit flooring. A claimant-adverse reading would ceil both.

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B3): **ruled ceil both.** 07 §6.1 wrote `orc.bond_bps × StakeAtRisk` with neither a `/10,000` divisor nor a rounding direction — both were unspecified, so this fixes them together: new *Units and rounding* paragraph makes the division and any parameter-representation conversion round **up**, resolved against the bonded party (a bond rounded down weakens exactly the deterrence §6 supplies); `max(·, orc.bond_floor)` applied after rounding; §6.3 admission is bps-space and unaffected either way. 13 §1 restates it. B10 already made the Perbill→bps conversion ceil (`runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:1397-1409`), so this completes that direction rather than reversing it. **Leaves a one-line code obligation:** `crates/oracle-core/src/lib.rs:1273-1277` still floors the base-unit product → **SQ-289** (X).
