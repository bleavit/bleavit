## Question

**Clamp/no-op/fail-soft limits cannot "assert the specific error".** 15 §4.6 mandates tests that "drive a dispatch past it and assert the specific error", but several normative limits enforce without any error by design: κ slew clamp + observation-cadence no-op (04 §7), keeper-budget fail-soft zero-pay latch (08 §6.3), FIFO ring eviction (`RecentCohortSummaries`, `ExecutionRecords`), bounded-progress `ReapBatch` (item 101 left for the next crank), latch-once extensions (`dec.extension`, `orc.ext_window`), neutral settlement at `OracleSettleDeadline`. S3's manifest distinguishes `error` (Module error asserted) from `behavior` (the specified boundary behavior asserted, lexically bound to the test body) — exactly one required per dispatch-limit key. Confirm this reading or amend §4.6's wording to "assert the specific error or specified boundary behavior".

## Status

resolved 2026-07-20 (batch B6): error-or-behaviour ratified in 15 §4.6 **and** the I-22 row, exactly one binding per key; the lexical anchor may itself be an error variant for a behaviour-classified limit, so the text does not claim the marker names the behaviour
