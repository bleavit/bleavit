## Question

The frozen `Proposals` map (02 §7.1, ≤32) holds only the post-qualification pipeline; A8 keeps pre-qualification `Submitted` proposal structs in a non-frozen `IntakeProposals` map (only their ids in the frozen `IntakeQueue`). Is id-only FE exposure of pre-qual proposals intended, or should 02/13 name a readable item?

## Status

resolved 2026-07-21 (batch C) — **closed as intentional.** Id-only pre-qualification exposure is consistent across 02 §7.1 (`IntakeQueue`, "pre-qualification scope only"), 11 §11.2 (submission reads queue length, not bodies) and the pallet, which documents `IntakeProposals` as an internal mirror outside the 02 surface. No ambiguity remains; a future pre-qual detail surface would be a new contract feature.
