## Question

**RULED 2026-08-05 (R-1), and the question as first filed was partly wrong — the correction is the useful part.** *As filed:* the 11 §11.12 degradation matrix is "25 rows by name and 12 by text", with E1–E14 pointing at doc 10 and doc 10 carrying nothing. *What a wider search found:* **15 §3.3 enumerates all fourteen by name** — E1 first visit desktop · E2 first visit mobile · E3 returning visit (gap-tolerant) · E4 after runtime upgrade · E5 deep link to an old proposal · E6 active market · E7 ten-month chart · E8 all proposals by an address · E9 IndexedDB corruption · E10 all providers down · E11 preferred gateway down · E12 slow peer discovery · E13 signing while chain advances · E14 obsolete release — and SQ-1 already ruled that **11 §11.12 is canonical and 15 §3.3 is its index**. So the row *set* was never lost; I filed on an incomplete search, which is exactly the under-research R-1 warns a deferred decision usually is. **The real defect is narrower and still real, in two parts.** (i) The **facet texts** (V/L/A/U/F/R) for E1–E14 exist nowhere — 15 §3.3 calls them *"self-describing, review-confirmed"*, which is why nobody wrote them out, and *self-describing* is not a specification a client can be held to. (ii) **11 §11.12's pointer is a citation defect**: it says their texts *"live with the data-layer they exercise in [10]"*, and doc 10 carries no E-row in facet form at all — only E3's correction, which explicitly disclaims ownership. **Ruling:** the facet texts are authored into **11 §11.12**, the canonical matrix 15 §3.3 already names, derived from what 10 §3.2/§6/§8.3 and 11 already specify about each failure mode; §11.12's doc-10 pointer is repaired to a *reasoning* reference rather than a text-ownership claim. Not doc 10, because the defect is that the matrix has two homes and a countable matrix needs one — nothing counted the rows, and that is what let fourteen sit unwritten. **F12 is unblocked by the ruling** and its remaining work is the authoring plus a checker binding the client registry to the section
