**Done 2026-08-03.** BigInt 64.64 port of the guarded-Q96 kernel (`exp2`/`log2`/`ln`, 32 guard bits, one final rounding), the stable log-sum-exp LMSR (cost, prices, buy/sell, displacement, `48·b` domain), the **base-unit quote pipeline** (`fx` floor → ×10⁶ truncate → maker-adverse ceil/floor → `feeUp`, `MinTrade`/`MaxTrade`), and the 1e9-grid TWAP (κ-slew with I-13 inward rounding, backward weighting, window staleness). Reproduces the runtime's integer path rather than out-precisioning it, because 04 §6.1 step 4 refuses a `max_cost` below the chain's own charge — a more accurate answer is the wrong answer here. **Every tunable is an argument**: `mkt.fee`, `mkt.kappa`, `mkt.obs_interval`, `MinTrade`, `MaxTradeRatio` have no defaults in the package (15 §5.4). **39 tests, all against `reference-model/fixtures/vectors.json` read in place** — V1–V6, the 9-sample high-precision corpus, all 1,286 transcendental rows at 04 §4's four *distinct* bounds, both TWAP scenarios, all 5 window-staleness scenarios, and the three 15 §4.8 properties. Plus a **second, cross-language gate**: `crates/market-core/fixtures/chain-quote-agreement.json`, written by the runtime's own `quote()`, checked on the Rust side and consumed by `app/tests/protocol/chain-agreement.test.js` — 45 tests total. It exists because the corpus certifies *mathematics* and is blind to the layer above, and it immediately proved the point: **both defects it found had passed all 1,286 corpus rows.** (i) the port *refused* an order above `MaxTrade` where `market-core::quote` prices it and publishes `max_trade` as data — 02 §4 / 11 §11.5 P-1 keep quote and precondition apart precisely so a client can say \"your order exceeds the cap, and here is what the cap would cost\"; (ii) an oversized sell reported `PriceBoundExceeded` where the runtime underflows a `Balance` first and returns `ArithmeticOverflow`. Both fixed; the raw kernel values agreed bit-for-bit throughout. **Anti-vacuity proved on 10 mutations** (exp2 ±1 ulp, slew rounding outward, fee down, charge down, domain 48→49, reviver disabled, corpus absent, both original defects re-injected, sell fee added not withheld) — each caught, baseline restored to 0. Two findings recorded: V-74 (the corpus's raw 64.64 integers exceed 2⁵³ and `JSON.parse` corrupts them) and the Decision-log ruling on `LMSR_DOMAIN_BOUND`. One observation logged in the fixture module: `QuoteView.within_domain` is never `false` on the `Ok` path, because the cost is computed first and a domain-leaving buy fails with `PriceBoundExceeded` before the flag is reached — not a contract violation, but a client must not read it as the domain check
