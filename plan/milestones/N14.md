**Done 2026-08-03.** Spec 16 §8.6 + the `svc.price_cap` registry row + `ScarcityMultiplier` in `pallet-question-service` + the runtime `price_cap()` reader + 7 tests; ships **inert** (`M ≡ 1`, bit-identical to pre-N14) until the ceiling is adopted. Decay is applied lazily on read rather than by a hook — nothing but `register` consumes the value, and a hook would spend block weight every block maintaining a number one extrinsic reads. The step is **additive**, `(cap − 1)/max_live`, not the geometric root the first draft wrote: a fractional root is a transcendental and runtime rule 2 admits none outside `futarchy-fixed`, while additive reaches the ceiling at full occupancy identically in exact integer arithmetic. Two things were *withdrawn* during implementation and both are recorded rather than quietly dropped — the direct-to-POL premium routing and the SQ-574 depth-fee scope enlargement (below). User ruling 2026-08-03, on the user's own observation that a fixed schedule cannot see demand. **The asymmetry is the whole mechanism.** Pure occupancy pricing is a state function with no memory of velocity: sixteen slots filled over three weeks and sixteen filled in one block price identically, and a bot taking a freed slot pays exactly what a patient buyer would. Making only the *downward* move gradual — so a released slot descends from the pre-release level rather than dropping to the new one — is what turns immediacy into something a sniper pays for, and it is simultaneously the per-slot Dutch auction, the velocity term and the demand-discovery loop. **There is deliberately no start-price parameter:** each descent begins from the running price, which is the accumulated record of prior demand. Same family as EIP-1559's base fee and on-demand coretime's spot price; one stored value plus a lazy decay, not a broker pallet. Degenerates to today's posted floor whenever capacity is spare, so sub-saturation behaviour cannot move. **`svc.max_live` stays a hard governance-set cap the mechanism can never sell past — price clears, quantity does not**, because a market-discovered quantity would make scarcity profitable and that is exactly the pressure TH-73 warns about. The whole scaled fee is credited at `seal` (never earlier — it is refunded on VOID) and lands in `MAIN` with the rest of instrument D; **the direct-to-POL routing an earlier revision specified was withdrawn during implementation**, on the finding that it needs this pallet to name Bleavit's protocol subsidy custody account and so opens a second money path into a custody §7.2–§7.5 leave with exactly one — the reasoning survived, the route did not, and `MAIN` is where 08 §1.1 funds POL from anyway. **The SQ-574 scope enlargement was withdrawn too, and for a harder reason: the claim under it was false.** It read that the fee prices depth at zero because one base unit of declared stake buys the flat 393-USDC floor while `b` has no upper bound — but a hosted question must escrow `2·seed_headroom(b)`, which the Phase-3 TVL cap bounds at `b ≈ 1,442,695` USDC, and the module raising the finding had computed that escrow all along. What survives is narrower and does not motivate a fee leg: **the fee is flat in depth; the capital is not.** N14 therefore ships as one mechanism with the job the user actually named — allocation and sniping under contention. Derivation anchor and its caveat are in the Decision log

<!-- source track narrative -->

Opened 2026-08-01 on the "make the futarchy infrastructure accessible to other parachains,
smart contracts and services via XCM" work order. Bleavit hosts conditional decision markets
for external clients and **sells price discovery, not decisions**: it publishes the conditional
TWAPs with provenance plus a manipulation-cost bound, and the client's own on-chain rule decides
and executes locally. Bleavit never runs foreign code, and no external state reaches any Bleavit
decision, welfare or settlement input. v1 scope by user decision — the spec batch, contract bump
and threat rows land together. Full approved design: `.claude/plans/` (session plan, 2026-08-01).
