**Why this is worth a milestone: it widens the proposal funnel without touching any pricing mechanism.** Proposing today requires being *both* technically capable of authoring a payload *and* able to lock 1,000–50,000 USDC. The chain's whole output is ~87 decisions/year (08 §10.4) and every one of them is gated on finding a single party at that intersection. **The split is already half-representable in storage**: `ProposalBond { proposer, held }` is a record distinct from `Proposal.proposer`, and the refund already releases to `bond.proposer` (`pallets/epoch/src/lib.rs:3116`) — one identity is custodial, the other authorial, and only `submit`'s equality check forbids them differing. **Four incidence rulings (user, 2026-07-31):** `trs.proposer_reward` → **author**; pre-qualification withdrawal → **either** party; the 06 §4 10 % non-decision-grade slash → **funder**; intake cap → re-keyed to the **funder**. **The fourth is a defect closure, not a preference, and the change is a net regression without it:** `crates/epoch-core/src/lib.rs:776` counts the ≤4-entries/epoch cap on `p.proposer == proposal.proposer` — the *author*. That is harmless only because `submit` currently forces author == signer. Split the identities and leave the key alone, and one funder backs 16 throwaway author addresses at 4 proposals each to take the entire 64-entry `IntakeQueue`, voiding 06 §4 rule 4 and the B-13 monopolization defense outright. Capital at risk is what prices spam, so the cap follows the capital. **The reward ruling needs no new code and must be pinned rather than assumed:** `runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/configs.rs:8381` already pays `&proposal.proposer`, so keeping `proposer` authorial satisfies it by construction — which is exactly the kind of property that silently regresses without a test. **Author-only rights stay author-only:** `bind_ratification` (`pallets/epoch/src/lib.rs:1955`) is the ratification identity 09 §1.1(4) requires the *proposer* to freeze, and nothing about funding touches it. **Contract:** `Proposal` and `ProposalSummaryView` each gain a trailing `funder: AccountId` (02 §13 rule 3 append — no existing field's name, type or offset moves), `INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_VERSION` 17 → **18**; no call index moves so `transaction_version` is unchanged, and the chain is pre-genesis so no migration is required. **No new 13 key**: `intake.max_acct` keeps its value and bounds, only its *subject* changes, so this milestone carries no R-2 parameter derivation. Verify: the sybil regression first (N funders × M authors driven against the cap — it MUST fail on the pre-fix keying and pass after, or the defect closure is unevidenced); withdrawal admitted for author and for funder and refused for a third party; the 10 % slash lands on the funder with the author's balance untouched; the reward lands on the author; `bind_ratification` refuses the funder; try-state binding every `ProposalBond.proposer` to a live proposal's funder; `pallet_epoch` weights regenerated for `submit`'s widened record. **Done 2026-07-31 — PR #201.** All eight legs discharged; nine tests, every one split-identity. Two-sided mutation proof on the cap (reverting the predicate fails *both* cap tests: the evasion succeeds in one and distinct funders are wrongly capped in the other); one-sided on the reward (routing to `&proposal.funder` fails it). The reward leg needed a **PARAM** fixture — TREASURY/CODE pay `min(0.05 %·Ask, 25k)` = 0 at the `ask = 0` every existing execution fixture carries, and a payout of zero pins no recipient; PARAM's flat `trs.reward.param` is `ask`-independent.
