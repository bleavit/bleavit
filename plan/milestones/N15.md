**Done 2026-08-03.** 16 §8.7 + `ContestHealthProbe` + the runtime probe over Bleavit's live decision pairs + 6 pallet tests + 1 runtime probe test, sharing N14's ceiling so **one adopted row arms both halves of `M`** and N15 is inert on exactly N14's condition. **The mechanism moves the price, not the quantity, and that is the finding rather than a shortcut.** A quantity latch was designed first and rejected twice over: a *binary* one creates the race it exists to prevent (operators register before the cliff), and *any* quantity latch grandfathers incumbents — locking entrants out while live questions run makes a slot-holder's position more valuable exactly when Bleavit is damaged, and 16 §8.4 prices damaging Bleavit's books at round-trip fees. Under a price response there is no exclusivity to win and the incumbent's own next question costs the same raised price, so starving Bleavit buys the attacker nothing; `svc.max_live` still bounds the harm structurally, unchanged. Three implementation choices are load-bearing: the measure divides the 04 §7a contest integral by the blocks **actually accrued** rather than the full window width (dividing a partial integral by the full width reports every young window as starved, which would fire the latch at the start of every epoch); the two halves combine by `max`, not a product, so `M ≤ cap` holds by construction instead of needing a second registry row to bound `cap²`; and the starvation reading is **never stored**, which forced `raise_scarcity` to ratchet `contention_multiplier` rather than the combined value — otherwise one admission during a transient starvation would bake it into the stored term and decay it out slowly over `svc.max_window`, hysteresis outliving its cause. Benchmark honesty note: `prime_register_scan` pointed every fixture proposal at markets 1 and 2, so the probe would have read two overlay-cached keys and understated the scan by a factor of `MAX_LIVE_PROPOSALS`; the fixture now seeds 32 distinct decision pairs with full window vectors, and `register` was re-measured. Original rationale follows. 16 §8.4 ends in *"the values layer MUST reduce `svc.max_live`"* — a vote required exactly when revenue argues the other way, on evidence that will always be arguable, in a system where every other protection (I-4 latch, PB-LEDGER-FREEZE, dead-man switch) is automatic. **Trigger on the input, not the output:** a `NotDecisionGrade` rejection caused by the hosted service is indistinguishable from one caused by disinterest, so the rejection count can never settle the argument; contest capital is the quantity itself. **Two gaming findings from the N14 pass make this N14's curve rather than separate machinery** — see the Decision log: a binary latch creates a race to register before the cliff, and a latch that grandfathers incumbents pays a slot-holder to suppress Bleavit's own contest capital. Both are answered by a graduated response that raises the price for everyone, riding N14's multiplier and therefore its destination — `MAIN`, not the POL an earlier revision named (the routing was withdrawn on implementation; Decision log). Failure direction stays safe: the hosted business goes dark, governance keeps working, and an attacker who drains Bleavit's markets to trip it only manages to switch the service off

<!-- source track narrative -->

Opened 2026-08-01 on the "make the futarchy infrastructure accessible to other parachains,
smart contracts and services via XCM" work order. Bleavit hosts conditional decision markets
for external clients and **sells price discovery, not decisions**: it publishes the conditional
TWAPs with provenance plus a manipulation-cost bound, and the client's own on-chain rule decides
and executes locally. Bleavit never runs foreign code, and no external state reaches any Bleavit
decision, welfare or settlement input. v1 scope by user decision — the spec batch, contract bump
and threat rows land together. Full approved design: `.claude/plans/` (session plan, 2026-08-01).
