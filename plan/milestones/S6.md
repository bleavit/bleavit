**Done 2026-07-31.** Extends the E5 precedent (`sustainability.py`, which found two spec defects on its first run) to three areas that had *no* executable form. Three modules + 115 tests in `reference-model` (suite 125 → 240): **`lifecycle.py`** (05 §2–§3) carries the T1–T26 table as data and computes what it closes — 93 reachable `(state, flags)` configurations, **no cycle** (termination is a consequence of the one-shot budgets, not an assertion), `{Settled, Cancelled}` as the only absorbing states, and I-15 as unreachability of `Executed`/`Queued` from Rejected/Expired/Cancelled; it parses §2.2's mermaid block from the document and proves the §2.2 ≡ §2.1 edge-set identity the heading claims (30 edges each, exact); and it **runs** §3.3's three wedge claims rather than restating them — `k = 3` wedges `qualify` permanently from epoch 4 while `k ≤ 2` never does, the superseded `current − 20` prune cutoff jams `record_snapshot` at epoch 20 while `current − 19` never jams, and the superseded `MAX_SNAPSHOTS = 20 × k` jams at epoch 13 under three lawful versions/epoch while `20 × (k + 1) = 60` saturates exactly. **`disputes.py`** (07 §5–§6, §11) reproduces every published figure — §5's 10k/20k/40k ladder and 70k stack, §6.2's escalation table and 17.5 %, §6.3's META table (30,000 / 210,000 / **−90,000** against the flat regime's **+50,000**), §11's d2/d7/d10/d13/d21 and the +4 d per-round-extension counterfactual — and implements §6.3's SQ-495 amendment screen with all three normative properties, including the non-directional variant it forbids, so the freeze can be exhibited rather than described. **`occupancy.py`** (13 §5) is the four re-derivations plus the by-value screen and the in-flight composition; it reproduces both worked laundering cases to the unit and then **searches** §5's central claim ("composing against reality closes the class") over lawful amendment sequences: the registry-only screen is breachable in two steps and the composed screen admits no breach to depth 8 over ~2,800 admissible states. **Findings.** One spec defect (SQ-535, doc half fixed here); two derived facts doc 07 states nowhere — the honest challenger's break-even confidence is **5/7 ≈ 71.4 %**, not 1/2, because §5.5 pays the winner 40 % and INSURANCE 60 %, and §6.2's `17.5 %` is a constant only at and above `StakeAtRisk = 400,000` USDC where `orc.bond_floor` stops binding (175 % at 40k); and two reachability facts 13 §1's ranges hide — item 2 caps `epoch.slots` at **5**, so seven of the row's twelve published values are nominal, and item 1's frozen **2,240** dominates a reachable maximum of **1,064**. **Codex connector review on PR #200: 3 × P2, all three valid, all three fixed** — and the second was the more valuable of the two spec findings this milestone produced. (1) **T21 was unconditional**, so the model let a pre-Seed T20 rejection reach `Measuring`/`Settled`, which 05 §2.1 forbids ("fires iff markets were deployed and the vault is open"; `Rejected` with no vault is terminal). The vault is now part of a configuration, `is_terminal` replaces a state-only set, and the terminal set computes to exactly the document's list — `{Cancelled·none, Rejected·none, Rejected·voided, Settled·open}` — with `Expired`-without-vault confirmed unreachable as §2.1 says. (2) **The `k = 3` wedge claim was false, and my test asserted the wrong thing.** `simulate_cohorts` recorded only the first admission failure while the test took a permanently saturated *live count* as proof of permanent *admission failure* — which a count cannot show. One cohort retires per epoch once the window fills, so `k = 3` admits four epochs in five indefinitely, failing at period `k + 2`. 05 §3.3 corrected under R-1 (Decision log); the ceiling is unaffected because a permanent unrecoverable 1-in-5 forfeit is decisive on its own. Admission is now recorded per epoch (`CohortRun.admission_rate`, `failure_period`). (3) **`InFlight.readable()` checked only 13 §5's first refusal cause**, not "or a bound already exceeded" — and the gap was live: a live `epoch.length` of 604,821 (21 above the maximum, still a multiple of 21, so nothing downstream refused it) let a `dec.window` reduction compose to an admitted verdict. `refusal_reason()` now screens both fields against their 13 §1 bounds and 05 §3.1's multiple-of-21 rule before composing. **Gates after the fixes:** `reference-model` **245/245**, `simulation` 53/53, vector freshness, LMSR doc-table, doc links, PLAN tables, spec-question batches, limit coverage (194 keys, unwired 0, byte-identical across both spec edits) — all green. No Rust changed
