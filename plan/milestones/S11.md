**Done 2026-08-01.** Six new modules — `oracle.py` (1,356, 35 tests), `frontend_budget.py` (791, 22), `values_layer.py` (815, 17), `void_pricing.py` (637, 28), `guardians.py` (393, 24), `release_channel.py` (417, 13) — plus `disputes.py` extensions (+471, +10) and the `tools/monitoring/tests/test_revocation_window.py` co-simulation (+1). **Findings** are SQ-551…SQ-560 below. Three worth naming here. **PB-RESERVE is *not* absorbing** — the audit said it was; no unhealthy state can *arm* a refill, but a decision armed before the flag still executes `fund_budget_line`, and that distinction is the difference between a wedge and an operational annoyance. **`registry.py`'s closed form was cross-checked exhaustively** over all 131,071 outcome strings to length 16. **The VOID counterexample the audit supplied was unlawful** (its branch weights did not sum to 1) and its near-degenerate witness would have been stopped by the gate vetoes; the corrected witness preserves complementary weights, clears both gate tests, and still prices a true REJECT (0.036 uplift) as an ADOPT (0.0391415) over a continuous `w_accept` interval of roughly [0.1964, 0.2880]
