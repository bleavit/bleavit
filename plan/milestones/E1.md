**The largest financial defect in the system, and a compliance fix rather than a design change.** Per settled proposal the cash at risk is `(pol.b + 2·pol.b_gate)·ln 2` — one `split` funds a branch pair, so cash is exactly **half** the 08 §3 commitment: **17,328.68 USDC PARAM**, 27,725.89 TREASURY, 51,986.04 CODE, 79,711.93 META, plus 17,328.68/epoch Baseline. On a 5×PARAM slate: **103,972/epoch ≈ 1,808,371 USDC/yr** swept to `INSURANCE` against a true realized cost of ≈ 18,830/yr — a **≈ 1,789,542 USDC/yr** diversion, **132 % of the entire derivable operating cost base**. Value is stranded, not destroyed (`sweep_insurance` recovers it, but only by a TREASURY decision that itself consumes a slot and that slot's POL). **The dangerous half is the accounting**: settlement releases the obligation while the cash has left, and `lines[Pol]` is never debited at seed, so `NAV = main_usdc + Σlines + escrow − obligations` counts POL money the treasury no longer holds — every NAV-derived control (`trs.cap_proposal`·NAV, `pol.budget_epoch`·NAV, the §4.1 arming floors) is computed on an inflated base, in the **over-permissive** direction. Audit scope A (R-7). Sites: `pallets/market/src/lib.rs` `observe_proposal_terminal` / `observe_baseline_terminal` / `reap`, `crates/market-core` (no `withdraw_book` inverse of `seed_book` exists), `pallets/conditional-ledger` `discard_protocol_inventory` (deletes positions, moves no custody) and the `MarketAuthority` surface (**no `do_redeem*` of any kind exists**), `runtime/.../configs.rs` `PayoutLine` (**no `Pol`/`PolBaseline` variant**, so POL funding moves no custody). Verify: try-state binding released obligation to returned custody; a settle→sweep→reap differential showing `POL` restored to seed − divergence loss; PT-5 extended over the new ordering. **Done 2026-07-29 — PR #195 (`bdec21d`) plus the #197 POL_BASELINE custody hotfix (`af2e5a0`), which a post-merge spec-reviewer audit caught: the R-4 endowment leg was not debited, and every existing test seeded at most one Baseline book while the defect needs two, so no amount of CI would have found it.**
