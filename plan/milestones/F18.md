XCM is asynchronous, so the Asset Hub extrinsic finalising proves the message was **sent**, not that funds arrived — and a tracker showing *done* there tells a user their money landed when it has not. `DepositProgress`'s `credited` arm therefore **requires the futarchy-chain observation** and cannot be reached from the AH leg alone. Each leg carries **its own `Verified<T>`**, because the two come from different light clients at different finalized blocks and one status covering both would assert a cross-chain consistency nothing established. **D-13's caps are gated on the bit, not a number** (V-115 — the spec said *"while PhaseFlags < Phase 4"*, which is unperformable and would have skipped them entirely), and an **unreadable** cap **blocks** rather than proceeding without the limit that applies. Degraded XCM health **warns and does not block** — I-24 is fail-static, the funds are held rather than lost, and blocking would refuse what the chain accepts. The dusting check is a **band, not a floor**, so a full withdrawal is permitted while a remainder below `min_balance` is refused. And an **unknown** destination is distinct from a bad one and from a good one, because collapsing unknown into either is exactly what *"silently skipped"* means.
