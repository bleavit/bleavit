**Done 2026-08-01.** `registry.py` (1,163 lines, 20 tests). Generalises `occupancy.py`'s machinery from its four occupancy keys to the whole registry; grounded by byte-checking the key set against `tools/limit-coverage/genesis-keys.json` and `registry.toml` so the model cannot drift from the real registry. **Reproduced:** all 107 seeded keys byte-matched against both registry artifacts; the frozen NAV floors; the boundary-screened controls (all four `gate.v_min` pairs and `ledger.rdm_fee ≤ mkt.fee`) come out screened, so the coupling test can fail in both directions. **Falsified:** SQ-546, SQ-547 and SQ-548 as summarised in *Current focus* — plus four post-arming amendments that re-run no first-arm resource check (`res.recover_thr` → 255 needs 642.5 USDC of runway against the genesis 12.5), and `QUOTE_CLAMP_MIN/MAX_1E9` as the only 13 §2 symbols neither consumed nor metadata-projected. The closed forms are cross-checked against exhaustive search. **Integration defect found on merge and fixed here:** the kernel-hygiene scan counted this repo's own nested `.claude/worktrees/` copies as consumers. One false lead the agent discarded against the spec: `pol.budget_epoch = 0` *looks* self-sealing at the consumer, but 08 §4.1/13 §5 boundary-screen it against the frozen NAV floors — which is precisely why the model separates a record's published range from what the amendment boundary actually admits
