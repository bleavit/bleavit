N12 adds the two that were still prose: **`reference-model/.../service_economics.py`** — the executable form of 16 §5.2/§8.1/§8.2 + 13 §1's fee-floor derivation, pinning `b_min` = 14.2368·S, escrow 19.736·S, instrument D at 77 % of evidenced per-question revenue and the 3,930-USDC floor crossover, and asserting the **superseded** displacement form and per-*market* tariff reading do NOT reproduce them — and the **competing-venue term** in the S4 simulation. The term's arming rung is *anchored* to 16 §8.4's arming condition (`Σ b_ext ≤ Σ pol.b(live)` ⇒ ½ **under proportional-to-depth allocation**, a behavioural model whose error direction is unsafe, so the ladder must probe above it — 0.25/0.50/0.75); it thins organic formation on decision/gate/Baseline books and leaves attacker budgets untouched. **Measured finding, and it redirects the milestone's own premise:** diversion's first-order effect is *denial*, not corruption — books fall below `dec.v_min` and reject `NotDecisionGrade` rather than flipping — so a false-pass-only criterion reads *cleaner* the more governance is destroyed. The arming criterion therefore has a gated security half and a measured-with-falsifier liveness half (15 §4.9, 16 §8.4). Zero diversion is a **strict** no-op (asserted on executed evidence, not on `x·1 == x`), so the published Phase-0 result is untouched

<!-- source track narrative -->

Opened 2026-08-01 on the "make the futarchy infrastructure accessible to other parachains,
smart contracts and services via XCM" work order. Bleavit hosts conditional decision markets
for external clients and **sells price discovery, not decisions**: it publishes the conditional
TWAPs with provenance plus a manipulation-cost bound, and the client's own on-chain rule decides
and executes locally. Bleavit never runs foreign code, and no external state reaches any Bleavit
decision, welfare or settlement input. v1 scope by user decision — the spec batch, contract bump
and threat rows land together. Full approved design: `.claude/plans/` (session plan, 2026-08-01).
