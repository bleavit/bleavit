All **59** published cells are now re-derived in CI by `tools/ci/check-frontend-budgets.py` from 13 §1/§3.1/§4/§5 plus the runtime's pin, so the targets this milestone measures against cannot silently rot again. **The measurement half landed 2026-08-06 and takes §9.4 from one enforced row to five of thirteen**: initial JS and smoldot were already gated (re-verified wired *and* bound), and F14 adds release-shipped fallback metadata plus chain specs (`app/tools/check-artifact-budget.ts`) and **first meaningful render** (`app/tools/render-budget/check.ts` — Lighthouse over the built release tree on §9.4's own reference profiles, median of 3; the initial 12.8.2 measurement was desktop **0.40 s** and mobile **1.80 s** FCP against 1.5 s / 3 s p50 targets). Every threshold is read from its published cell and nowhere else, **including §9.4's reference-hardware sentence** — its 4× desktop throttle and its Moto-G-class device are values, not prose, and Lighthouse's desktop preset is 1× so that override *is* the reference machine (V-179). Both new gates carry witness legs (5 artifact cases, 1 render case), plus 8 hand-run render mutations and 17 new mutation tests in `tools/ci/tests` (48 in that file, 262 in the suite). **PR #254's review round corrected both new gates, and neither defect was a wrong number (2026-08-06).** Codex filed two P1s and both are this milestone's own defect class — a gate measuring something other than what it claims — so `check-frontend-budgets.py` proving both gates carried their published cells proved nothing about either. **The render gate compared a three-run median against the p95 column**, which tests the wrong statistic: samples of 2 s, 2 s and 20 s have a 2 s median and pass a 6 s p95 threshold while a third of the sample sits over three times above it.
