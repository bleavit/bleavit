Audit scope A. **`redeem` is exempt and that is the load-bearing ruling**: it is the par leg every D-3 wrapper buy leaves with the buyer, so charging it would falsify G-3, D-3, I-2(b), I-5 and PT-2 together. The work order's default was to charge it; overturned, taking the "exempt the mirror leg" branch it offered. **Solvency**: the fee is not a second draw on escrow — `escrowed` decrements by the gross, the claimant receives net, the difference stays as sovereign USDC in `RedemptionFeesAccrued` (lawful surplus in exactly the sense L-2 already admits for the genesis endowment and dust), swept by `sweep_redemption_fees()`. §6.5's induction extends in place: charged redemptions stay class (iii) with *strict* slack. **Rate derived, not picked**: `ledger.redeem_fee ≤ mkt.fee` is exit-neutrality — above it, holding to settlement costs more than round-tripping *conditional on the position surviving to a charged redemption*, so the schedule would pay traders to close before d18, draining the contest capital `dec.v_min` requires and `L̂` measures. Default sits at the bound (30 bps). The "in every state of the world" form was an over-claim, corrected 2026-07-29: the losing-branch wrapper buyer exits the mirror leg through the exempt `redeem` at zero cost, so the true neutral point is nearer `2·mkt.fee` and `≤ mkt.fee` is a deliberately conservative ceiling. Bound and default unchanged (08 §10.6; 13 §1). **The S4 obligation cannot be discharged as written, and not for the reason the work order assumed.** E1's fee-*destination* changes do not touch the simulation — destination never enters its trade ledger, so the committed `phase0-calibration.json` economics stand. The **redemption fee** is different: it is not modelled at all, and it changes exit economics, hence held capital, hence 04 §7a contest capital, hence both the 0.995 quantile `sec.flow_cap` = 16 was calibrated from and the decision-grade rate the δ calibration rests on. A re-run would not fix that, because the model has no redemption-fee path to run; adding one is a prerequisite and composes with SQ-506's demand-side obligation, since both change how the sim prices trader behaviour. Until then **no claim may be made that E4's rate is Phase-0-calibrated** — it is derived from exit-neutrality (08 §10.6), which is an argument, not evidence. Verify: PT-2 at a **non-zero** rate (the regression that catches a future widening into the par leg), PT-3 `[E − r − F, E − F]`, PT-7's relative form over many `(a, s, rate)` including the `a = 20,000, s = 0.70005` witness where both paths must read 19,957, L-7, the named coupling test `e1_no_amendment_may_carry_the_redemption_fee_above_the_market_fee` (a `param-bounds` key cannot carry a `// limit-coverage:` marker — the checker rejects one on any non-`dispatch-limit` key and on any ignored test; the SQ-495 precedent is a plainly named test instead), fuzz over fee-bearing redemption. **Done 2026-07-29 — PR #196 (`76ee39e`); `ledger.rdm_fee`'s unwired limit-coverage exemption expired with it and the registry is back to `unwired = 0`.**
