title: "**Genesis endowment of the ledger sovereign, treasury sub-accounts and book accounts (03 §7 R-4).** R-4 states these accounts 'are genesis-endowed and can never be reaped'; no genesis path endows any of them with USDC — `runtime/bleavit-runtime/src/genesis.rs` registers the USDC/DOT assets and metadata but sets `accounts: vec![]`, and `deploy/genesis/allocations.template.json` carries none. Because `settle_collateral` uses `Preservation::Preserve`, the **last full redeemer of the last open vault** fails `Token(NotExpendable)` once the sovereign would drop below USDC `min_balance` — a redemption legal under 03 §5.3 becomes impossible. Scope: decide which of the three account classes are genesis-seedable at all (book accounts are created dynamically at Seed, so R-4 may need truing for those), seed the ones that are, and carry the amounts through the allocations template and the chain-spec validator."
spec: [03 §7 R-4, 03 §5.3]

status: done

**Done 2026-07-21.** Ten statically derived protocol accounts genesis-endowed with exactly `min_balance` (`USDC_CENT`) through the `ForeignAssets` genesis `accounts` field: the ledger sovereign plus its INSURANCE/BOOK/POL/POL_BASELINE/FEES/TREASURY sub-accounts, and treasury MAIN/KEEPER/ORACLE. `TreasuryPotFunding::fund` flipped `Expendable` → `Preserve`, because an endowment is only half the mechanism — an `Expendable` outflow ignores `min_balance` and reaps the account regardless, so MAIN's floor would not have bound. Regression proven in both directions: restoring `accounts: vec![]` makes `last_redeemer_of_last_vault_can_fully_exit` fail `Token(NotExpendable)`; with the endowment it passes with `Vaults::escrowed == Some(0)` and L-2 green. **The book-account sub-question resolved against the easy answer.** Per-market accounts genuinely cannot be genesis-endowed (their addresses embed a runtime-allocated `MarketId`), but "therefore R-4 does not bind them" is false: decision/gate books and every per-market fee account custody positions only, yet a **Baseline** book holds plain USDC because it retains the sell-side fee. A binary search against real `pallet-assets` custody put the flip at 6,349,782 planck (fail) → 6,349,783 (pass, retaining exactly `min_balance`), so with `MinTrade` = 1 USDC the entire band of ordinary Baseline sells from 1 to ≈6.35 USDC was being rejected — invisible because `pallets/market/src/mock.rs` genesis-endows its shared BOOK account and the in-memory differential oracle models no custody at all. Fixed by endowing the Baseline book at Seed from `POL_BASELINE`, inside epoch `tick`'s storage layer so a failed endowment rolls the seed back (G-1). `Expendable` was evaluated for that path and **rejected**: it folds the sub-minimum dust into the debit, silently sweeping the book's accrued fee into the sovereign as excess escrow where L-2 (`liability ≤ custody`) cannot detect it — a worse outcome than a hard failure. Spec trued per R-1 (03 §7 R-4 rewritten, also repairing 03 §1's contradictory "1-unit genesis endowment"; 04 §6.1 gains the Baseline sell-side degeneration; 08 §1.4 pins funding to `MAIN − min_balance`); no 02 surface touched, so no contract bump. **Dual review (spec-reviewer + adversarial Codex) found one critical defect in the fix itself and two spec over-claims, all fixed in-session.** The Baseline endowment was a hard `?`: `POL_BASELINE` funded to exactly `min_balance + headroom` left reducible = 0 after the seed, propagating `CoreError::Ledger` out of `tick` and reverting the whole proposal batch permanently — reachable because the POL floor telemetry carried no per-book allowance. Now best-effort + idempotent behind an affordability pre-check, with the allowance added to the floor; regression pinned (failed `ModuleError { index: 61, error: [15,…] }` before). Both reviewers independently disproved the amendment's claim that the reaped Baseline residue is R-6-recoverable — `recover_foreign` refuses `Usdc` and moves no custody — so R-4 now records it as unrecoverable; and R-4's blanket preserve-MUST is qualified for the XCM-addressable ledger `TREASURY` seam, where the SDK `FungiblesAdapter` is hardcoded `Expendable`. **A third review round (Codex on the PR) then found a P1 hole in the new chain-spec gate itself**: it matched only the USDC Location and `min_balance`, leaving `owner` and `is_sufficient` unchecked — and `pallet-assets` genesis installs `owner` as owner *and* issuer *and* admin *and* freezer, so the release gate would have approved a production spec handing an external key mint/burn/freeze authority over all protocol collateral, with `is_sufficient = false` violating R-4's opening clause on top. The test fixture itself carried exactly that shape (owner = ALICE), which is why nothing caught it. All four tuple fields are now gated, for **both** declared assets (DOT carries the identical authority and funds coretime renewal), with five negative tests and the reported payload verified rejected. Originally raised as SQ-288 and reclassified here because the spec was unambiguous and simply unimplemented.
